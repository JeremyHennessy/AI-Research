# What would make a digital process genuinely life-like?
**Research synthesis; not a legal, biological, philosophical or consciousness verdict.**

## 1. There is no universally agreed definition of life
A historically influential working formulation emerging from a NASA exobiology group emphasizes a self-sustaining *chemical* system capable of Darwinian evolution. Gerald Joyce explicitly described it as a **working**, not officially binding definition, and explains why its chemical emphasis suits astrobiology rather than computational life. See [NASA's account](https://astrobiology.nasa.gov/news/defining-life-qa-with-scientist-gerald-joyce/) and [NASA's teaching resource](https://science.nasa.gov/astrobiology/learning-resources/alp/alive-or-not/).

Its **chemical** requirement excludes purely digital systems *by definition*, not by an experiment proving no computer can exhibit interesting life-like organization. Conversely, dropping the word chemical does **not** prove a program is alive. Avoid forcing a single answer where researchers disagree.

### Competing emphases
| Framework | Key proposed criterion | Contribution to digital tests | Why it is disputed or incomplete |
|---|---|---|---|
| Darwinian evolutionary | Heritable variation + differential reproduction over generations | Lineage descent, variation, selection and capacity for new function | Viruses/dependency, initial nonreproducing life, sterile individuals, very narrow evolution |
| Autopoiesis (Varela, Maturana, Uribe, 1974) | Organization continuously produces components and its own boundary | Causal dependence of repair and boundary on internal processes | Computational vs material instantiation and test specification debated |
| Organizational/constraint closure | Network of mutually maintaining constraints and processes | Intervene on production/repair loops | Circular update code alone is not causal organizational closure |
| Metabolic / dissipative | Harness environmental throughput to resist spontaneous loss of organization | Material/resource ledger, nonzero cost of upkeep, decay when upkeep stops | Digital cost may be simulator accounting, not physical thermodynamic metabolism |
| Informational/evolutionary | Persistent information carriers that shape future organization | Inherited program/state and descendant changes | Information storage alone includes nonliving software |
| Ecological / individuality | Distinct entities and interaction networks, transitions in cooperation and individuality | Ecological niches, persistent lineage and emergent grouping | Where an organism starts/ends can itself change |

Sources: [Varela et al. 1974](https://doi.org/10.1016/0303-2647(74)90031-8), [Szathmáry & Maynard Smith 1995](https://doi.org/10.1038/374227a0), [Taylor 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.07403), [Synthetic protocell biology](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2442389/), [OEE York workshop](https://doi.org/10.1162/ARTL_A_00210).

## 2. Necessary, optional, contested: *for an experiment*, not universal law
We propose a strong empirical **minimum test battery** for a candidate digital-organism study, not a necessary-and-sufficient definition of life.

| Property | Experimental stance | Minimal test | Common impostor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Individuation/persistence | Required for studying individual *organisms* | Track causally cohesive process despite state turnover | Any stable attractor or frozen sprite |
| Self-maintenance | Required for **autonomous organization** claims | Remove periodic external reset; deplete/perturb components; observe endogenous restoration | Simulator respawns or immune-memory regions |
| Resource coupling | Required for **resource-dependent survival** claim | Budget conservation / inflow / work and measure survival response | Arbitrary "energy" number never linked to update actions |
| Counterfactual regulation | Required for **adaptive agency** claim | Unfamiliar perturbation, measure regulation towards viability | Fixed scripted stimulus-response lookup |
| Reproduction with heritability | Required for **Darwinian population** claim; not for one individual | Verify descendants, genotype/organizational transmission and mutation | Exact animation duplication or state cloning by engine |
| Ecological interaction | Valuable, not universal | Cross-feeding, competition, environment engineering in matched worlds | Decorative agents sharing a field |
| Development / morphology | Valuable | Reconstruct body from inherited information in varied conditions | Pre-painted target image or hard-coded initial pattern |
| Nervous system / language | **Not required** | Detect sensorimotor learning before symbol use | Assuming natural language is a prerequisite |
| Unlimited growth / consciousness | **Not required and unproven** | No accepted finite test proves either | Open-ended-looking movies and anthropomorphism |

No fixed simulation can empirically demonstrate **infinite** unbounded novelty in finite time. Instead measure finite-window hallmarks, counterfactuals, and whether trends persist out-of-sample.

## 3. Operationalize self-maintenance with an intervention
Take a persistent structure at time t with an independently chosen boundary measure and constituent-process network. Evaluate:
1. **Baseline stability:** not just unchanged state; constituent turnover and process flux.
2. **Damage:** remove a component or weaken the boundary, with comparable damage to neutral structures.
3. **Recovery:** restoration caused by the system's own rules/interactions, not by a system-specific reset or designer reward.
4. **Dependency:** disable an internal regeneration process; observe whether restoration fails.
5. **Resource:** deny a necessary inflow; measure failure modes and recovery after resupply.
6. **Generalization:** vary perturbation location, timing, and environmental parameters unseen during search.
7. **Null:** inert patterns, scripted repair agents, random patterns and no-resource/control physics.

**Causal test:** an internal process must *make a difference* to continued organization after controlling for simulator invariants. Static persistence under immutable update rules is weak evidence for living self-maintenance.

## 4. Organizational identity without a human-assigned name
Try at least two independent identity trackers:
- **Material lineage:** temporal overlap and transfer of informational/components states.
- **Process lineage:** stable network of functional relations and flows even while constituents change.

Record ambiguous merging/splitting cases explicitly; forcing one predetermined connected component count creates false "individuals". For adaptive boundaries, define detection on development data then freeze before testing.

**Original research hypothesis:** a process-based identity measure may recognize genuine repaired bodies where pixel overlap fails, but is also vulnerable to classifying persistent oscillations as organisms. Falsify against oscillators, fluid vortices, periodic patterns, and isolated self-replicating programs.

## 5. Origins of biological agency
From an enactive/viability perspective, an entity can behave *as if* it has a norm because some perturbations threaten its continuation and certain actions oppose those threats. The normative interpretation is theoretical; empirical task is to show **selective compensatory regulation** (e.g. mobilizing resources when starved, reducing energy use when scarcity increases) under conditions not directly hard-coded as a mission.

One must separate four pressures:
- **Hard-coded objective:** experimenter explicitly scores distance to a target, image, task solution or complexity.
- **Viability constraint:** state dynamics make certain configurations persist only if they maintain some processes (still designed substrate).
- **Intrinsic motivation:** internal preference/surprise/novelty signal learned or implemented by the organism.
- **Selection pressure:** differential reproduction and survival from interactions, scarcity and neighbors.

A system can combine these. The label *intrinsic* cannot mean "free of the researcher's assumptions."

## 6. Individual lifetime vs lineage evolution
Learning means change within an individual's history that improves or alters its interaction with the environment. Evolution means inherited population change across generational histories. They must be measured independently.

Design factorial controls for:
- Mutable body/state but **no inherited adaptation**;
- Heritable variation but **no lifetime learning**;
- Both lifetime plasticity and inheritance;
- Neither plasticity nor inheritance.

If an agent carries a human-designed frozen policy that simply reproduces, it may be Darwinian *in reproduction* but does not demonstrate emergent cognition. If it learns during one life but has no descendants, do not call that population evolution.

## 7. Why complex simulations are not conclusive
A sufficiently detailed animation can reproduce convincing biological appearance by developer instructions. A life-like process needs **counterfactual dependence, maintenance, operational reproduction and measurable transfer**. These are much stronger than trajectories, screenshots, program length, or observers saying it looks alive.

This dossier takes no stand on digital consciousness; computational viability, subjective experience and ethical status require separate investigation.
