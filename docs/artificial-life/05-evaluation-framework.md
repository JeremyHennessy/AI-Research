# A falsification-first evaluation framework for candidate digital life
**Research specification only.** No organism has been created or evaluated; proposed metrics are not evidence that any substrate meets them. Do not build a single composite "aliveness" score that hides failures.

## 1. Separate level of claim
| Stage | What must be seen | What does **not** suffice |
|---|---|---|
| M0 Dynamics | Nontrivial pattern/activity exists under fixed rules | Beautiful snapshots |
| M1 Persistence | Consistent identity through time under state turnover | An immutable sprite or cached program |
| M2 Self-maintenance | Internal causal loop restores organization after damage | Engine resets, externally scripted repairs |
| M3 Resource-dependence | Persistence depends on measurable uptake/throughput/cost | Energy counters unrelated to actions |
| M4 Functional adaptation | Beneficial compensatory response under unseen perturbations | Same reflex in training condition |
| M5 Heritable descent | Verified descendants retain mutating informational/functional differences | Engine-created duplicates or image twins |
| M6 Ecological evolution | Persistent genotype/phenotype diversity and differential success caused by interactions | Populations growing on free perpetual resources |
| M7 Adaptive innovation | New function in lineage vs ancestors and neutral controls, sustained on hidden transfer tests | Longer genome/novel color/movement |
| M8 Open-ended hallmarks | Continued innovations and possible transitions over increasingly long windows under multiple independent measures | Long animation or arbitrarily lengthened run |

**M8 is not a proof of mathematical unboundedness**; all finite simulation experiments provide bounded evidence.

## 2. Concrete outcome variables (definitions require preregistration)
- **Persistence fraction**: among seeded or spontaneously detected structures, fraction persisting at T under fixed rules, with confidence interval across independent seeds. Report identity ambiguities.
- **Constituent turnover**: fraction of parts replaced during a period while functional/organizational identity stays stable. Without turnover, distinguish static robustness from active upkeep.
- **Causal repair ratio**: recovery after randomized damage with repair process enabled vs process selectively disabled, controlling initial damage extent. Use effect sizes and uncertainty.
- **Resource balance**: conservation ledger `R(t+1)=R(t)+supply+production-uptake-decay-outflow` on an explicit spatial domain; check numerical residual. Energy as a fictional cost is distinct from physical entropy.
- **Reproductive fitness**: expected *viable descendant* production under controlled environmental conditions, with lineage identity and copy/trait fidelity.
- **Heritability**: parent/offspring trait association corrected for shared environment and neutral mutation; identify transmission rather than resemblance from a deterministic rule.
- **Adaptation**: relative recovery/survival/functional success on hidden perturbations; compare to randomized or history-shuffled controls.
- **Functional repertoire**: distinct demonstrated affordances (consume resource X, resist perturbation Y, enter niche Z), identified via intervention, not arbitrary visual clustering.
- **Ecological network**: resource or causal interaction graph among contemporaneous entities, with null generated by shuffled links, density matched and species identity controlled.
- **Evolutionary activity**: use a published quantitative method (e.g. Bedau/Packard lineage activity) and compare to **neutral drift** and environment-forcing controls. Be explicit about assumptions required to infer fitness from observations.
- **Innovation persistence**: survival/reuse of new function over multiple descendants/generations and after migration or environmental change.
- **Novelty saturation**: moving-window rate of independently verified new capabilities; report windows, thresholds and estimator biases. A flat curve cannot prove no future novelty, but is strong evidence of plateau at tested budget.

## 3. Avoid a single cherry-picked complexity measure
The following can **increase with no emergent intelligence**: entropy, genome length, pixel variance, movement speed, visual embedding distance, taxonomic counts, cumulative population, number of agent messages, one benchmark score.

Prefer **several uncorrelated outcomes** with different failure modes:
1. Objective functional innovations judged by changed input/output behavior.
2. Lineage-dependent persistence.
3. New mechanism/organizational level (verified causal topology).
4. Generalization to unfamiliar environmental regimes.
5. Measured information gathering that predicts future viability improvements.

Report **per-metric** trajectories; never convert all into one life badge.

## 4. Essential negative and neutral controls
| Control | Purpose | Expected informative result |
|---|---|---|
| No reproduction / randomized parent linkage | Detect false heredity and lineage innovation | Claimed descent metrics collapse |
| Neutral mutation / fitness-neutral replay | Distinguish adaptive activity from drift | Adaptive scores exceed neutral baseline only under selection |
| Remove resource constraint | Test whether necessity/competition drives novelty | Effects vanish/change, not pre-assumed |
| Remove external novelty/image reward | Separate intrinsic selection from researcher objective | Intrinsic capability changes might persist; if not, report dependency |
| Disabled internal repair process | Distinguish self-maintenance from engine persistence | Recovery fails in mechanistically predictable way |
| Static environment vs changing world | Test environmental affordances and actual transfer | Adaptation depends on relevant pressure |
| Time-reversed / history-shuffled cues | Test memory/informational dependence | True learned cue utility disappears |
| Inert oscillating attractor | Reject visual/metabolic false positives | Functional/repair/lineage tests fail |
| Frozen genotype / shuffled offspring | Reject mere population growth | Evolutionary innovation disappears |
| Baseline with same designer intervention | Prevent unfair variant advantage | Benefits attributed to substrate not extra reward/data |
| Duplicate test families unseen during selection | Test generalization rather than chosen targets | Correctness remains after future conditions change |

Negative results are first-class evidence and must remain recorded.

## 5. Statistical design before coding
- Define *experimental unit*: world/replicate or independently seeded lineage, not one frame sampled from a correlated video.
- Predeclare matched initial conditions, random seed distribution, perturbation size/duration, source hashes and known engineered rewards.
- Where stochastic, use many independent worlds and repeat across parameter regimes; a suggestion is **20+ independent seeds if budget allows**, adjusted by a power analysis and outcome variance.
- Hold out entire new **ecological regimes or rule combinations**, not merely later frames of the same stochastic run.
- Use paired bootstrap across independent worlds for differences, survival analysis for time-to-extinction, and uncertainty around rate-of-novelty slopes; distinguish multiple testing.
- Audit failures manually/blindly on cases selected before knowing treatment labels.
- Where generation runs long, preregister checkpoints and stopping criteria. Longer runtimes change compute opportunity; match cost.
- Include data/metadata lineage and code revision IDs; independently compute summaries from raw events.

## 6. Source-backed criteria and their caveats
[OEE York workshop](https://doi.org/10.1162/ARTL_A_00210): researchers distinguish *behavioral hallmarks* from hypothesized *causal mechanisms* and emphasize multiple definitions. [Taylor 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.07403): suggests robust reproduction, large interaction possibility space, more-complex offspring potential, viable mutation paths and ongoing drive. These are scientific perspectives rather than universally agreed axioms. [OEE counterexample 2019](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00289): formal OEE indicators may label uninteresting processes "open-ended"; therefore measure meaningful functionality and causal change. [Flow-Lenia 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08569): uses evolutionary activity measures, but functional open-endedness still requires separate tests.

## 7. Reporting schema for any future separately authorized experiment
A research receipt must contain:
- Claim level M0..M8; operational definition; explicit interpretation exclusions;
- Simulator physics/space/clock precision, update rule, external interventions, conserved quantities;
- Seed and whether the *initial viable organism* was designer supplied;
- Individual/lineage identifier rules plus ambiguity and split/merge handling;
- Variant/controls, training/search procedure, and any external human/AI evaluation signal;
- Environment and resource ledger; raw action/state events; data rights;
- Held-out test settings; quality of replication/functional novelty; confidence intervals;
- Exact source commit and system run hashes; evaluator version and negative controls;
- Verdict *for each criterion*, not a global assertion of artificial life or consciousness.

This document specifies **how to evaluate** an organism research program, not a design ready for deployment.

## Added 2026-10-08: protect against observer-only transformation claims
The [complete 2020 Stringmol novelty paper](30-stringmol-2020-novelty-full-review.md) distinguishes **intrinsic code-enabled innovations** from **extrinsic scientific meta-model** changes. An evaluator who invents a new category (e.g. "reaction network" or "hypercycle") may detect interesting ecological organization, but its new category does **not** necessarily become a novel instruction or a usable functional property to participants.

**Additional required control for AL-C09 and AL-H14:** freeze at least one scientific/engineering model **before observing** the world; repeat analysis under a more complete taxonomy already containing candidate networks; then independently inspect genome/program/interpretation differences and test daughters' newly reachable functions. A model-relative label cannot substitute for **causal executable change and inherited viability**.

This is a proposed evidence check, not an additional organism run. [Full failure atlas](32-why-evolvable-computation-fails.md).
