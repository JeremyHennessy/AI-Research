# Hypotheses and unanswered scientific questions
**Original proposals clearly separated from author reports. None is experimentally tested by this repository.** Structured hypotheses: [data/alife/hypotheses.jsonl](../../data/alife/hypotheses.jsonl).

## Ranked unknowns
1. **Organizational emergence**: Can unseeded computational processes produce a boundary plus mutually sustaining internal processes, rather than relying on a prebuilt replicator?
2. **Self-maintenance**: What intervention proves that survival is maintained by endogenous activity, not numerical invariants or implicit engine protection?
3. **Heritable organization**: How can a spatial/metabolic pattern transfer functional information to descendants without a centrally imposed genotype schema?
4. **Open-endedness**: Which measurable adaptive novelties continue after diversity/niche/evaluator controls, rather than plateau at a fixed affordance set?
5. **Novel capability without a task reward**: Under what viability constraints is new sensing, memory or learning actually selected?
6. **Individuality**: Can organism boundaries and organizational level be discovered *after* simulation rather than fixed by the experimenter?
7. **Genotype→phenotype evolvability**: Which mappings admit viable mutations that rearrange modular functions instead of mostly destroying them?
8. **Ecology and major transitions**: Which interactions create lasting cooperation, shared reproduction or new evolutionary units, not temporary clustering?
9. **Energy analogy**: When is artificial "metabolism" more than software-managed tokens? What physical/computational flux is really indispensable?
10. **Measurement robustness**: Can independent observers agree that a new function emerged, using blinded intervention tests rather than aesthetic novelty?
11. **Development and sensorimotor cognition**: Can adaptive internal organization and useful predictive state emerge before language, symbolic planning or an explicitly trained controller?
12. **Substrate dependence**: Which claimed principles transfer across program, cellular-field, artificial-chemistry and neurodevelopment substrates?

These hypotheses do **not** imply all proposed features should be built together. Most are mutually competing directions; study **one at a time**.

## Hard-to-answer theoretical issues (not silently "solved")
- Defining life independent of carbon chemistry may be philosophically defensible but no consensual operational criterion exists.
- Infinite novelty cannot be demonstrated from finite experiments; "unbounded" claims depend on theory and asymptotics.
- Complexity is multidimensional. Genomic length, minimum-description-length of a snapshot, number of processes, and functional diversity can disagree.
- A virtual physics system can be formally closed and still exist only because external hardware supplies resources and time. All organisms also depend on environments; the scientifically useful question is **which organization is internally produced and maintained**.
- A digital agent acting to stay viable does not thereby have desires, consciousness or moral standing.
- Selection cannot explain capabilities absent a viable hereditary path or an environment in which those capabilities make a difference.
- Novelty selected by a human-chosen evaluator is not independently "unscripted" even when outcomes surprise the evaluator.

## Independent deciding experiments
For each hypothesis pre-register a *null* outcome and a critical ablation, then evaluate across at least two substrates if feasible. If two substrates disagree, preserve separate results rather than averaging them into a general life claim.
