# Future experimental program — planning only, do not implement yet
**No authorization exists to create, train, run or deploy a digital organism.** All stages are proposed research designs for a later, separately approved project.

## Phase R0 — lock evidence and terms (research, now)
**Goal:** define what will count as self-maintenance, heredity, new function, ecological interaction and open-ended hallmark *before* an experiment.

Deliverables: reference corpus, frozen hypotheses, outcome and neutral-control definitions, feasible hardware budget, rights/source manifest. Test evaluator schemas on manually authored toy observation tables (not running an organism). **Exit:** independent reviewer can tell what positive and negative evidence would look like.

## Phase R1 — smallest possible comparisons (future authorization required)
Three **separate** proof-of-concept designs, not one integrated platform:

### R1A Artificial chemistry: reaction-maintenance
- Abstract stochastic finite reaction network with mass and energy ledger, spontaneous or seeded autocatalytic motifs **separately labeled**.
- Compare mutually sustaining network / equivalent catalysis with one critical feedback removed / passive reaction cycles.
- Endpoint: causal restoration of components after perturbation, resource-dependent lifetime and correct mass accounting.
- Counterexample: recovery is entirely maintained by external replenishment schedule.
- Low compute plausible for small reaction sets; full molecular realism beyond this stage.

### R1B Digital ecology: heritable computational replication
- Bounded instruction ecology with actual copy operations, mutation and competing resources.
- Compare a seeded replicator / nonviable instructions / mutation-disabled baseline, then one-vs-two-resource ecology.
- Endpoint: descent and heritable functional novelty on predeclared unseen resource conversion tests.
- Counterexample: function only exists when directly awarded artificial CPU bonuses.
- Low-compute CPU option; no need to introduce human language or chat goals.

### R1C Cellular self-organization: active maintenance
- Generic local spatial field with matter/decay; start with unstructured conditions versus designer-selected viable patterns.
- Compare fixed Lenia-like physics / conservative flow / localized rules, then repair process ablations.
- Endpoint: nontrivial active persistence and repair plus **true lineage** if reproduction arises.
- Counterexample: patterns are merely motion attractors with no effective heredity or resource upkeep.
- Small grid may fit consumer compute; pattern search can dominate cost.

**None of the above is built in AI-Research.** The study notes identify proposed variables rather than parameter recipes that guarantee success.

## Phase R2 — add ecology (only if R1 gives genuine evidence)
Ablate single vs multiple resources, spatial heterogeneity, replenishment, waste/reciprocity, partner/competitor interactions and niche construction. Match total resource inputs and environment compute. Track independently validated new functions across lineage branches and ecological perturbations.

**Advance only if:** functional novelty exceeds drift/neutral controls and appears under more than one ecosystem random seed. A beautiful complex animation is not an exit criterion.

## Phase R3 — evolution of organization and developmental learning
Only after heritable variation and survival effects are verified, allow endogenous changes to division timing, repair pathways, signaling and developmental morphology, potentially with *separate* learning mechanisms. Compare frozen lifetime update rules, mutable within-life states, inherited changes and combinations.

**Advance only if:** new structures/behaviors make *causal* differences to survival or viable reproduction on held-out resource/perturbation regimes, not just target-image score.

## Phase R4 — new capabilities not on a designer's task list
Pre-register an **affordance discovery audit**: blind reviewers or independent property tests propose novel functions *after* searching a limited development world, then freeze probes and test different worlds and descendants. Track lineage origin, test transfer, comparator ancestors and neutral drift. Do not let a foundation-model vision score be the sole judge.

**Advance only if:** multiple independent methods detect retained functional novelty, with clear evaluation artifact and no reward/controller leak.

## Phase R5 — open-endedness and major transitions (very high bar)
Long-run comparisons with controlled budgets; varying world sizes and resource regimes; lineage/organizational transition detection and explicitly measured event-rate plateau. Evaluate whether new levels of individuality and ecological niches arise in multiple independent seeds, and whether continuing novelty rates exceed finite-system scaling/null expectations.

**Provisional conclusion permitted:** "This system shows specified open-ended-like hallmarks through horizon H on benchmarks B and controls C." **Not permitted:** "unlimited evolution" or "living conscious organism achieved."

## Low-compute prioritization
| Choice | Why first / cost characterization | Why not start with grandiose simulation? |
|---|---|---|
| R1B instruction ecology | Simple deterministic semantics, built-in lineage testing, CPU-friendly | More parameters hide heritability failures |
| R1A small artificial chemistry | Clear stoichiometric conservation and causal reaction knockouts | Molecular fidelity would obscure minimal causal question |
| R1C local field models | Clear space and repair probes, easily visualized | Visual progress is precisely the deception to control |
| POET / FM-powered search | Valuable **comparator** for externally optimized novelty | Externally selected objective is not evidence of intrinsic organism purpose |

Keep these alternatives independent through R2. Don't automatically choose an LLM because it is familiar or reuse Ora's phase machinery as a shortcut.

## Stop/redirect gates
- No self-maintenance beyond inert attractor + engine reset after precommitted runs → **pause** and revisit definition/repair mechanism.
- No lineage identity or heritable trait fidelity beyond neutral resemblance → **do not** call it evolution; redesign/stop heredity study.
- New capacity disappears when externally scripted reward removed → document **objective dependence**; don't label intrinsic emergence.
- Novelty rises only in pixel/embedding score but not in verified affordances → report visual search result only.
- Extinction/collapse dominates all but finely tuned world settings → report fragility rather than hiding those runs.
- Cost required for a statistically informative trial exceeds approved budget → stop, simplify question, or request new authorization.
- Unsafe or irreversible external action would be needed → cannot be undertaken under this research-only authorization.
- Ambiguous ethical questions about potential digital welfare/consciousness → do not presume resolution; require separate oversight before more ambitious life-related experiments.
