## I. Origins of life, organizational closure and biological transitions
1. **Francisco G. Varela, Humberto R. Maturana, Ricardo Uribe (1974).** [Autopoiesis: The organization of living systems, its characterization and a model](https://doi.org/10.1016/0303-2647(74)90031-8), *BioSystems*. **Question:** can a living organization be characterized by ongoing self-production of components and boundary? **Evidence:** conceptual construction and a simulated minimal autopoietic organization. **Limit:** theory does not prove every digital analog has biological life properties. **Possible test:** perturb internal production paths and quantify endogenous boundary repair. Rights/full text not separately archived.
2. **Eörs Szathmáry and John Maynard Smith (1995).** [The major evolutionary transitions](https://doi.org/10.1038/374227a0), *Nature*. **Question:** how can new units of selection/organizational hierarchy evolve? **Evidence:** biology-theoretical synthesis about changed information transmission and cooperation. **Limit:** no guarantee or monotonic complexity ascent; not directly a digital architecture. **Test:** stable co-reproduction and transition of information transmission under controlled synthetic ecology.
3. **Yu Liu and David J. T. Sumpter (2018).** [Mathematical modeling reveals spontaneous emergence of self-replication in chemical reaction systems](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.RA118.003795), *Journal of Biological Chemistry*. **Method:** mass-conserving chemical-reaction models with reaction energetics/kinetics. **Reported:** spontaneous collectively catalytic or whole-network self-replicating regimes for some generated networks. **Limit:** abstract chemical universe ≠ complete self-maintaining cell. **Test:** disrupt catalytic feedback and track compositional inheritance.
4. **Ricard Solé and collaborators (2007).** [Synthetic protocell biology: from reproduction to computation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2442389/), *Royal Society Interface / review*. **Focus:** membrane, metabolism and informational coupling in synthetic protocells. **Limit:** synthesis of proposals, not universal proof of an origin-of-life sequence. **Test:** compare systems with/without feedback among metabolism, compartment and heredity.
5. **Gerald Joyce / NASA astrobiology (2013).** [Defining Life: Q&A](https://astrobiology.nasa.gov/news/defining-life-qa-with-scientist-gerald-joyce/). **Important nuance:** NASA's widely repeated definition is provisional, emphasizes chemistry for astrobiology and is not officially binding for digital organisms. **Do not** classify digital life true or false by definition alone.

## II. Digital self-replication and experimental evolution
6. **Thomas S. Ray (1991).** [An approach to the synthesis of life](https://tomray.me/pubs/index.html), in *Artificial Life II* (primary author publication index includes paper). **Tierra:** instruction programs competing for CPU/memory, mutating and evolving. **Limit:** protected memory / scheduling and lack of endogenous upkeep. **Test:** independent lineage and internal resource-budget auditing, then ecological coupling.
7. **Charles Ofria and Claus O. Wilke (2004).** [Avida: A Software Platform for Research in Computational Evolutionary Biology](https://doi.org/10.1162/106454604773563612), *Artificial Life*. **Reported:** experiment-ready, mutation/heredity, virtual CPUs. **Limit:** designed instruction economics and logic rewards; not an automatically emergent life definition. **Test:** selection neutrality and survival without human-chosen logic bonus.
8. **Richard E. Lenski, Charles Ofria, Robert T. Pennock, Christoph Adami (2003).** [The evolutionary origin of complex features](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature01568), *Nature*. **Reported:** cumulative evolutionary stepping stones produce complex logical phenotypes in Avida. **Limit:** bounded experimental task environment; no sustained universal OEE. **Test:** withhold whole task families and compare evolutionary routes.
9. **Emily Dolson and Charles Ofria (2021).** [Digital Evolution for Ecology Research: A Review](https://doi.org/10.3389/fevo.2021.750779), *Frontiers in Ecology and Evolution*. **Evidence:** reviewed ecological interactions including cooperation, parasitism, predation, cross-feeding. **Limit:** cross-system designs differ and may depend on hand-built resource interactions. **Test:** resource-byproduct ablations and ecological cause/effect.
10. **Russell K. Standish (2002).** [Open-Ended Artificial Evolution](https://arxiv.org/abs/nlin/0210027). **Reported negative finding:** a size-neutral Tierra study shows increased organism length without growth in its chosen complexity statistic. **Limit:** one run/measure does not prove all potential is capped. **Test:** independent functional complexity vs genome-size measures.

## III. Continuous cellular artificial life and morphogenesis
11. **Bert Wang-Chak Chan (2018 preprint / later publication).** [Lenia — Biology of Artificial Life](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.05433). **Evidence:** diverse complex spatial localized motile patterns. **Limit:** visual/recovery dynamics ≠ heredity/biological evolution; historical rule sets can be incompatible.
12. **Erwan Plantec, Gautier Hamon, Mayalen Etcheverry, Pierre-Yves Oudeyer, Clément Moulin-Frier, Bert Wang-Chak Chan (2022 preprint, 2023 conference).** [Flow-Lenia: Towards Open-Ended Evolution in Cellular Automata through Mass Conservation and Parameter Localization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.07906). **Mechanism:** mass transport + localized rule parameters supports coexisting field-based "species." **Limit:** open-ended novelty not conclusively proven.
13. **Erwan Plantec et al. (2025).** [Flow-Lenia: Emergent Evolutionary Dynamics in Mass Conservative Continuous Cellular Automata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08569), *Artificial Life* 31(2), DOI [10.1162/artl_a_00471](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00471). **Mechanism:** flow, conserved matter, local parameters and activity metrics. **Limit:** no blanket proof of organisms or infinite innovation. **Test:** lineage heritability, repair, resource-dependence under hidden perturbations.
14. **Alexander Mordvintsev, Ettore Randazzo, Eyvind Niklasson, Michael Levin (2020).** [Growing Neural Cellular Automata](https://doi.org/10.23915/distill.00023), *Distill*. **Reported:** learning distributed local rules for growth and regeneration. **Limit:** training selects the target image or phenotype. **Test:** change damage and target selection; compare under zero image reward.
15. **Bo Yang (2025 volume; published online 2024/2025 metadata variants).** [Emergence of Self-Replicating Hierarchical Structures in a Binary Cellular Automaton](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00449), *Artificial Life* 31(1). **Reported:** self-duplicating structures across spatial scales from a symmetric two-state rule. **Limit:** new *function* and heritable evolution unverified. **Test:** reliable descendant identity, mutation retention and real adaptive effect.
16. **Uljad Berdica, Jakob Foerster, Frank Hutter, Arber Zela (2026; v2 June 22).** [Evolving Many Worlds: Towards Open-Ended Discovery in Petri Dish NCA via Population-Based Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11248). **Reported:** diverse emergent NCA patterns under meta-optimized novelty/diversity. **Critical limit:** designer explicitly supplies novelty/diversity objective; it is not a demonstration of goal-free origin.
17. **Mikhail Akhtyrchenko, Mikhail I. Katsnelson, Andrey Ustyuzhanin (2026 preprint).** [Directing Open-Ended Evolution in Artificial Life via Multi-Scale Path Divergence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17091). **Proposes:** multiscale functional of dynamics as a search/analysis criterion. **Limit:** optimizing any complexity metric risks reward hacking/metric-specific aesthetic progress. **Test:** independent functional novelty, with metric frozen and neutral controls.

## IV. Measuring open-endedness: positive theory and counterexamples
18. **Tim Taylor (2015).** [Requirements for Open-Ended Evolution in Natural and Artificial Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.07403); [complete author-hosted text](https://www.tim-taylor.com/papers/taylor2015requirements.web.html). **Five argued requirements:** robust reproduction; huge space of interactions; offspring capable of increased complexity; viable mutational paths; ongoing evolutionary drive. **This is a theory/research proposal, not a proven sufficient theorem.** **Full public text read** (sections 1–3 and notes).
19. **Tim Taylor, Mark Bedau et al. (2016).** [Open-Ended Evolution: Perspectives from the OEE Workshop in York](https://doi.org/10.1162/ARTL_A_00210), *Artificial Life*. **Key conclusion:** multiple types of OEE; hallmarks separate from causes. **Limit:** conceptual consensus conference report, not a demonstrated universal engine.
20. **Arend Hintze (2019).** [Open-Endedness for the Sake of Open-Endedness](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00289), *Artificial Life*. **Counterexample:** simple evolving model may satisfy formal OEE constraints while missing biological richness. **Test:** require independent functional novelty and organizational transitions.
21. **Joel Lehman and Kenneth O. Stanley (2011).** [Abandoning objectives: evolution through the search for novelty alone](https://doi.org/10.1162/EVCO_a_00025), *Evolutionary Computation*. **Evidence:** nonobjective novelty search can escape deceptive objectives in studied problems. **Limit:** novelty is still a specified reward; not synonymous with intrinsic natural selection.
22. **Jean-Baptiste Mouret and Jeff Clune (2015).** [Illuminating search spaces by mapping elites](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.04909). **MAP-Elites:** builds diverse archive across researcher-selected descriptors. **Limit:** choice of descriptors channels what can be discovered.
23. **Rui Wang, Joel Lehman, Jeff Clune, Kenneth O. Stanley (2019).** [Paired Open-Ended Trailblazer (POET)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.01753). **Evidence:** co-generating environments and solvers yields transfer/stepping stones. **Limit:** environment generator and acceptance criteria are explicit, not metabolism or heredity.

## V. Recent search, ecological and hybrid directions
24. **Akarsh Kumar, Chris Lu, Louis Kirsch, Yujin Tang, Kenneth O. Stanley, Phillip Isola, David Ha (2024).** [Automating the Search for Artificial Life with Foundation Models (ASAL)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.17799). **Method:** VLM-judged search across Lenia, Boids, CA, NCA. **Limit:** external high-level evaluator can determine the appearance of novelty; do not conflate searcher with organism.
25. **Nikhil Baid, Hannah Erlebach, Paul Hellegouarch, Frederico Wieser (2025).** [Guiding Evolution of Artificial Life Using Vision-Language Models (ASAL++)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22447). **Mechanism:** models propose changing visual targets. **Limit:** goals are supplied from outside the evaluated simulated organism.
26. **Ricard Solé / Carlos Gershenson (2023; check authors in full metadata).** [Emergence in Artificial Life](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00397). **Focus:** emergence as scale-dependent information. **Limit:** information alone is not causal self-maintenance.
27. **Alan Dorin and Susan Stepney (2024).** [What Is Artificial Life Today, and Where Should It Go?](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_e_00435), *Artificial Life*. **Role:** editorial/agenda spanning many ALife challenges; *not* an experiment.
28. **Adrien Ecoffet, Jeff Clune, Joel Lehman (2020).** [Open Questions in Creating Safe Open-ended AI](https://doi.org/10.1162/isal_a_00323), *ALIFE*. **Role:** open-endedness and control/safety tradeoffs.
29. **Aidan Barbieux and Rodrigo Canaan (2023).** [EINCASM: Emergent Intelligence in Neural Cellular Automaton Slime Molds](https://doi.org/10.1162/isal_a_00703), *ALIFE*. **Method:** NCA/NEAT with resource limits and simulated physical transport. **Limit:** preliminary prototype, explicitly selects growth fitness; do not call general intelligence.
30. **Claire Glanois, Shyam Sudhakaran, Elias Najarro, Sebastian Risi (2023).** [Open-Ended Library Learning in Unsupervised Program Synthesis](https://doi.org/10.1162/isal_a_00685), *ALIFE*. **Method:** recursively reusable symbolic programs. **Limit:** domain/DSL-dependent innovation, not organisms.
31. **James M. Borg et al. (2022).** [Evolved Open-Endedness in Cultural Evolution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.13050). **Proposal:** cumulative cultural change as a distinct open-endedness reference. **Limit:** transmission may depend on pre-existing intelligent agents and institutions.
32. **Recent ALIFE proceedings portal.** [ALIFE 2026](https://alife.org/conference/alife-2026/) (Waterloo, Canada, August 17–21, 2026), [MIT conference proceedings](https://direct.mit.edu/isal/isal2026/volume/38). **Coverage status:** provenance/venue confirmed; **individual 2026 proceedings not comprehensively indexed** in this pass.

## VI. Second research pass — newly confirmed primary sources

35. **Hiroki Sayama and Chrystopher L. Nehaniv (2025).** [Self-Reproduction and Evolution in Cellular Automata: 25 Years After Evoloops](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00451), *Artificial Life* 31(1), 81–95. **Review:** distinguishes self-copying from evolution-capable offspring; compares von Neumann and Langton conditions. **Limit:** survey is not new independent ALife reproduction.

36. **Randall D. Beer (2020).** [An Investigation into the Origin of Autopoiesis](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00307), *Artificial Life* 26(1), 5–22. **Method:** statistical treatment of creation, persistence and destruction of Game-of-Life gliders as minimal organizational examples. **Limit:** glider is an explicit toy model, not a metabolizing cell.

37. **Thomas Gabor et al. (2022).** [Self-Replication in Neural Networks](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00359), *Artificial Life* 28(2), 205–223. **Method:** self-application and training of neural network weight configurations, artificial soups and robustness to noise. **Limit:** weight-space fixpoints are not a full organism with inherited evolving function.

38. **Doron Segré, Barak Shenhav, Ron Kafri, Doron Lancet (2001).** [The molecular roots of compositional inheritance](https://doi.org/10.1006/jtbi.2001.2440), *Journal of Theoretical Biology* 213(3), 481–491. **Result in abstract:** inheritance fidelity depends on catalytic coefficients and assembly size; particular distributions produce compositional error catastrophe. **Limit:** model-dependent rates.

39. **Hanna Derets and Chrystopher L. Nehaniv (2025).** [Survival and Evolutionary Adaptation of Populations Under Disruptive Habitat Change](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00457), *Artificial Life* 31(1), 106–123. **Method:** stochastic spatial Darwinian CA; explores habitat-related survival/extinction thresholds. **Limit:** experiment parameters and original code not yet audited.

## VII. Fourth research pass — self-replicating distributed organization and evolving semantics

40. **Arend Hintze and Clifford Bohm (2026).** [Rethinking self-replication: detecting distributed selfhood in the outlier cellular automaton](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44260-026-00074-2), *npj Complexity* 3, article 11, published **16 February 2026**; [full arXiv manuscript v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.08047). **E2:** authors build a necessary-cause DAG and report **15 generations of exact c2 cluster replication**, showing spatially distributed causal lineage. **Limits:** one selected Outlier seed/physics and exact-copy cluster criterion; no inherited novel function or independent organism execution. ## VIII. Fifth research pass: inherited computational semantics and its failure modes
48. **Attila Egri-Nagy and Chrystopher L. Nehaniv (2003).** [Evolvability of the Genotype-Phenotype Relation in Populations of Self-Replicating Digital Organisms in a Tierra-like System](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-540-39432-7_26), ECAL 2003, LNCS 2801:238–247. **E2 [full author-deposited paper review](28-physis-2003-full-review.md):** genomes encode processor structures and intermediate instruction semantics, but a fixed universal processor executes them. Structural variants rarely replace ancestry under five-seed tests; no clear increased evolutionary potential demonstrated over fixed processors. [Public current Python port](https://github.com/alyssa-adams/physis_python) inspected at pinned Git SHA; no code run or root software license established. The 2026 GPU Physis implementation is **not proven to be this port**.
49. **Edward B. Clark, Simon J. Hickinbotham and Susan Stepney (2017).** [Semantic closure demonstrated by the evolution of a universal constructor architecture in an artificial chemistry](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsif.2016.1033), *Journal of the Royal Society Interface* 14:20161033, open [PMC article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5454285/). **E2 [full-publication review](27-semantic-closure-2017-full-review.md):** 39 viable takeover events among 500 deliberately hand-initialized UCA worlds, 32 affecting copier/expressor, including changes in interpreting an unchanged genome. **Negative result:** "bureaucratic death" from incompatible expression variants; payload HELLOWORLD does not constitute new ecological function.

## IX. Sixth ALife research pass — observer-defined novelty versus intrinsic change

50. **Wolfgang Banzhaf et al. (2016).** [Defining and Simulating Open-Ended Novelty: Requirements, Guidelines, and Challenges](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12064-016-0229-7), *Theory in Biosciences* 135:131–161. **E2 [31-page full primary review](36-banzhaf-2016-open-ended-novelty-full-review.md):** type 0/1/2 novelty depends on scientific model/meta-model; **pre-installed individuality, replication and fitness are shortcuts** and cannot be re-credited as emergent mechanisms at their supplied level.
