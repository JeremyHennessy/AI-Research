# Pass 19 — Brock 2011: original observation, incomplete access

Debra A. Brock, Tracy E. Douglas, David C. Queller and Joan E. Strassmann (2011), **Primitive agriculture in a social amoeba**, Nature 469:393–396. [Primary publisher page](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature09668), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature09668).

**E1, checked 2026-10-08.** The public publisher page supplies an abstract and figure titles but marks the main article as subscription content. Complete methods were not obtained. A freely visible citation or PDF search result is not proof of full-text access. No source PDF is copied or redistributed.

The abstract reports carriage of bacteria during spore dispersal and a benefit when suitable food is missing at the destination, offset by costs where food is available. This establishes the historical research question, not a fully audited transmission mechanism.

The [2015 causal follow-up](101-pass19-disalvo-2015-transmitted-carriage-full-review.md) and [2019 localization/dispersal study](102-pass19-khojandi-2019-propagule-context-full-review.md) were read in full. They must be cited for their own findings rather than used to retroactively label this article E2. Their results also make an exclusively host-genetic explanation inadequate. All three papers belong to a connected research program; publication count is not a count of independent origins.

For future reading, obtain a lawful complete primary copy with its online methods before promoting this record. No missing methods, sample sizes or molecular causes are inferred here.
