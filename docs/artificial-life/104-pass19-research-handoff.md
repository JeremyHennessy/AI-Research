## Scientific conclusion and next priority

There is source-reported evidence of useful function after transmission and relocation, but it is not universal transmission through isolated offspring. The inherited process may enable acquisition of replacement partners rather than preserve every original component. These studies do not demonstrate new digital life, general intelligence or subjective consciousness.

Next: an independent host system with quantitative tracking of vertical transmission versus environmental reacquisition and measured descendant function. Keep host ancestry, partner ancestry, carried environment and new recruitment separate. Retain every starting propagule in an unconditional outcome measure.
