# Pass 20 — Kikuchi 2007: an experimental environmental-acquisition contrast

Yoshitomo Kikuchi, Takahiro Hosokawa and Takema Fukatsu (2007), **Insect-Microbe Mutualism without Vertical Transmission: a Stinkbug Acquires a Beneficial Gut Symbiont from the Environment Every Generation**, Applied and Environmental Microbiology 73:4308–4316. [Complete publisher article](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/aem.00067-07), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1128/AEM.00067-07).

**E2, 2026-10-08:** complete main article, Methods, Results, Discussion, main captions and Tables 1–2 read from ASM. Supplemental Table S1, raw sequence data and scientific experiments were not independently analyzed. Public reading access does not confer unrestricted redistribution; this review contains original analysis, not a copy of the source.

## Author-reported evidence

Table 1 reports no detected symbiont in 144 eggs or 107 adults reared with infected parents in clean cases; 104/114 adults reared without parents on soil-grown plants were positive. These are five family broods, not independent origins of the association.

In a separate controlled addition comparison spanning four broods, adult detection was 0/83 without symbiont addition and 93/93 with it. Table 2 starts with 120 eggs per arm: detection is conditional on adult emergence, not success across every starting egg. Colonized adults were heavier and larger; emergence did not improve consistently across families. The biochemical benefit mechanism remained unknown.

## Causal reading

Environmental acquisition is supported by converging interventions, not merely by finding matching bacteria outside hosts. The first parent/environment contrast changes more than parental contact, so it would be insufficient alone. The controlled addition comparison adds a more specific causal test. Sequence matching and localization strengthen identity, while finite sampling and detection limits constrain negative findings.

High prevalence in the field is consequently not evidence of direct parent-to-offspring transfer. Likewise, a relationship can be beneficial without being essential under every laboratory condition. Reports labelled unpublished in the Discussion must not be treated as another independently audited experiment.

The outcomes separate partner acquisition from host performance. Body dimensions are growth-related endpoints; they do not measure lifetime offspring production. Do not translate the article's broad fitness language into a demonstrated multigenerational reproductive advantage or a complete ecological life cycle.

## Independence and external support

Individual eggs and adults are nested within families and shared rearing environments. Family terms in the analysis do not turn each insect into an independent evolutionary origin. Field observations also draw on previous work; the prevalence estimate is background rather than a newly replicated origin in this paper.

Researchers supply the rearing environment and, in the controlled comparison, the partner. The host's capacity to establish the association is naturally present; its evolutionary origin was not observed. This is evidence for reassembly of an existing association, not creation of a new living unit. No digital architecture, organism or consciousness conclusion follows directly.

## Consequence for the research path

A future digital claim of inherited partnership needs source-tracked partner ancestry. Successful reconstruction from environmental partners is a distinct, potentially useful outcome. Report both without forcing a vertical route or calling all recruitment inheritance. Evidence of improved growth should remain separate from evidence of improved descendant production.
