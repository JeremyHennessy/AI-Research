# Pass 20 — Russell 2018: egg association is stronger evidence than environmental DNA alone

S. L. Russell, E. McCartney and C. M. Cavanaugh (2018), **Transmission strategies in a chemosynthetic symbiosis: detection and quantification of symbionts in host tissues and their environment**, Proceedings of the Royal Society B 285:20182157. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2018.2157), [complete primary PDF supplied by the author](https://www.shelbirussell.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Russell-et-al.-Transmission-strategies-in-a-chemosynthetic-symbio.pdf).

**E2, 2026-10-08:** complete main article, main Methods, Results, Discussion and captions read; Figure 2 visually inspected in the nine-page PDF. Separate supplementary methods/results and source code were not read or executed. The PDF states all rights reserved; the author-hosted copy was read in place and is not redistributed here. The article's “no additional data” statement is not a claim that this project reproduced the measurements.

## Author-reported evidence

The estimated abundance is 64.1 ± 17.4 s.e. symbiont gene copies per egg. Egg-associated signal, including in surface-treated samples, and ovarian localization support vertical transmission. The authors nevertheless leave precise membrane location unresolved. Environmental symbiont DNA is scarce and commonly accompanied by host DNA, which complicates interpretation as free-living bacteria. The authors infer infrequent ecological horizontal transmission despite earlier evolutionary-genomic evidence of exchange.

## What those measurements do not identify

A single-copy marker estimates genome equivalents, not automatically viable bacterial cells, successful colonization or functional descendants. DNA can persist without a living, transmissible organism. Host DNA in an environmental sample can indicate carried host material; absence of that marker does not independently establish bacterial viability.

The design combines egg association, tissue localization and environmental sampling. It does not observe and count source-tracked horizontal acquisition events across generations. A low environmental concentration is a proxy for opportunity, not a measured transmission probability. Genomic evidence accumulated across evolutionary time can reflect rare events with large consequences; it does not specify how often an offspring recruits a partner now.

## Replication, detection and localization

Methods describe 25 egg extracts, including single and pooled eggs, with technical replication. Figure 2 labels 13 untreated and 12 treated samples, whereas the Results' abundance summary reports n=79. Do not relabel that last figure as 79 independently tracked offspring. Reconciling every nested sample and reaction would require the supplemental material or underlying records.

Failed amplification cannot be read directly as failed inheritance: assay sensitivity, treatment and extraction also affect detection. Equal group means do not establish that every egg carries the same load. The microscopy cannot conclusively distinguish immediately intracellular from closely external association at all relevant boundaries. The authors explicitly retain that limitation rather than infer a complete cellular entry mechanism.

## Source independence and claim scope

This is an independent clam system relative to the amoeba and insect studies. Within the clam research program, it builds on earlier genomic findings, rather than supplying a second direct measurement of historical exchange. Those cited studies were not newly reviewed or counted as sources in this pass.

The paper strengthens evidence for an egg-associated route but does not independently test the functional output of tracked descendants. It therefore answers a different part of the transmission question from the insect acquisition intervention. A cross-species comparison cannot estimate a universal rate or identify a single superior mechanism.

## Consequence for the research path

Record provenance, physical association, live activity and descendant function separately. An inherited object identifier or a copied state in software could imitate molecular presence without reproducing causal function. Future evaluation should require the corresponding functional evidence while preserving uncertainty about the physical route.
