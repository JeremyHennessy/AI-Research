## Interpretation and next step

Partner prevalence cannot identify the route of transmission. Environmental acquisition can reconstruct a beneficial association, while egg-associated DNA does not itself prove viable partner function in descendants. These are evidence-supported distinctions, not new demonstrations of a reproducing digital organism or consciousness.

Next review: causal selective recruitment.
