# Pass 21 — Ohbayashi 2015: what a sorting result establishes

**Primary source:** Ohbayashi et al., [Insect’s intestinal organ for symbiont sorting](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1511454112). **E2: complete published main article read**, including Methods and captions on the publisher HTML. Embedded supporting tables were visible; the separate supplemental package, raw data and scientific code were not independently audited. No experiment reproduced.

## Evidence receipt

Spatial observations distinguish passage from anterior exposure. Bacterial perturbation and rescue implicate motility, but motile comparators can still fail entry. The comparator panel is narrow. The study examines existing anatomy and partner capacities; proposed evolutionary ancestry is an interpretation, not an observed origin.

## Original causal assessment

The unit of inference matters. Images of cells, counts of cultured colonies and counts of colonized insects answer different questions. Cells within one insect are not independent host replicates. An absent posterior signal can reflect a barrier, mortality, insufficient proliferation or detection limits; localization near the boundary and converging measurement types make the stage interpretation more informative than a single final count.

Perturbing a bacterial property and restoring it can support a causal requirement. It does not by itself identify the complementary host mechanism. In particular, the logical statements “a partner requires this capacity” and “the host recognizes the best partner” are distinct. Demonstrating the former cannot establish the latter without an appropriate host-side contrast and functional outcome.

A filter may be permissive for several candidate partners, followed by differential persistence. Therefore the relevant comparison class must include plausible alternative colonizers, not only distant negative controls. The [2019 access-bounded note](110-pass21-itoh-2019-source-boundary.md) identifies a follow-up that must be read completely before its competition mechanism receives E2 weight.

## Consequence for EERC

For a future digital comparison, report where a candidate failed: availability, contact, entry, persistence or contribution to reproduction. A pre-installed interface can test consequences of filtering, but its existence cannot count as an evolved filter. This is an interpretation of evidentiary requirements, not a new experiment or change to EERC v1–v7.
