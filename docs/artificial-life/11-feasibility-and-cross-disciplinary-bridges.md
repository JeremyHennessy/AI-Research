# Feasibility today and cross-disciplinary opportunities

**2026-10-08 | Research synthesis, not implementation.** Claims about computational feasibility are qualitative engineering inferences, not measured benchmarks on allocated hardware. No test world/organism has been built.

## A. What is reasonably testable with accessible compute?

| Scientific question | Candidate substrate | Smallest useful measurement | Likely compute constraints | What is *not* credible from a small positive run |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Can copying instructions produce heritable variation? | Bounded instruction-based digital ecology | Parent/offspring instruction lineage, mutation and nontrivial viable descendants | CPU-friendly small populations; long generations can still be costly | Open-ended cognitive evolution |
| Can internal processes repair organization? | Abstract stochastic reaction network | Intervention on feedback cycle, controlled damage, restoration and resource ledger | CPU-friendly small networks; stiff chemical kinetics can be costly | Complete living protocell or biochemistry |
| Can local physics produce long-lived localized structures? | Lenia / continuous conservative field | Persistence, damage and passive-attractor controls | 2D convolution grids CPU or consumer GPU; large multi-species/multiscale simulations costly | Autonomous life or heritable intelligence |
| Can a developmental update rule regenerate after unseen injury? | Neural or non-neural cellular automata | Repair beyond trained pattern/damage type, independent tests | Small models/grids feasible; training and image-target bias may dominate | Intrinsic goal emergence if image loss supplied |
| Can ecological diversity sustain novel functions? | Multi-resource instruction ecology, reaction network or localized fields | Resource/byproduct intervention, new capability transfer to descendants | CPU prototypes possible; repeated long-horizon matched replicates raise cost | Proof of unlimited ecological innovation |
| Can a lineage evolve its own sensing/learning? | Ecological simulator with mutable state | Fitness difference under delayed cues, held-out resource change, cue scramble | Toy tasks plausible; robust evolutionary path unknown | General intelligence without selected benefit |
| Can new organismal units arise? | Symbiotic/collective ecology | New stable group lineage, reproduced collective functionality | Long stochastic runs and group identity tracking; high variance | Major biological transition demonstrated by one cluster |
| Can open-endedness continue indefinitely? | Any finite virtual world | Multiple finite-window functional innovation trajectories | Longer compute/time, huge parameter sweeps and hidden test designs | Infinite novelty or unrestricted intelligence |

**Practical prioritization:** choose the test with the **highest ability to falsify a core idea per unit of cost**. A 20×20 virtual ecology with trusted lineage logging can be more informative than a beautiful million-cell evolving landscape with no independent identity, resource or heredity measurement.

## B. Candidate intersections of disciplines, not yet established as integrated successes

### B1. Autopoiesis × software fault tolerance × resource accounting
**Observed research strands:** autopoietic organizational production [Varela et al., 1974](https://doi.org/10.1016/0303-2647(74)90031-8), computational digital ecology [Avida](https://doi.org/10.1162/106454604773563612), endogenous tissue regeneration [NCA](https://doi.org/10.23915/distill.00023).

**Hypothesis:** an organism-like structure requires not just error correction but a maintenance process that actively replaces the conditions that make its own error correction possible.

**Future discriminator:** damage the repair machinery itself versus damaging only stored data; see whether an internally maintained network restores functionality, compared with an external simulator recovery hook and a purely protected checksum system. **Counterargument:** self-repairing distributed software may satisfy operational tests while still being defined as not alive under material-metabolism theories. Clearly keep that interpretation distinct.

### B2. Open-ended heredity × developmental cellular computation
**Strands:** cumulative functional innovation in digital programs [Lenski et al., 2003](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature01568); morphogenesis in [Lenia](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.05433), [Flow-Lenia](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08569) and [NCA](https://doi.org/10.23915/distill.00023).

**Hypothesis:** a heritable program can encode operations that construct and repair a mutable spatial body, potentially making viable functional mutations easier to reach than brittle program-only or field-only systems.

**Discriminator:** modular hereditary program driving shared local physical interactions vs program and field controls, matched complexity/variation/compute. Check actual child traits and unfamiliar damage. **Negative result:** extra layers only inflate system complexity, while mutation destroys development or no new inherited functions arise.

### B3. Ecological niche construction × active inference × untrained agency
**Strands:** multi-resource ecological dynamics [digital ecology review](https://doi.org/10.3389/fevo.2021.750779), active inference exploration [2020 RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.12636), coupled environment challenges [POET](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.01753).

**Hypothesis:** in changing resource economies, processes that track predictive regularities may eventually gain a viability advantage even without a designer rewarding explicit prediction or task success.

**Discriminator:** in a future environment, scramble predictive cues while preserving immediate conditions, then test survival and descendant outcomes for plastic versus non-plastic lineages. **Counterargument:** selection may prefer simple reflexes; active inference theory does not establish spontaneous evolution of information-seeking cognition in an arbitrary ALife substrate.

### B4. Major transitions in individuality × symbiotic metabolic cooperation
**Strands:** evolutionary transitions [Szathmáry & Maynard Smith](https://doi.org/10.1038/374227a0), resource cross-feeding and ecological relations [digital ecology](https://doi.org/10.3389/fevo.2021.750779), artificial chemistries [Liu & Sumpter](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.RA118.003795).

**Hypothesis:** complementary byproduct exchange could select stable joint reproduction when single organisms cannot access scarce resources alone.

**Discriminator:** analyze whether groups generate inheritable new-level traits and survive partner manipulation; compare group benefit with mixed individual reproduction. **Negative result:** temporary clustering with no stable cooperative reproduction or partner-specific inheritance.

### B5. Multiple measures of open-ended evolution × causal interpretability
**Strands:** non-equivalent OEE metrics [Taylor et al., 2016](https://doi.org/10.1162/ARTL_A_00210), metric counterexamples [Hintze 2019](https://doi.org/10.1162/artl_a_00289), causal representation critique [Joshi et al., 2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/joshi26a.html).

**Hypothesis:** identifying changes in the *causal organization* of functional flows across lineages could reject attractor aesthetics and proxy optimization more reliably than pixel/entropy/trajectory novelty scores.

**Discriminator:** independent reviewers, perturbed process networks, blinded inherited function tests and null-generated graph structure. **Failure:** the organization scorer is itself a gameable proxy or cannot reliably identify boundaries when organisms split or merge.

### B6. Cultural information × genetic heredity × ecology
**Strands:** cultural OEE [Borg et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.13050), evolved program libraries [Glanois et al.](https://doi.org/10.1162/isal_a_00685), major transitions [1995](https://doi.org/10.1038/374227a0).

**Hypothesis:** ecology-dependent *horizontal* transfer of reusable procedures may increase the range of viable innovations even without a direct genome-like inheritance mechanism for every behavior.

**Discriminator:** compare inherited-only, horizontally shared-only and joint transmission with the same total information and resource costs. Require functional descendants and activity after loss of external copying helpers. **Caution:** cultural transmission requires a working interpreter, replicator or learning medium; transmission alone does not create a living organism.

## C. Architecture selection as a research decision
These are **not rankings of which substrate is most "alive"**. They rank ability to answer a narrowly specified scientific question with constrained resources.

| Fundamental uncertainty | Fastest informative first approach | Necessary alternative comparator |
|---|---|---|
| Lineage/historical contingency | Digital instruction ecology | Neutral randomized heredity and mutation controls |
| Causal repair and coupled metabolism | Abstract chemical network | Passive equilibrium and external-repair hooks |
| Emergent morphology and localized dynamic identity | Conservative cellular field | Nonconservative field and inert attractor |
| Developmental adaptation | NCA or local growth rule | Scripted fixed body and damage-trained control |
| Ecological niche creation | Coupled resource ecology | Matched forced environmental changes |
| Evolution of sensory prediction | Ecology with delayed resource cues | Reflex-only and cue-shuffled lineages |
| Open-ended dynamics | At least two independent substrates | Metric-based null/counterexample worlds |

**The first experiment should not combine every idea.** Select one intervention, one control, one outcome, frozen seeds and objective bounds. Every apparently positive result must survive a changed environment and an alternative evaluator.

## D. Findings that would shift our judgment
- **Toward genuinely endogenous life-like autonomy:** same process survives resource variations through constituent replacement; destruction of a specific internal repair cycle causes predictable failure; lineage descendants inherit regulation; null oscillators don't pass.
- **Toward Darwinian evolving organismhood:** confirmed reproduction, mutating hereditary units and measurable new niche functions that survive lineage transfer and removal of explicit human-task rewards.
- **Toward open-endedness hallmarks:** many independent seeds show sustained increasing *functional* possibilities over precommitted horizons, new levels of organization and no early metric saturation versus neutral controls. This still does not establish mathematical infinity.
- **Against an attractive theory:** removal of designer resets or novelty scorer erases progress; no functional heredity; only appearance changes; evolution cannot continue after early niche saturation; meaningful effect vanishes after controlling extra compute.

## E. Evidence and authority boundaries
- **Empirical publication report:** explicit source link and studied conditions, not independent replication.
- **Cross-disciplinary bridge:** original synthesis and hypothesis, not established precedent.
- **Proof-of-concept feasibility:** rough engineering judgment based on state size and computation, not a measured GPU bill.
- **Digital consciousness:** no supported claim, measurement or inference here.
- **Separate project authorization:** absolutely required for building or running any proposed artificial organism, evolutionary ecosystem or world. This document does not grant that authorization.
