# Pass 35 — Evolving genetic templates versus reproducing physical compartments

**2026-10-09 | Original primary-source critical review | Research only.** Starting live main at preparation: `9ed45ff5105dd8bd615c295dd04cbc8532ac62c8`. This pass follows [Pass 34](127-pass34-dispersal-life-cycle-versus-ecological-endogenisation.md) but changes the scientific system: it compares an evolving DNA template in manufactured liposomes against abiotic polymer vesicles that reportedly produce vesicle offspring. It asks whether either establishes **joint, independently propagating inheritance of useful genetic function and a workable daughter compartment**. These are **two different technologies, not independent replications of one protocol**.

No new organisms, artificial environments, software models, simulations, experiments, training or code execution were made for this scientific review. No interpretation here implies consciousness, intelligence, a new species, or open-ended evolution.

## A. Abil et al. (2024): adaptive template replication inside externally reconstructed vesicles

**Primary:** Abil, Restrepo Sierra, Stan et al., *Darwinian Evolution of Self-Replicating DNA in a Synthetic Protocell*, *Nature Communications* 15:9091 (22 October 2024), [DOI 10.1038/s41467-024-53226-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-53226-0), [publisher primary text](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-53226-0). **E2-depth** main article Results, Discussion, Methods, figure captions, limitations and rights reviewed. Supplementary protocols, underlying sequencing and source data were *not* independently recomputed, and this is not E3 replication. Main article is CC BY 4.0 subject to third-party notices; this review uses original paraphrase and links, not copied figures or data.

**Actual inherited unit.** A roughly 3.2-kb linear engineered DNA template expresses bacteriophage Phi29 DNA polymerase and terminal protein. The **PURE cell-free translation system**, supporting proteins, lipids and nucleotide resources were deliberately supplied. In vesicles, copies of DNA can express the factors that copy the DNA. This is a compelling genotype–protein-phenotype loop, not an endogenously synthesized translation or membrane infrastructure.

**Two intervention regimes cannot be combined into one autonomous lineage:**

- **Intermittent selection:** after growth in experimentally prepared liposomes, investigators opened compartments, extracted the DNA, PCR-amplified and sometimes selected the full-length product, then repackaged the next generation in newly prepared liposomes. There were **three named evolution histories**: Int-WT(1), Int-WT(2), and Int-Mut. Int-WT(1) improved self-amplification at least fivefold across 12 rounds; **Int-WT(2) showed persistence without a comparable gain**. Int-Mut's claimed gain is causally difficult to parse because NGS detected a likely contaminating influx from Int-WT(1) and reversion at the initial F62Y proofreading mutation. The paper itself recognizes this limitation. PCR-dependent diversity and size-selection-assisted survival are researcher-mediated, not unassisted offspring transmission.
- **Semi-continuous Con-WT:** between reaction rounds investigators supplied fresh premanufactured feeding vesicles, diluted cultures **100-fold**, and forced membrane mixing and content redistribution with **freeze–thaw**. This removed the inter-round extraction/PCR requirement but **did not** produce membrane growth, spontaneous daughter divisions, or passive ecological establishment. Full-length DNA persisted for **at least five rounds**, and approximately **50% DNA leakage** during freeze–thaw plus external DNA amplification permitted some genetic material to survive outside compartments. Thus DNA persistence cannot establish continuous ancestry of an intact parent liposome and its daughter liposome.

**Comparator and causal reconstruction.** A bulk, no-liposome serial-transfer experiment lost the full-length self-replicator by round six as shorter molecular parasites gained ground. Its **10-fold dilution differs from the 100-fold Con-WT transfer**; the authors report supplementary altered-dilution controls, but those raw controls were not independently reanalyzed here. Reverse-engineered DNA substitutions **S79G and A80T** gave significant in-liposome amplification improvement, whereas the corresponding purified polymerase proteins did not outperform wild-type under bulk replication assays. This supports *assay-context-specific inherited DNA-level fitness*; it is **not** evidence of a new ecological function independently maintained after synthetic-cell support is withdrawn. Replicate assay measurements are nested under the campaign histories, and independent experiment count is not the number of wells or rounds.

**Key disconfirming evidence:** one WT evolution history with no gain; the mutator campaign's contamination/reversion; competition from short parasitic fragments without liposomes; substantial extracompartment DNA leakage; a genotype effect that does not transfer to purified bulk enzyme performance. The initial synthetic template was optimized to limit a specific parasitic 1.4-kb fragment, itself an investigator design decision. Each finding constrains the interpretation rather than negating the molecular result.

## B. Katla, Lin & Pérez-Mercader (2025): membrane-like offspring without demonstrated selectable genotype

**Primary:** *Self-reproduction as an autonomous process of growth and reorganization in fully abiotic, artificial and synthetic cells*, *PNAS* 122(22):e2412514122 (online 27 May 2025), [DOI 10.1073/pnas.2412514122](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2412514122), [full PNAS Results, Discussion and Methods summary](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2412514122), [PMC archive](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12146737/). **Upgraded from the previous abstract-only candidate to E2-depth complete PRIMARY MAIN TEXT.** The SI appendix, figures at raw-pixel level, individual micrographs and spreadsheet of original measurements were not independently reanalysed. PNAS marks the main paper **CC BY 4.0**; archive availability is not proof of separate supplementary/third-party rights.

**What actually happens.** A prescribed **photo-RAFT polymerization-induced self-assembly** chemical system is prepared by investigators and exposed to green light. Amphiphiles form, self-organize into micelles and then polymer vesicles. The authors' mechanistic proposal is that continued reaction inside older vesicles builds up amphiphile congestion; material is expelled, reorganizes outside, and yields new vesicles that can in turn repeat the production process *while reagents and illumination remain available*. Their term 'autonomous' applies to this local chemical process, **not** independence from a prepared reactor, starting reagents and energy.

**Source-critical positive and negative contrasts:**

- Investigators filtered the active mixture through a **0.22-micrometer PTFE filter**. The preexisting-vesicle-enriched **retentate** produced a rising count of vesicles under **12 hours of pulsed green illumination**. The amphiphile/micelle-containing **filtrate** did **not** form visible vesicles under the compared irradiation. This is positive evidence of *parent-associated generation* as opposed to straightforward visible bulk nucleation in that measured configuration. It is not, by itself, individually tracked parent–offspring pedigrees.
- Figure 3's population and mean-diameter time series average **three experiments run on separate dates using the same stock solutions**. Those are independent runs of a physical assay, **not three evolved founder genotypes**, three independent origin-of-life transitions, or three successfully founded distant ecosystems. Multi-peak changing size distributions and nonmonotonic average diameter support reproduction but do not uniquely reconstruct every vesicle's physical ancestry.
- **94% of newly observed vesicles** fluoresced for **ZnTPP photocatalyst** in the reported assay. Its presence is relevant chemical continuity. However, the filtration control also found no detectable ZnTPP in the filtrate, and that filtrate produced no new vesicles. **Catalyst localization and parent-material carryover are therefore not individually isolated causes of the negative filtrate result.** A ZnTPP-positive vesicle is not necessarily a distinct inherited *genetic* lineage, and the exact per-parent success/failure denominator is not reported in the main text reviewed.
- The authors propose **loose conformational/chain-length heritable variation** in expelled polymeric material. This is a mechanistic hypothesis consistent with chemistry; the inspected paper does **not** reconstruct stable variant identities over multiple pedigreed generations, experimentally select competing variants for inherited fitness, or demonstrate an RNA/DNA-coded functional template. The described ejection and multiple generations are partly mechanism-based inferences from optical and separation data, not a prospectively enumerated phylogeny.
- The reaction receives no additional chemical feed after its aliquot is sealed onto a microscope slide, which **is an important reduction of manual intervention during the observation**. It still depends on precisely chosen initial chemical stores, externally maintained temperature/illumination and a limited resource budget; the monomer is consumed and population growth changes regimes. Environmental energy input alone should not be misclassified as a defect—ordinary life uses energy. The unresolved issue is autonomous *renewal, inherited function and persistent parent-to-daughter identity*.

**Remaining method questions:** the full supplemental population-analysis and additional controls remain unaudited; microscopy detection thresholds, intermittent field of view, ZnTPP-dependent filtrate behavior, alternative microassembly below the filter threshold, the full failed-lineage distribution, and effects when initial monomer supplies run low are not resolved by this review. These are **uncertainties**, not falsifications or claimed findings.

## Cross-study synthesis: distinct achievements, missing compound gate

| Reproduction or inheritance gate | Abil et al. 2024 | Katla et al. 2025 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A heritable encoding molecule causally reconstructed for fitness | **Yes**, engineered DNA S79G/A80T in liposome replication | **No demonstrated DNA/RNA genotype**; proposed polymer-composition continuity |
| Vesicle populations forming additional vesicles by internal physical chemistry | **No**; compartments manufactured/reconstituted and freeze–thaw mixed | **Reported**, supported by retentate/filtrate comparisons and microscopy |
| Unambiguous individual parent–daughter vesicle ancestry over many cycles | **Not shown**; DNA leak and synthetic fusion/fission | **Not established** from direct individually tagged pedigrees |
| Independent founders and disconfirming cases recorded | Three intermittent campaign histories; no-gain and contamination; one semi-continuous campaign | Three assay-day repeats using shared stocks; incomplete parent-level failures |
| Meaningful absence of researcher intervention | Partial: no-PCR Con-WT, but fed vesicles and forced freeze–thaw | No feed added during observation, but prepared batch/light/temperature |
| Integrated inherited genetic adaptation **and** natural compartment offspring reproduction | **Not demonstrated** | **Not demonstrated** |
| Scientific independence | Distinct DNA/liposome experimental program | Different abiotic-polymer group; **not a replication** of DNA study |

**Evidence-supported conclusion.** These studies establish *complementary pieces* of a possible life-like system: context-adaptive DNA replication in vesicles and parent-associated formation of abiotic daughter compartments with detectable transferred catalyst. **Neither reports a prospective, end-to-end, unassisted joint genome-plus-membrane lineage with causally inherited advantage, counted failed founders, and sustained daughter establishment.** The absence of that compound demonstration is an evidence gap, not proof it is chemically impossible, and it does not establish any form of cognition or consciousness.

## Falsifiable hypotheses for future independent researchers — NOT experiments performed

- **P35-H1 — genetic persistence can masquerade as vesicle inheritance.** A multicyle DNA template transferred with externally mixed membranes may persist even if no intact daughter compartment inherits both encoded function and its parent structure. **Reject** if individually marked parent compartments repeatedly produce identifiable functional daughter compartments that themselves transmit the exact encoded variants without external vesicle rebuilding, DNA rescue or nonparental DNA carryover, including all failed founders.
- **P35-H2 — parent-associated vesicle counts can overstate functional heredity.** Increasing retentate vesicle counts and daughter-associated ZnTPP need not imply stable, selectable inherited functional differences between separate lines. **Reject** if multiple prospectively tracked polymer-vesicle family lines pass distinct causal structural or catalytic differences through independently identified offspring with replicated, controlled differences in cumulative descendant formation, against shared-environment and catalyst-localization alternatives.
- **P35-H3 — complementary mechanisms need not compose into joint closure.** Genetically adaptive replication and chemically self-producing vesicles may each operate in separate experimental regimes while their coupling fails to transmit a viable genotype–compartment unit. **Reject** if independent, fully enumerated lineages maintain both functional encoded replication and physical daughter compartment production together for predeclared generations without investigator re-encapsulation, imposed membrane fusion, founder selection or rescue, with negative outcomes retained and naturally available resources distinguished from manual intergenerational transfer.

**Highest-information next audit:** identify genuinely independent prospective literature that explicitly traces a coupled genotype and enclosing physical compartment through daughter production and changing ecological resource conditions; classify each intervention as natural resource/energy input or deliberate reproductive rescue. Seek negative and nonreplicating lines, not just survivor exemplars. An unrelated independent RNA/protocell paper may supply a comparator, but it must not be silently called a replication of either source reviewed here.
