# Pass 42 — Biochemical constructor regeneration versus autonomous organizational closure

**Source-critical literature review; 10 October 2026; research only.** This pass evaluates published work on making the protein-synthesis machinery that, in turn, synthesizes proteins. It distinguishes *functional regeneration of some factors*, *reconstitution of a second-generation protein-synthesis reaction*, *sustained replacement over multiple rounds*, and *closure of the full constructor including ribosomes, RNA machinery, hereditary information and resource costs*. This document is not an experimental protocol or authorization to create an organism, run a simulation, train a model or modify Ora.

## A. Three original studies and three non-equivalent achievements

### A1. Mottaghi & Maerkl (2026): second-generation PURE reconstitution, but below sustained-renewal capacity

**Original:** Seyed Saeed Mottaghi and Sebastian J. Maerkl, “PURE makes PURE: reconstitution of the PURE cell-free system from self-synthesized proteins,” *Nature Communications* 17, 6756, published 22 May 2026; DOI https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-73337-0 ; [publisher main article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-73337-0). **Depth:** E2 original main Methods, Results, Discussion and reporting/statistics inspected through indexed original article; raw source ZIP and supplementary figures not independently reanalyzed. **Rights:** article CC BY 4.0, subject to third-party exclusions. This review is original paraphrase and reproduces no images, data or code.

The authors synthesized each of the 36 **non-ribosomal** PURE proteins. They purified products first in five pools, tested activity with corresponding omission reactions, and combined the pools to assemble a functional new PURE mixture. They also expressed the 36 proteins in a **single input PURE batch**, purified/concentrated the protein products, and assembled a functional second-generation protein-synthesis mixture. This demonstrates substantial encoded production and post-production functional recovery, *not* a self-running multigeneration loop.

**Controls and denominators:** full PURE positive control; a deletion PURE reaction with purification control as negative; concentration-matched E. coli-produced proteins as comparison. The main reconstitution functional tests and pool titrations report **n=2 technical replicates** rather than two independent evolutionary lineages. One reconstitution is described as approximately **7.6-fold below** the full reaction's protein concentration; authors estimate PURE synthesis capacity still approximately **an order of magnitude below** nondegenerative regeneration of all non-ribosomal proteins. Authors report no prespecified sample-size calculation, randomization or blinding. One-pot success was repeated in supplementary results, which were not independently audited here.

**Dependency/counterevidence:** preexisting ribosomes, nucleic acids, substrates and externally made starting PURE factors power the first reaction; beads, purification, concentration and operator mixing occur between generation 1 and 2. Ribosomal fractions can contain unintended other PURE proteins, so nominal deletion does not always prove that newly made copies alone supplied the essential activity. Not all proteins that are nominally included are individually demonstrated essential at the experimental concentrations. The experiment does not synthesize and self-renew the ribosome ensemble, tRNAs or the full source of energetic and material substrates.

**Constrained verdict:** second-generation functional *non-ribosomal protein-set* reconstitution = supported; indefinite self-maintaining constructor or independently reproducing cells = not shown.

### A2. Schwarz-Schilling et al. (2025/2026): local functional co-biogenesis of 30 translation factors, not whole-machine replacement

**Original:** Matthaeus Schwarz-Schilling, Ilan Cohen, Aurore Dupin, Noa Avidan, Yoav Barak, Yoshihiro Shimizu, Shirley S. Daube and Roy H. Bar-Ziv, “Autonomous biogenesis of all thirty proteins of the Escherichia coli translation machinery,” *Nature Communications* 17, 1028; first published **30 December 2025**, version of record January 2026; DOI https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-67772-8 ; [publisher main text](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-67772-8). **Depth:** E2 complete accessible original main through Discussion, Methods and reporting; supplemental/source data not independently rerun. **Rights:** the article is **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0**, *not* blanket CC BY. Original paraphrase only; no adapted article material redistributed.

The investigators target **20 aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases (aaRS) plus 10 initiation, elongation, termination/recycling factors**. In bulk complete PURE, adding all corresponding genes did **not** yield a distinguishable reporter boost against dummy DNA because initial machinery saturated output. Individual threshold/depletion assays, grouped factor tests and a two-reaction EF-Tu functionality assay support functional nascent factors. For simultaneous expression, they concentrated DNA templates in glass-bound **DNA brushes**, which localize genes, transcripts, protein machinery and products near a large external reaction reservoir. TIRF imaging indicated reporter output near translation-gene brushes compared with dummy-gene brush controls.

**Failures and rescue matter:** the first **all-30** mixture at the empirically determined threshold levels did *not* produce the required reporter signal. Detectable all-30 reporter accumulation required adding **25 nM of each initiation and release factor** to the starting mixture; for improved imaging, most factors were raised from 100-fold to 50-fold dilution while EF-Tu stayed at about twofold dilution of its full concentration. Thus “autonomous biogenesis” means nascent factors can participate under a *prepared, partially supplemented reaction*, not total replacement of every original active factor.

**Measured unit:** the all-30 image comparison reports **six DNA-brush clusters from two slides**; clusters and images are not six independent reproductive lineages. Bulk plate-reader assays generally have at least three independent reaction preparations. Authors state nonrandomized, unblinded experiments, and exclusion of technically defective observations after author discussion. They identified residual factor activity in supposedly deleted preparations, with mass spectrometry implicating protein contamination in ribosome stocks; chaperone mixture was added in connection with ArgRS. A full formulation contains about **10,500 nM** non-ribosomal translation proteins while a single batch can yield about **1,600 nM total protein** (the reduced threshold mixture is about **1,360 nM** initial proteins). These values identify a severe capacity/initial-inventory tradeoff, not a proof of sustained conservation.

**Constrained verdict:** coordinated in situ synthesis and functional contribution of 30 translation factors = supported; complete replacement of ribosomes, tRNAs and the operator-made surface or multiple genetically inherited reproducing constructor generations = not shown.

### A3. Hagino et al. (2025): meaningful 20-cycle regeneration of 20 aaRS, but the other machinery is externally replenished

**Original:** Katsumi Hagino, Keiko Masuda, Yoshihiro Shimizu and Norikazu Ichihashi, “Sustainable regeneration of 20 aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases in a reconstituted system toward self-synthesizing artificial systems,” *Science Advances* 11, eadt6269, published 2–4 April 2025; DOI https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adt6269 ; [original open full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11963985/). **Depth:** E2 original main Introduction, Results, Discussion and Methods reviewed; complete supplement and raw mass-spectrometry files not independently reprocessed. **Rights:** article **CC BY-NC 4.0** according to the open original, not unconditional commercial reuse.

This is an important **positive multicycle counterexample**: after specific sequence, composition, activity and gene-ratio optimizations, 20 aaRS were regenerated for **up to 20 rounds of 2.5-fold serial dilution**. At round 20, isotope-resolved mass-spectrometry ratios of newly synthesized to transferred aaRS were near the expected **1.5** under the 2.5-fold dilution accounting. Authors state the 20-round condition was reproduced twice. Translation reporter activity survived conditions under which start-of-series aaRS were extensively diluted.

**Negative findings and controls:** an earlier **10-aaRS** mixture declined rather than regenerated despite promising individually predicted yield, exposing competition and nonlinear resource allocation. In the successful set, higher DNA concentration worsened performance. Omitting a gene for any of five checked aaRS reduced reporter output below **10% after nine rounds**, restricting the contamination explanation for those five, not proving all other input fractions clean. Reversing most optimizations reduced activity; reversing zinc addition was a notable exception.

**Critical external replacement boundary:** each serial-transfer round receives fresh operator-prepared PURE **lacking the 20 target aaRS**, but retaining the other active machinery and necessary substrates, and receives **fresh supplied DNA templates**. Those externally supplied ingredients are not regenerated autonomously. The system shows genuinely sustained **partial constructor-factor regeneration**, not replacement of the entire protein-synthesis constructor, hereditary genomic closure, or compartment birth. The paper notes earlier DNA-coupled partial regeneration (four generations) lost translation functionality—another counterexample to equating copying with stable constructor activity.

**Constrained verdict:** multicycle maintenance of the selected 20-aaRS subset = supported; all-component resource-accounted renewal without added active machinery or templates = not established.

## B. Causal evidence and dependency ledger

| Question / unit of observation | Mottaghi 2026 | Schwarz-Schilling 2025 | Hagino 2025 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Target regenerated | 36 non-ribosomal PURE proteins | 30 translation factors/enzymes | 20 aaRS subset |
| Functional readout | GFP activity after separate purification/reassembly | GFP-HA local output, depletion/group and EF-Tu tests | Luciferase over serial dilution, isotope-resolved aaRS at round 20 |
| Time/dependency axis | Two-stage input batch to functional reconstructed reaction | One prepared DNA-brush spatial reaction, no lineage | 20 operator-controlled dilution rounds; fresh non-aaRS PURE and templates each round |
| Positive result | Newly produced protein set supports second functional reaction | Newly expressed factors participate in protein synthesis | 20-aaRS subset survives repeated replacement challenges |
| Strongest negative | Insufficient throughput by ~10x for nondegenerative all-36 turnover; ribosome-fraction contaminants | Initial all-30 threshold condition failed, rescued by extra starting factors; saturation masks activity | Initial 10-aaRS multicycle mixture failed; many conditions reduce retention |
| Ribosomes/tRNA rebuilt? | No | No | No |
| DNA templates generated/transmitted by system? | No | No | No; replenished |
| Causal operator independence? | Purification/reassembly externally required | Prepared DNA brush, replenished external resource bath | Regular prepared transfer and replenishment required |
| What is **not** proved | Continuous constructor/cell renewal | Self-contained all-protein factory | Unassisted whole-constructor reproduction |

**Boundary accounting:** ordinary environmental food/energy supply is not the same as supplying **already functional missing machinery** or newly fabricated genetic templates. A fair closure judgment must separate (1) material/energy inputs, (2) preassembled catalysts/ribosomes, (3) new genetic templates and RNA, (4) human-controlled concentration, purification and serial-transfer operations, (5) output that remains functional under the actual downstream conditions, and (6) disposal/waste/loss. Neither a fluorescent readout nor a concentration mass balance by itself proves that a whole constructor can pay its complete formation, release, replacement and repair costs.

**Intervention-withdrawal interpretation:** withdrawing *all* environmental energy would extinguish ordinary biology too and is not a proper null. The scientifically discriminating comparison would retain matched basic feed and permissible habitat while removing **researcher-specific rescue, preassembled essential machinery and stepwise product purification/assembly**. None of the three reviewed papers reports this whole-system comparison through independent descendant cycles.

## C. Source independence, methodology and counterhypotheses

- The EPFL/Maerkl program (A1), Weizmann/Bar-Ziv program (A2), and Tokyo/Ichihashi–RIKEN program (A3) involve **three research programs, not three independent replications of an identical endpoint**. Yoshihiro Shimizu is a coauthor on both A2 and A3: acknowledge investigator/material-method overlap rather than counting them as entirely unrelated validation. Prior EPFL Ganesh/Maerkl PURE dilution work and Maerkl/Lavickova microfluidics are relevant background from the *same lab* as A1, not separate replication votes.
- **Carryover/contamination alternative:** initially supplied ribosomal protein fractions can hide essential-factor deficits. The A2 depletion mass spectrometry and A1 discussion document this; A3 five targeted gene-omission controls narrow, but do not universally remove, the confound.
- **Reporter vs complete-molecule alternative:** successful GFP or luciferase synthesis establishes some functional translation capacity, not necessarily full essential-machine stoichiometry, quality control, repair and descendant production.
- **Optimization / selected operating point:** reaction composition, template ratios, chaperone addition, DNA brushes, pools and serial transfer schedules are researcher-selected. A robust, independently arising architecture is not inferred from a functioning prepared configuration.
- **Population/replicate unit:** A1 n=2 technical wells, A2 brush clusters on two slides and A3 serial reaction rounds are *not* equivalent to independent biological founders, lineages, laboratories or transfer/generalization cohorts.
- **Contrary evidence preserved:** A3 really sustains 20 rounds; do not claim no multicycle molecular self-regeneration exists. Conversely, A1 “fully reconstituted” means its 36 **non-ribosomal protein fraction**, not the entirety of molecular translation infrastructure; A2 “autonomous” describes localized protein expression, not autonomous organismhood.
- **Replication and rights:** no independent reproduction, raw-data recomputation, live laboratory experiment or E3 grade was performed here. Publisher license differences (A1 CC BY; A2 CC BY-NC-ND; A3 CC BY-NC) are preserved. No figures, raw observations, protected supplements, proprietary recipes or paper passages were copied.

## D. Six falsifiable **future-only** hypotheses (not executed designs)

**P42-H1 — Carryover audit.** Some reported reconstitution efficiency is materially driven by initial ribosomal-factor contamination or inherited protein activity. **Falsifier:** verified clean input-protein provenance and specific omission/functional rescue controls reproduce the same full output without hidden residual factors.

**P42-H2 — Replacement versus augmentation.** Nascent factors can raise a reporter signal in a seeded system without fully displacing essential starting factor activity. **Falsifier:** quantitative identity-specific mass/function tracing establishes complete active-factor replacement with no performance decline across later use.

**P42-H3 — Throughput threshold.** The post-purification yield of all required functional components, not just their expression, is below the material cost of continuing whole-constructor renewal. **Falsifier:** a complete material/energy/function ledger demonstrates net enough usable active parts for sustained cycles after replacement and losses.

**P42-H4 — Programmed rescue dependence.** The 20-cycle aaRS result would cease to support full translation if external machinery/template replacement were removed while ordinary small-molecule feed remained. **Falsifier:** a lineage retains the same functional output while regenerating previously supplied indispensable machinery and heritable templates from allowable primitive feed without experimenter-mediated reassembly.

**P42-H5 — Spatial localization vs true closure.** High-density DNA-brush colocalization produces local reporter rescue without a heritable, independently propagated spatial organization. **Falsifier:** independently tracked daughters reconstruct their own localized gene/machinery arrangement and maintain component-specific functional advantages over controls.

**P42-H6 — Cross-program transfer and robustness.** Measured regenerative success may be concentrated in carefully tuned reaction/surface compositions and fail under other equally feasible operating conditions. **Falsifier:** independently replicated, prespecified source-neutral substrate comparisons maintain functional throughput with failure-inclusive denominators and consistent support accounting.

These are *research questions* for separately authorized, preregistered future work. They are **not executable instructions** and no experiment, organism, model, simulation, persistent world, data collector or cost-bearing process is authorized by this pass.

## E. Decision and next evidence gap

**Decision:** retain three achievements distinctly: (i) a functional second-generation 36-nonribosomal-protein PURE reconstitution after external purification, (ii) locally coexpressed and functionally contributing 30 translation proteins in prepared DNA brushes, and (iii) sustained **20-cycle partial regeneration** of 20 aaRS with other essential machinery and gene templates replenished. None independently establishes the full conjunction of *complete active machinery replacement + ongoing resource-balanced internal production + heritable autonomous descendant reconstruction*. These are **evidence gaps, not impossibility results**.

**Next distinct literature-only pass, if worthwhile:** search for a genuinely independent full-primary demonstration coupling ribosome/tRNA production, DNA/RNA inheritance, function of newly made proteins and repeated self-produced construction after preassembled-machinery withdrawal. Prefer explicit failure and negative controls.
