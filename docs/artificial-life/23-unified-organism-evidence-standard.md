# Unified empirical standard: self-maintenance, lineage, functional heredity and open-endedness

**ALife research protocol v0.1 | 2026-10-08 | DOCUMENTATION ONLY.** This is a **cross-substrate measurement design**, not an approved or executed artificial organism experiment. The [JSON criteria register](../../data/alife/operational-tests.json) is the machine-readable complement.

## Central principle — four claims must never collapse into one
**(1) Repetition** is not **(2) causally branching replication**; branching replication is not **(3) inherited adaptive novelty**; and none alone proves **(4) autopoietic self-maintenance** or **life**. These properties can be studied on *independent axes*, not a single linear "aliveness scale".

Our minimum evidence structure deliberately retains **10 distinct claim tests**, AL-C01 … AL-C10. Report pass/inconclusive/fail **per criterion and context**, not a green life badge.

## A. Research axes and source expectations
| Axis | Operational question | Distinguishing readout | Most important false positive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Causal replication | Do two daughters branch **independently** from one parent? | Necessary-cause cell/cluster DAG and separated daughter-lineage paths | Glider/oscillator repeating shape |
| Multi-generation | Do independent daughters themselves reproduce? | Verified branching depth and viable descendant counts across worlds | Founder emits offspring that cannot reproduce |
| Functional heredity | Are *new abilities* transmitted to descendants? | Parent-linked new function under held-out ecological probes | Shape/copy similarity or shared-environment effect |
| Organizational maintenance | Does internal organization replace/repair its own prerequisites? | Targeted repair-loop ablation and constituent turnover | Immutable state or engine-scheduled healing |
| Resource coupling | Is continued organization dependent on actual constrained resources? | Balanced ledger and causal resource denial/return | Fictional energy score with unlimited reservoir |
| Agency | Do internal processes regulate toward viability under unfamiliar conditions? | Cue-/process-ablated survival benefit | Human-scripted task reward or hardcoded reaction |
| Open-ended hallmarks | Does functional innovation persist over finite held-out windows? | New retained affordances, neutral/shadow comparisons, plateau indicators | External novelty archive/image embeddings |
| Transformational change | Can new **effective computation rules** originate and persist? | Inherited interpreter semantics and expanded behavioral capability | Syntax relabeling or external software upgrade |
| Individuality | Can causally connected processes be spatially disjoint? | Intervention-supported causal organization and competing trackers | Only connected pixel cluster identity |

Some dimensions may be inapplicable to a substrate; record **not applicable**, not zero or automatic failure.

## B. Causal genealogy procedure (future design)
1. Freeze local update physics / automata rule and declare whether activation events can emerge without preceding active inputs.
2. Define causal necessity vs correlation. In a deterministic binary CA, identify which prior inputs are necessary for a cell activation; in nonbinary/stochastic worlds choose a scientifically justified causal model and uncertainty bounds.
3. Construct time-indexed event/cluster causality, handling redundant inputs, merging, branching, and *negative/absence* conditions where relevant.
4. Declare phenotype identity separately from causal-lineage identity. Perfect same-shape copies are not proof of heritable **different** capabilities.
5. Require independently branching offspring: no ancestor/descendant dependence *between sibling offspring*.
6. Measure **daughter fertility** and further branching depth, not only the founder's emissions.
7. Track inherited *functional variation* across generations using previously sealed environmental challenges and controls where parent links are randomized.
8. Distinguish a replicator evolving because of ecology from a fixed rule generated in an **external search**.

**Outlier application:** [Yang](20-outlier-original-2025-full-review.md) established periodic multi-scale structures; [Hintze & Bohm](21-outlier-causal-selfhood-2026.md) provide a causal DAG and **15-generation** c2 lineage in one selected 1024×1024 setup. **Not yet shown:** stable inheritance of beneficial *new functions*.

## C. Maintenance, agency and evolution must be tested separately
Susan Stepney's [2025 engineering overview](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2024.0298) proposes autopoiesis, agency and open-ended adaptation as high-level requirements. We translate that into **testable candidate assays** but do not presume a single framework resolves disputed definitions of life.

**Maintenance intervention:** damage a specific internal repair process, keeping world physics/resource inflow identical; compare to damage of similar magnitude elsewhere, passive attractors and engine-protected patterns. A repair effect should be causally specific and reproducible across novel damage locations.

**Agency intervention:** replace time-correlated cues with shuffled/counterfactual signals without changing underlying resource dynamics. A viability benefit from action that depends on prediction is stronger than "it moves toward a resource", especially if resource attraction is coded directly.

**Evolvability intervention:** lock an external novelty evaluator at zero; compare systems with the same resources and compute. Require *inherited novel function* rather than re-optimizing a known benchmark. Track extinction, collapse, rare catastrophic failure as carefully as successes.

## E. Independent standards of reporting
Every future test needs:
- **Source code, paper DOI and version** for reference implementations, with corresponding licenses assessed.
- Experiment/run manifests including rule/physics seed, allowed environmental feedback and resource accounting.
- Individual-world replication as statistical unit. Never treat millions of cells or frames from one run as independent repetitions.
- Parent/offspring causality graph with explicit sibling independence and collision/split merge ambiguity.
- Blind functional challenge generation, with task/reward **not fed back** to evolving organism.
- Matched nulls (inert oscillators, connected-only tracker, external copy API, neutral shadow process, random interpreter scramble, resource-free world, novelty optimizer on/off).
- Predeclared confidence intervals, sensitivity to world size and initial density, and an **abort** criterion for invalid provenance or statistics.
- Independent raw metric recomputation from logged observations when access/authorization permits.
- Conclusions always specifying substrate, finite horizon, task/metric and what cannot be inferred.

## F. Priority future comparisons
**One at a time; no deployment or implementation authorized here:**

1. **AL09 proposed causal lineage validation:** compare connected-pixel tracking against causal graph tracking on fixed archived data (where rights allow), then measure false positive/negative rates on glider, oscillator and distributed replicator patterns. Analysis of existing data should be separately scoped if it would require actually running simulation code.
2. **AL10 proposed mutable-language viability:** compare fixed/interpreter-mutable/externally-upgraded languages at identical compute and resource budget; blind assay previously unavailable functional transitions and cross-generational transmission.
3. **AL11 organizational closure/agency assessment:** apply autopoiesis repair, resource causality and cue-shuffle/viability tests to competing chemistry and instruction-substrates, independent of an image-based novelty score.

**Status:** these are future research proposals, not extra already executed experiments. They do **not** alter the previously approved AL01–AL08 designs.

## G. What this framework cannot establish
No finite collection of metrics proves consciousness, subjective experience, a universally accepted definition of life, or *infinite* innovation. Some theoretical positions deny digital organization constitutes biological life even when functional analogs are observed. We report empirical properties plus interpretation, never present a universal ranking.
