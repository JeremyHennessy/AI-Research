# After Outlier: the next scientific questions for digital life

**Research roadmap updated 2026-10-08.** Scientific design only; no digital organism, world, evolutionary program or source-analysis runner was authorized or built.

## What has genuinely changed after this pass?

- **Original Outlier** demonstrated two-scale repeating structures arising under a GP-selected binary CA rule. [Full-paper review](20-outlier-original-2025-full-review.md).
- **Hintze & Bohm (2026)** independently analyzed the same system and tracked branching causal lineages—including **15 generations** from `c2`. [Complete review](21-outlier-causal-selfhood-2026.md).
- **Stepney 2025/2026** articulates autopoiesis, agency, open-ended adaptation as an *engineering proposal* requiring separate operationalization. [Evidence level E1 for the conceptual sources examined here](22-engineering-life-and-transformational-novelty.md).
- **Adams et al. ALIFE 2026** offers a late-breaking research lead on mutable computational language/interpreter rules, **not** a proven route to transformationally growing intelligence.
- **Unified independent tests** now separate causal replication, multigeneration heredity, endogenous maintenance, resource regulation, agency, and functional novelty. [Research standard](23-unified-organism-evidence-standard.md).

**Big scientific shift:** we now have credible **published evidence of causally identified digital replication** under one unusually productive rule. The hard open questions concern *new inherited functions*, *repairable organization* and *innovation not driven by external scores*.

## Prioritized literature and evidence tasks (no executable simulation)

### Priority A — separate replication from evolution
1. Compare the **original Yang** method with the later **Hintze/Bohm** necessary-cause definition, identifying where exact patterns, repeated causal processes, biological heredity and branch independence differ.
2. Validate from published material which numerical results are from **one selected seed** and which are across independent random initial conditions. Record 1024-square periodic-boundary effects separately from 75,000-tick effectively unbounded analysis.
3. Identify any **later critiques or independent reproductions** of the 2026 causal tracing approach. In particular, analyze sensitivity to exclusion of dead-cell negative causes and exact-copy-only lineage identity.
4. Map conceptual lineage trackers to alternative substrates (Tierra/Avida, Flow-Lenia, artificial chemistries) and state why cell-level necessary causes are not directly interchangeable with stochastic reactions or neural fields.
5. Check the Zenodo [published analysis/data archive](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17904018) metadata/rights, and author repo version; no dataset or code needs execution for this research phase.

**Exit:** a falsifiable scientific argument specifying what Outlier establishes, what it does *not*, and the smallest published-data audit that could reveal mistaken ancestry.

### Priority B — confront organizational-closure and agency definitions
1. Read the **complete** 2025 [Stepney overview](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2024.0298), including all sections and cited counterarguments; elevate to E2 **only after** full paper sections and caveats are reviewed.
2. Obtain legitimate access to ALIFE 2026 three-page summary and eight-page *World Views* paper; check that summaries do not imply empirical tests.
3. Compare her triad to **autopoiesis** (Varela, Maturana, Uribe), **organizational closure**, **Darwinian population** criteria and **process-based individuality**, without forcing any as universal law.
4. Map each requirement to a counterfactual knockout and a minimal control, especially passive attractors vs actively sustained self-production.
5. Separate **organism-level properties** (maintenance/regulation), **population-level dynamics** (evolution/adaptation) and **world/substrate-level affordances** (fixed vs mutable rules).

**Exit:** terms have operational measures; researchers can distinguish a positive from a counterexample *before observing results*.

### Priority C — determine whether computational primitives may themselves evolve
1. Read the full published 2016 [Stringmol chemistry design](https://doi.org/10.1162/ARTL_a_00180) and 2020 [innovation/variation/emergence](https://doi.org/10.1162/isal_a_00265) papers, including their differing model/meta-model levels.
2. Validate exactly what the 2026 **Physis GPU port** was claimed to reproduce, which interpreter rules are mutable, and whether changes arise from internal organization versus an external optimizer.
3. Distinguish a new **program syntax**, an *additional computation pathway*, a changed **instruction semantics**, and a changed *physical/chemical substrate*.
4. Review the MetaChem formalization and see which invariants must be maintained if an interpreter evolves: decoding, resource/budget, executable semantics, and causal provenance.
5. Specify one neutral comparator per original claim—fixed interpreter, random corruption, externally upgraded instruction set, and no hereditary semantics—and a blind novel-function assay.

**Exit:** a model-independent falsification design for **heritable functional new primitives**, not an optimistic story about self-modifying code.

## What would constitute genuine progress?
For each hypothesis, seek evidence of *all the properties it actually claims*:
- **Observed:** distinct new capacity plus lineage/causal dependence; not just image novelty.
- **Inherited:** at least grandchildren retain causally traceable altered function in hidden environments.
- **Self-maintained:** active constituent replacement depends on internal process and constrained resource throughput.
- **Independent of external objective:** capability persists when novelty/appearance evaluator is no longer shaping selection.
- **Robust:** positive outcome across independent worlds, alternative environmental conditions, and methodologically distinct observers.

Failing any property does not invalidate the value of the other findings. It narrows the scientific claim.

## Sensible stop rules
- No credible direct source or known contradictory evidence → leave E0/E1, do **not** present as E2.
- Full paper has no necessary mechanism or independently measured functional outcome → record **uncertainty**, do not infer design details from a headline.
- Causal tracing overcounts repeated patterns, or its controls reject no false positives → re-evaluate lineage definitions before any proposed experiment.
- A new instruction set is actually externally injected → relabel as human-designed environment variation.
- An ALife proposal requires running autonomous worlds or research code → **separate explicit approval required**; the present repository remains literature/design only.
- If computational resources are unknown, do not assert a runtime budget or promise an innovation timeline.
