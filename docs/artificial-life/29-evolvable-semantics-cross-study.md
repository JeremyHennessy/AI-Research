# Changeable computational meaning — a critical research comparison (2003 → 2026)

**Research synthesis updated 2026-10-08.** The cited full-paper E2 audits and primary source material support a narrow, important result: digital systems can undergo **heritable changes to interpreters and genome expression** under fixed substrate rules. This is neither proof of self-produced physical organization nor indefinite new intelligence. No model, chemistry, simulator or organism was run in AI-Research.

## A. Four different places where "rules" could change

| Level | What changes | What remains fixed | Relevant primary evidence | Not yet established |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **L0 — program sequences** | Genes/opcode order/behavior | Runtime instruction meaning, scheduler, rules of reproduction | [Avida](https://doi.org/10.1162/106454604773563612), [Stringmol 2016](26-stringmol-2016-full-review.md) | A broader range of possible operations |
| **L1 — intermediate instruction definitions** | Genome-encoded instruction set, registers/stacks/queues, decoded behavioral meaning | Universal processor and primitive operation set | [Physis 2003](28-physis-2003-full-review.md) | Evolution of underlying physics; demonstrated superior cognition |
| **L2 — interpreter encoded and maintained by its own genome** | Mutating expressor, copier, genome, interactions; the semantic interpretation of genotype | Stringmol base opcodes, collision, stochastic decay and resource/scheduling | [Stringmol UCA 2017](27-semantic-closure-2017-full-review.md) | Metabolizing closed autonomy, unlimited complexity |
| **L3 — meta-model / substrate operations** | Types of types, entirely new semantic primitives, allowed ecological interaction kinds | Whatever meta-physics remains in the host implementation | [Stepney 2025](25-stepney-2025-complete-review.md), [2026 Physis abstract](22-engineering-life-and-transformational-novelty.md) | Reproducibly realized open-ended transformation |

A given system can be very sophisticated at one level but **not advance the next**. In particular, a powerful fixed interpreter permitting arbitrarily long programs may be **computationally universal** yet *not* exhibit intrinsically evolving **semantics** or organizational self-maintenance.

## C. The core trade-off: expressiveness × viability × ecological usefulness
These are **different** criteria:
1. **Expressiveness:** how many distinct computations can be represented through inherited constructs.
2. **Mutational accessibility:** proportion of possible local inherited changes leading to **still viable** organizations.
3. **Resource economy:** CPU/time/memory/energy cost of maintaining the interpreter/copying apparatus.
4. **Compositional closure:** whether critical elements continue to make each other after mutations.
5. **Novel functional affordances:** whether inherited changes grant objectively measurable resource use, repair, sensing, symbiosis, or coordination absent from ancestor.

An important original hypothesis is that **a moderately adaptable expression system with explicit error buffering** may outperform maximally unconstrained semantic mutation on retained *functional* innovation at fixed resource budget. Too little mutability can freeze innovation; too much can cause the Stringmol 2017 "bureaucratic death" cascade.

### Two independent kinds of fidelity
- **Copy fidelity:** how accurately hereditary information is duplicated.
- **Interpretation/expressor fidelity:** whether copying/constructing the interpreter yields a viable and consistently executed semantic mapping.

These may evolve together or oppose one another. A mutation that changes gene "meaning" might be beneficial only if the copier and expressor continue to reproduce one another. The 2017 paper makes this distinction particularly concrete.

## D. Proposed cross-substrate study (research-only, NOT implemented)
**Central question:** does inherited interpreter change increase the rate of viable **new ecological functions** rather than simply the number of syntactic novelties?

**Primary designs for separate future authorization:**
- **Fixed-language null:** the same virtual physics/resources/mutation budget but interpreter frozen.
- **Heritable semantic change:** interpreter semantics can change through offspring genomes.
- **External upgrade control:** externally inject new opcodes/functions on a matched timetable (tests whether emergence requires internal causation).
- **Blind random mutation:** random semantic changes at equal operator/edit cost.
- **Expression-stability variants:** independent control of genome-copy fidelity and expressor error, including feedback dropout and boundary-resource tests.

**Independent outcomes:**
- Previously unseen ecological affordance first appears and is validated by external test, not fed back to organism.
- The function persists in daughter and granddaughter lineages through their own reproduction.
- Internal interpreter/meaning change is **causally necessary** for new behavior.
- Organization remains viable and maintains an independently specified internal process under resource depletion/perturbation.
- Event rates and failure cases across many independent worlds, with cost-normalized compute/time and no designer rescue.

**Falsifier:** semantic novelty rises while functional behavior, offspring inheritance or viable self-maintenance remains at fixed-language baseline or becomes worse.

## E. Distinguish separate theoretical questions
1. **Can a program change meaning?** Yes, bounded cases are documented in Physis and Stringmol experiments.
2. **Can such changes be heritable and viable?** The Stringmol UCA report documents some such transitions, under seeded and fixed chemistry.
3. **Can these changes generate ongoing new useful ecological functions?** Not established by those studies.
4. **Can a system produce/repair its own organizational boundary under constrained resources?** Separate unsolved research question.
5. **Would any of this establish a living digital organism or consciousness?** Not by itself; definitions and ontological inference remain disputed.

## F. Remaining reading, source and engineering gaps
- **2020 Stringmol conference:** [full eight-page paper reviewed E2](30-stringmol-2020-novelty-full-review.md). Its type-2 hypercycle-network meta-model is EXTRINSIC; no new independent simulation was reported in that publication.
- **2026 Physis 3-page ALIFE late abstract:** project currently uses author abstract; full booklet and implementation comparison have not been examined end to end, source version/rights unclear.
- **Original Physis 2003 vs contemporary Python port:** the root README documents a port and **no root LICENSE**, but the source's relation to the 2026 GPU port is not proven. No code run.
- **2017 UCA replication/independent audits:** author reports 500 runs/39 takeovers, raw trajectories not reanalyzed, causal lineage and external functional capabilities unverified.
- **Stepney's assumption:** "virtual life is possible" is a premise she states explicitly for the review, not a universal scientific result.

**Scientific decision:** do not automatically combine all four architectures into one design. Pursue *first* the cheap, falsifying comparisons showing whether mutable semantics produces **function and viable heredity**, and whether more expressive operations can avoid interpreter-collapse selection loops.
