# Why evolved computation fails: a cross-source failure-mechanism atlas

**Pass 6 | 2026-10-08 | Original technical synthesis from primary published work.** Companion structured records: [data/alife/failure-modes.jsonl](../../data/alife/failure-modes.jsonl). This is **not an experiment** and does not imply any independent replication. Use [scientific evidence standards](../05-evidence-standards.md).

## Scientific framing

**Three gates that cannot be substituted for one another**:
1. **Retention / viability:** does a structure reproduce/continue without external rescue?
2. **Heritable change:** does a lineage pass altered **interpretation and function**, not merely appearance or copy errors?
3. **Adaptive novelty:** is the newly available behavior useful under previously unseen ecological constraints after removing researcher-directed objectives?

A system may pass one while failing others. A global "fitness" or "novelty" score hides which one failed. In particular, Stringmol 2020 makes **observer-relative novelty classification** explicit: an externally updated *scientific meta-model* is not the same as an internally modified *engineering system*. [2020 original review](30-stringmol-2020-novelty-full-review.md).

## Information fidelity and selection: a mechanistic hypothesis

For a heritable mutating interpreter, two failure channels matter:

- **Copy errors** change encoded organism programs/instructions when duplicating the genome.
- **Expression/interpretation errors** change the actual machines built from an identical genome.

The 2017 UCA makes the latter concrete. A mutation affecting an expressor can create further incompatible expressors; additional diversity then undermines construction of needed copier/expressor components. This suggests a **viability region** in a conceptual plane:

`survival_probability = f(copy_fidelity, expression_fidelity, resources, partner_availability, world_physics)`.

We do **not** assert this function is monotonic or universal. Better fidelity could also reduce exploratory access to new viable phenotypes. We propose a *Pareto frontier*:

- survival/viable descendant production,
- independently verified novel *function*,
- compute and resource consumption,
- rate of extinct populations.

High mutation could produce novelty but no stable descendants; low mutation could preserve a static ecology without functional progress.

**Falsifier:** there is no reproducible tradeoff after matching resource/time budgets and initial populations, or fixed-expression controls achieve the same rate of inherited novel function.

## Separation between biological metaphors and observations

| Popular claim | Observed evidence required instead |
|---|---|
| "It repaired itself" | Internally causal replacement after targeted damage versus engine-rescue/attractor control |
| "It is immortal" | Reproducing descendants with viable functions and bounded resource consumption, not a surviving loop |
| "It evolved intelligence" | Unrewarded, hidden-condition improvement in sensory/predictive behavior transmitted across generations |
| "Its language evolved" | A **different inherited mapping** from genome to executed operations, with causal necessity and useful retained effect |
| "The software invented new physics" | Explicit distinction between mutable interpreter level and the host system's fixed ultimate transition primitives |
| "It has open-endedness" | Independent functional/organizational innovations at finite preregistered horizons, with neutral-activity controls and no claim of mathematical infinity |

## Highest-information cross-paper contradictions

1. **Physis 2003:** inherited virtual-processor changes are *possible* but structural takeover is limited. **Stringmol 2017:** some viable semantic takeovers are *possible*, but mismatch drives collapse. This is **compatible** evidence of a high-fidelity/mutability constraint, not proof that one system is superior.
2. **Stringmol 2020:** "type-2 emergence" can be a scientist's *new meta-model*, whereas **2026 Physis abstract** proposes changing code *and language*. These require **different proofs**: observer classification versus internally altered executable meaning.
3. **Stringmol 2021:** ecological parasitism can select slower but more defensive programs, challenging simplistic "fastest copying equals highest adaptive complexity" claims. The observed *defensive* function should still be assessed under independent changed-parasite and no-parasite controls.
4. **Flow-Lenia 2025 and PBT-NCA 2026:** measured "novelty" depends on mass scaling or designed visual scores, so high numbers cannot be compared to semantic-closure or functional heredity evidence.

## What this map does *not* establish

The compiled papers use different physics, species definitions, initial seeded programs, stochastic selection, resource budgets and evaluation outcomes. They are **not** a joint randomized study; do not compute pooled success odds or rank which system is most "alive." Full original studies and negative experimental traces have not been executed by AI-Research.
