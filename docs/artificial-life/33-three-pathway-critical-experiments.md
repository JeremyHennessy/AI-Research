# Three fundamentally different mechanisms for inherited novelty — competing preregistration designs

**Proposed research (2026-10-08). This is a documented experimental plan only; no organism, artificial chemistry, world, interpreter or simulation may be implemented without separate explicit authorization.** Use [ten independent ALife criteria](23-unified-organism-evidence-standard.md) and [failure-map ledger](../../data/alife/failure-modes.jsonl) to avoid substituting complexity for organismhood.

## The decision question

**Which kind of computational substrate offers the clearest, lowest-cost evidence that a *new adaptive capability* can be produced, transmitted, and sustained by the system's own internal organization rather than an external reward/novelty metric?**

Do not presuppose an LLM, neural network or language comprehension. Interpret "language" below as an **instruction/semantic mapping**, not a chatbot language.

### Path A — Heritable virtual processor / instruction definitions (Physis family)

**Primitive:** a virtual computing substrate with fixed low-level operations and a heredity-encoded *intermediate* processor description (register/stack/queue structure, interpreted macroinstructions).

**Origins:** hand-seeded replicators in historical Physis; future null contrasts random programs separately, not as proof of de novo origin.

**Self-preservation:** CPU/time, program memory, correct decoding/copy operations and execution validity; organism *organizational* boundary is often engine-provided.

**Lifetime adaptation:** historical task reward changes processor/fitness dynamics, but within-life learning is not guaranteed by inherited instruction definitions.

**Reproduction/heredity:** offspring copy genome encoding processor structure and instruction semantics. **Novel capacity route:** new macroinstruction definitions and recomposed instruction meanings.

**Critical false signal:** designer awards CPU bonuses for arithmetic tasks—this is **human-selected cognition**, not spontaneous intelligence.

**Source support:** [Physis 2003 complete review](28-physis-2003-full-review.md); 2026 GPU-port abstract [E1 only](31-physis-2026-source-boundaries.md).

**Proposed nulls:** frozen interpreter, hand-upgraded interpreter, random genotype/edit control, no-extra-task-reward ecology. Outcome is **new independently assayed functional capability per viable descendant and unit of compute**, with novel conditions never included in selection.

### Path B — Universal constructor / semantically closed artificial chemistry (Stringmol UCA family)

**Primitive:** molecules are programs that bind, execute on partners, copy and express each other's codes. Genome `G` expresses copier `C` and expressor/interpreter `E`; meaningful heredity depends on their mutually sustaining network.

**Origins:** historical initial UCA hand-engineered with `G,C,E` plus inert payload, not evolved from random molecules.

**Self-maintenance:** replication requires viable co-production of genome and machinery; decay/resource model imposed by a fixed chemistry. An executable loop protected from decay is **not** equivalent to productive self-maintenance.

**Lifetime adaptation:** reactive binding/copying and evolved ecologies can change a molecule's function; no necessary learning-memory system.

**Heredity:** viable changes to expressor may reinterpret a **nominally unchanged genome** in daughter populations. Historic study reports 39/500 takeover runs but many eventually collapse through incompatible expression.

**Novel capability route:** a new function can be expressed through a changed genotype→product mapping. The historical inert payload `HELLOWORLD` cannot be credited as a demonstrated ecological skill.

**Source support:** [Stringmol 2016](26-stringmol-2016-full-review.md), [UCA 2017](27-semantic-closure-2017-full-review.md), [2020 taxonomy](30-stringmol-2020-novelty-full-review.md).

**Proposed nulls:** fixed expressor, genome-copy-only mutation, random expressor perturbations, externally injected compiler upgrade, broken mutual dependence, fixed and varied decay; test **daughter/granddaughter viability and hidden resource capability**.

### Path C — Reaction/compartment-based artificial chemistry (non-program genetic organization)

**Primitive:** molecular species and reaction networks under stoichiometric constraints, stochastic kinetics, finite reservoirs, chemical flow and candidate compartments with endogenous production.

**Origins:** random reaction networks versus deliberately supplied autocatalytic cycles separated; not assumed that a complete living chemistry spontaneously appears.

**Self-maintenance:** internally produced catalysts and potential boundary materials offset decay by resource uptake; conservation and replenishment must be explicit.

**Reproduction/heredity:** compositional network and eventually endogenous compartment division. Distinguish steady-state amplification of intermediates from transmission of **functional organization** after division.

**Novel capability route:** an additional viable reaction pathway converts a formerly inaccessible resource and persists in daughters. No digital instruction-string mutation required.

**Source support:** [Liu–Sumpter 2018 E2](12-chemical-replication-full-review.md), [GARD compositional errors E1](14-negative-results-and-measurement-disagreements.md), [protocell synthesis](01-life-and-organization.md).

**Proposed nulls:** infinite external-feedstock versus finite flux, feedback reaction knockout, inert/no-catalyst network, shuffled parent composition, no boundary production, equal total mass/energy. Outcome is **causally maintained organization with heritable new pathway**, not simple molecule count increase.

### Optional fourth comparator: conservative spatial fields (Flow-Lenia)

Mass-conserving transport and localized parameters support varied spatiotemporal patterns but do not guarantee heritable new function. Use [Flow-Lenia E2](16-flow-lenia-2025-full-review.md) only as a *comparator for spatial organization*, not forced into a genome/interpreter architecture. Track parameters after split, normalize for mass, require functional descendants.

## Comparison matrix — not a ranking of “aliveness”

| Criterion | Physis processor | Stringmol UCA | Reaction-network/protocell |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evolvable level | Intermediate ISA and processor description | Genome-coded expressor/copy machinery; inherited interpretation | Catalytic organization and compartment/network composition |
| Fixed substrate remains? | Universal processor and scheduler | Primitive opcode set, binding/decay physics | Reaction templates, conservation and flow |
| Published partial success | Changed ISA definitions with viable replicators | Viable semantic takeovers in some seeded containers | Network self-amplification under specified reservoir |
| Most visible published failure | Strong structural conservation and task reward leakage | Interpreter incompatibility, parasitic and loop collapse | External reservoir and weakly specified heritable compartments |
| Strongest accessible low-compute test | Interpreter mutation and blind function tests (future) | Copy fidelity × expression fidelity under viable descendants (future) | Reaction-feedback knockout and finite-resource balance (future) |
| Critical organism claim still missing | Self-produced maintenance / human-free cognition | Long-term external-resource-coupled self-maintenance and new independent functions | Genotype-like faithful inherited novelty and organismal identity |
| Paper-evidence confidence | E2 historical methods, no E3 | E2 historical methods, no E3 | E2 reaction model, no E3 |

**No cross-substrate accuracy benchmark exists in our repo.** Apparent superiority is currently undecidable; the systems implement different primitive operations and selection economies.

## Shared preregistration template for future separate project

Each future study gets its **own** predeclared:
- **Research question**, mechanism and testable falsifier;
- Exact prior public source, version and license, plus method deviations from original;
- Three arms: intervention of interest, strongest within-substrate baseline, mechanism-specific null;
- Environment/rules, seeded vs unseeded origin, resource ledger, mutation and decay, expected viable replication periods;
- Primary endpoint: **first previously absent function**, independent hidden-condition validity, inherited viability to ≥grandchildren where meaningful;
- Alternative endpoint for non-genetic chemistry: faithful transmitted *reaction organization* across independently identified daughter compartments;
- Copies vs interpretation-error rates, no-product loops, parasites, population collapse, compute/memory energy costs;
- Multiple independent worlds as statistical units with initial-condition sensitivity, no cherry-picked run;
- **Independent grader** not accessible to evolutionary selection/visual novelty optimizer;
- Predefined maximum trial cost / stopping rule, preserving null results and error artifacts;
- No "new living organism" conclusion even if one test succeeds; report each independent property.

**Pre-registration must be completed before running anything.** This document does not authorize implementation; the original AL09–11 future experiment registry remains `not_authorized`.

## Outcome gates: which result would change our judgment?

| Observation in a future authorized study | Update to scientific conclusion |
|---|---|
| Only opcode syntax changes | No evidence of a new capability, just genotypic variation |
| Interpreter semantics change and daughter/granddaughter remain viable | Stronger evidence of sustainable hereditary **interpretation changes** |
| Novel resource/sensing function passes a hidden test and persists in descendants | Credible **heritable functional innovation** under specified ecology |
| Same effect obtained with externally inserted interpreter upgrades | Benefit may derive from new primitives, **not endogenous origin** |
| Internalized function disappears without expert-defined novelty/task reward | Novelty attributed primarily to external objective |
| Interpreter change gains function but self-production feedback knockout has no effect | Heritable adaptive computing, **not** self-maintaining organization under our criterion |
| Results fail with independent seed, rule or conserved-resource variation | Fragile, context-limited observation; avoid universal generalization |

## Recommended near-term order **within AI-Research**
1. Keep the three substrate architectures as **independent competing theories** rather than merge them into a single digital-life blueprint.
2. Deeply audit remaining negative results (parasite-driven evolutionary complexity and collapse; 2026 Physis full booklet once accessible).
3. Develop paper-backed model selection criteria using the ten operational axes; choose only a single **highest-value falsifier** to propose for future separate authorization.
4. If the user later approves implementation, start with the cheapest control that could **disprove** the core hypothesis, not a flashy organism simulation.

**Long-term research objective:** find evidence for autonomously maintained, heritably evolving functional organization. The mere ability to reprogram an interpreter is an important component, but is **not** by itself life, intelligence or open-endedness.
