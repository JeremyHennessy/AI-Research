## Confirmed scientific findings: scoped, not reproduced in AI-Research
- **2020 Stringmol conceptual taxonomy:** distinguish variation within one model; innovation requiring new model concepts; emergence requiring new meta-model concept, and **intrinsic vs extrinsic** novelty. A changed observer category is not a new internally available instruction or ecological function.
- **2003 Physis:** intermediate virtual processor/instruction interpretations can be heritably modified inside a still-fixed universal processor; observed structural takeover was limited.
- **2016 Stringmol:** stochastic molecular binding can change parasitoid resistance, while special decay policies select unproductive bound loops and overly costly sequences.
- **2017 Stringmol UCA:** some viable shifts in genome interpretation occur, but incompatible expressor variation can destabilize the organization ("bureaucratic death").
- **2021 spatial Stringmol E1:** primary published summary of 20 experiments, 12 extinct and eight still running at 2m ticks; external ecological pressure can select defenses; complete methods not audited here.
- **2026 Physis E1:** abstract-only CPU→GPU port and "new results" lab statement; do not invent numerical benchmarks from unavailable booklet.
