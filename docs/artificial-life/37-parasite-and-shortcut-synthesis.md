# When parasite pressure creates capability—and when the simulator supplies it

**Cross-paper research synthesis, 2026-10-08.** No simulation, organism, LLM or evolving-world code was executed. This is an **original scientific comparison**, not a pooled statistical analysis or independent reproduction.

## Two distinct claims must be kept separate
The [2021 Stringmol full-paper review](35-stringmol-spatial-parasitism-2021-full-review.md) describes **author-observed adaptive coding strategies**: parasite pressure in spatially local replication selects more sophisticated and *slower* replication algorithms, including self-scan, changed binding, execution redirection, and indirect macromutations. **12 of 20 worlds were extinct** at the finite 2-million-step horizon.

The [2016 Banzhaf complete theory](36-banzhaf-2016-open-ended-novelty-full-review.md) shows why such within-world developments should not be confused with **emergence of the physical substrate, personal identity, replicator API, or novelty metric**. The grid, VM opcodes, hand-coded ancestor, mutation, decay, and local output constraints are **designed generative physics**. Yet the evolved within-world defensive strategies can still be meaningful evidence of *new behaviors from ecological pressures*.

## The next highest-value distinction: new *niches* versus novel *solutions*
A self-copying algorithm evolving a defense against parasites represents a **new tactic in a fixed niche economy**. A stronger open-endedness result would require:
1. A **previously unavailable interaction/resource opportunity** is created by the evolving population, not scripted at construction time.
2. Novelty is represented in **the participants' operational possibilities**, not just in the scientific observer's taxonomy.
3. A new function causally depends on evolved organization and is **transmitted to distinct daughter and granddaughter lineages**.
4. New ecology affects survival/repair/interaction *without* a researcher-provided target or scoring reward.
5. The result survives changes in seed, grid geometry, spatial placement and constrained resource accounting.

These are original **prospective criteria**, **not** reported findings in the cited papers.
