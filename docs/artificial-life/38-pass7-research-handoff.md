## Scientific findings and limitations
**2021 author-reported causal mechanism:** short parasites exploit existing host copying, while spatial wavefronts can preserve replicators. Survivors evolve self-scanning, slower copying, partner checks and non-complementary binding. Some behavior changes effective mutational opportunities and triggers large copying rearrangements, while all primitive Stringmol opcodes remain **unchanged**. **12/20 worlds extinct** by 2m steps, and no de novo origin from an unseeded soup was tested.

**2016 theoretical distinction:** the simulated origin of *individuality* cannot be claimed if the platform already supplies bounded individual entities; similarly a built-in replication API or externally assigned fitness reward changes what a particular study can demonstrate. Such shortcuts can be legitimate but restrict causal attribution. Variation/innovation/emergence are model-relative descriptions, not automatically independent organism-executable functions. Authors discuss finite hierarchy/resource limits and do not demonstrate a universally open-ended program.

**Not established:** indefinite functional innovation, endogenous metabolism, self-produced physical boundaries, intrinsically evolved new instruction *primitives*, emergent general intelligence, consciousness or any measured cross-substrate winner.
