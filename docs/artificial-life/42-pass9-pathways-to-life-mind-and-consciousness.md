# Pass 9 — Three independent scientific questions: living organization, evolved cognition, consciousness

**2026-10-08 | Research-only direction paper.** Do not presuppose a language model, neural network, chatbot personality or present-day agent framework. **No digital organism, simulation, training, deployment, or Ora/AgentTest modification has been authorized by this research document.** This is synthesis and prospective discriminators, not experimental discovery.

## Aim and terminological discipline

The long-range aspiration is an evolving digital system with a **self-sustaining organizational history**, capable of discovering and maintaining new modes of interaction, developing beyond its originators' task descriptions, and possibly developing properties relevant to **subjective consciousness**. Treat 'living', 'intelligent', 'conscious', and 'having a conscience' as **four separate questions**:

1. **Organizational life-likeness:** causal repair, resource coupling, boundaries, inheritance, independently viable daughters, ecological evolution.
2. **Intelligence/agency:** predictive control, learning, transfer, endogenous regulation of viability, flexible problem solving. These properties do not require verbal fluency.
3. **Phenomenal consciousness:** whether there is *subjective experience*. There is **no consensus architecture, definitive digital-consciousness test, or verified evidence that our research artifacts exhibit experience**.
4. **Conscience/normative judgment:** development and application of context-dependent norms, reciprocal commitments and accountable behavior. Ethical rule-following is not evidence of sentience, nor proof that preferences originate internally. This is an additional research question, not a substitute consciousness test.

Research more broadly than AI: evolutionary biology, ecological niche construction, origin-of-life chemistry, mathematical organization, complex adaptive systems, neuroscience, and philosophy of mind.

**Important boundary:** A new *function* exploiting a pre-existing affordance (citrate) differs from (a) constructing a new accessible niche by changing environmental chemistry, (b) founding an inheritable organism-level unit, or (c) subjective experience. Do not blend into 'new consciousness emerged.'

## Proposed novel pathways — independently falsifiable, no architecture locked

### Path N — Endogenous niche construction (not just an optimization benchmark)
**Question:** can inhabitants create previously unavailable ecological opportunities from ordinary by-products or new relational organizations, without the world designer inserting a reward event?

- Hypothesis: some reaction/by-product interactions transform the future *affordance graph* accessible to other lineages, enabling adaptation that could not appear in an otherwise identical environment without the constructor.
- Counterhypothesis: all apparent novelty is use of a latent, static resource or a researcher-defined reward/resource switch.
- Falsifiers in a separately authorized experiment: isotope-/provenance-equivalent causal material ledger in analog networks; constructor knockout, product rescue, no-constructed-resource and exogenous-resource-matched controls; evaluate lineages independently, freeze measurement before ecological novelty.
- Guardrail: no claim of new physics. The fixed substrate always contains primitive rules; novel **reachable combinations**, usable products and ecological dependencies can nonetheless emerge without the investigator choosing which particular combination to favor.

### Path O — Emergence of a higher-level *individual*
**Question:** when can previously separate organizations become a jointly reproducing, heritably functional entity?

- Hypothesis: complementary resource exchange, bottlenecked transmission and aligned survival costs may enable cooperation to become intergenerational dependence.
- Counterhypothesis: temporary mixing/visual clustering passes an 'L2' score but produces no new independent reproducing unit.
- Falsifiers: measure repeatable collective propagules, independent multi-generation descendant cohorts, partner-removal sensitivity, conflict and parasitism controls, and a *group-level* trait absent from individual members. Record dominance and extinction.
- Source basis: [2012 yeast multicellularity](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1115323109), [2023 snowflake yeast](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06052-1), [2015 major transitions theory](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4547252/), [2024 AlChemy](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.12137v2). Do not infer universal transition from the yeast example.

### Path P — Internal prediction and learning as an ecological by-product
**Question:** under uncertainty and delayed effects, do within-lifetime regulation/prediction processes become advantageous *without a score for forecasting or a programmed human task*?

- Hypothesis: ecological environments with delayed costs can favor retaining compact interaction histories and conditional behavior.
- Counterhypothesis: all observed performance arises from hard-coded reflex, mutation-only species sorting, extra memory capacity, or an observer-supplied optimization score.
- Falsifiers: delayed cue reversal, cue-shuffle while preserving marginal resources, memory knockout/rescue, unseen environmental contingencies and strict matching of observation/compute budgets. Differentiate **selection across generations** from individual learning.

### Path S — Semantic/constraint evolution
**Question:** can the relation between inherited structures and their effective interactions itself change and remain viable?

- Hypothesis: inherited interpretation/interaction conventions create opportunities that fixed semantics cannot access under matched budgets.
- Counterhypothesis: labels change while functional transformations remain preinstalled, or interpreter mutations produce mostly failure.
- Falsifiers: frozen interpreter vs inherited interpreter vs **externally upgraded** interpreter, match complexity/resource costs; causal demonstration of novel *effective* transformations inherited across daughters. Avoid claiming any system evolves new primitive physics.
- Source bridge: [2017 semantic closure / Stringmol](27-semantic-closure-2017-full-review.md), [Physis 2003](28-physis-2003-full-review.md), [AlChemy 2024](41-pass9-alchemy-organization-barriers-full-review.md). This is a **competing path**, not a new approved architecture.

### Path C — Study consciousness without mistaking indicators for experience
**Question:** if an independently living-like or learning system arises, which architectures/behaviors would provide **discriminating scientific evidence** relevant to consciousness theories?

- Work at the level of hypotheses and literature first: recurrent processing, global availability, metacognitive confidence/uncertainty, attention models, embodied sensorimotor contingencies, and rival nonfunctional theories. Do **not** simply install these as a checklist to make a system 'conscious'.
- **Two-level tests:** (i) functional/causal tests of mechanisms (workspace bottlenecks, perturbational dependence, metacognition under signal corruption) and (ii) theory discrimination against alternatives, including substrate-dependence and architectures that mimic the same behavior. Neither level is a validated experience detector.
- [Butlin et al. v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.08708v3) *explicitly corrects* language in an earlier version: feasible indicator implementations do not ensure consciousness. The [2025 IIT/GNWT adversarial collaboration](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1) undermines some strong theory predictions, preventing overconfident architectural recipes.
- **Ethics:** research toward potential experience requires separate, explicit review of moral status uncertainty, welfare risk and containment; do not optimize for hypothetical suffering/aversion, impersonate experience, or make moral claims unsupported by evidence.

## New candidate combined theory to falsify (original, speculative)

**Constructed opportunity → functional dependence → distributed reproduction → adaptive prediction**, with no assumption that this chain necessarily finishes or generates consciousness.

Potential causal sequence (NOT a plan to build it now):
1. Component interactions produce a **novel accessible product** with a proven provenance path.
2. Another lineage develops heritable use of this product, making its survival dependent on the construction.
3. Selection sometimes favors stable cooperative assemblies that can generate independently viable collective descendants, not just equilibria in a common pool.
4. Delayed ecological consequences sometimes favor individual historical learning and internal predictive state.
5. Any awareness or subjective-experience hypothesis remains separate even if steps 1–4 occur.

**Strong disconfirming outcomes:** apparent niche innovation vanishes once externally refreshed resources are controlled; higher-level organization never establishes group offspring; learning benefits disappear with cue timing preserved in reflex controls; consciousness-indicator behavior is easily copied by mechanistically inert comparators. Publish negative outcomes alongside any positives.

## Research choice, not an architecture decision

**Highest information per reading effort now:** characterize **AlChemy's failed higher-level combination and source-dependent basin of attraction**, cross-check against experimentally selected collective yeast and historical-contingency bacteria, and find a direct lab example of *constructing* (rather than merely exploiting) an ecological niche. This prioritizes causal conditions for open-ended change rather than endlessly expanding chatbot training literature.

A future build must be **separately authorized** and sandboxed, with lineage and scientific measurement gates and no conflation with Ora/AgentTest development.
