# Pass 9 — From unused resources to evolved environments: a causal research bridge

**2026-10-08 | Literature review and proposal only.** No independent statistical reproduction or simulated organism. The focus is a genuine uncertainty left by earlier Stringmol/AlChemy reviews: **does a lineage merely discover an affordance already present, or cause conditions under which a new affordance becomes accessible to another lineage?**

## Core empirical chain (do not double-count one evolution experiment)

1. **Blount, Borland & Lenski (2008)**: [PNAS 105:7899–7906](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0803151105) reports 12 long-term E. coli lines and one Cit+ innovation by about 31,500 generations. Replay experiments from frozen time points support background-dependent potentiation. **Citrate was supplied by experimenters from the beginning.** This establishes a newly evolved *ability to exploit an existing resource*, not de novo organism construction of that resource.
2. **Blount et al. (2012)**: [Nature 489:513–518, full PMC manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3461117/) traces the proximal genetic change to duplication/promoter capture enabling aerobic expression of a citrate transporter. This is a **molecular follow-up on the same LTEE lineage**, not an independently evolved citrate innovation.
3. **Quandt et al. (2015)**: [eLife 4:e09696](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.09696), [full article](https://elifesciences.org/articles/09696), report that pre-existing `gltA` citrate synthase changes favored growth on **acetate**, a metabolic by-product, and altered which citrate-uptake changes were adaptive; subsequent evolution reversed the earlier favored activity. This supplies a testable bridge from *constructed environment* to *historical access* to the next innovation.
4. **Bajić, Vila, Blount & Sánchez (2018)**: [PNAS 115:11286–11291](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1808485115), [author-linked data](https://github.com/vilacelestin/Bajicetal2018) mix genome-scale dynamic metabolic simulations with mutations experimentally reconstructed from the **same historical LTEE Ara-3 lineage**. In this system, citrate transporter **CitT** can import citrate while exporting other intermediates, changing extracellular opportunities; the work also manipulated acetate-kinase-related metabolic release in a model. Authors report empirical **noncommutative epistasis** (order/environment changes mutation fitness effects), but large-scale landscape deformation usually had little consequence at short mutational distances. **Do not infer from this that a perpetual novelty feedback loop has been demonstrated.**
5. **Bajić, Rebolleda-Gómez, Muñoz & Sánchez (2021)**: [Frontiers perspective](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmicb.2021.718082) proposes that niche construction may create sequential 'bridge environments' that make otherwise inaccessible innovations adaptive. Its authors explicitly identify major macroevolutionary implications as **underexplored, partly hypothetical**, not a validated universal principle.

**Independence accounting:** 2008/2012/2015/2018 are overlapping analyses/manipulations of the **same LTEE evolutionary history**, supplemented by new genetic/physiological interventions and models; the 2021 perspective synthesizes related work. Distinct *mechanism assays* strengthen causal understanding but do **not** yield five independent origins of citrate innovation.

## Distinct hypotheses, with controls for future research only

### H-N1: Access innovation, not construction
A lineage exploits an **unused but preexisting** environmental substrate.
- Signature: previously unconsumed substrate exists at t0; a novel hereditary uptake/process step causes consumption and survival.
- Counterexample: laboratory nutrient addition coincides with new ability, or phenotype is only observer classification.
- Historical positive example: LTEE citrate use.

### H-N2: Environmental construction
A lineage produces a by-product or changes a physical constraint that becomes a usable resource/environment **for descendants or a second lineage**.
- Signature: produced material can be directly traced to constructor; constructor deletion erases ecological opportunity; reintroducing the purified product rescues the function.
- Critical comparator: exogenously supplying the same product at matched concentration and flux, demonstrating novelty belongs to *producer-controlled ecology* rather than physics.
- Caveat: this produces a newly accessible niche **within fixed chemical/virtual rules**, not new laws of physics.

### H-N3: Evolution changes its own adjacent reachable possibilities
Constructed niches *alter the accessibility of future functional mutations* rather than merely changing population density.
- Signature: genotype A helps create environment E that changes sign/magnitude of genotype B's effect; time-order reversed and matched-environment contrasts.
- Evidence to scrutinize: Bajić 2018's noncommutative epistasis and the 2015 acetate-conditioned citrate pathway.
- Strong null: all apparent differences vanish under ancestor-conditioned media, constant resource ledger, or random-mutation controls.

### H-N4: New niches sustain major transitions
An environmental product becomes so crucial to two lineages that their joint unit develops **heritable new collective capabilities** and reproduces independently.
- Signature: repeatable collectively inherited organization + causal group-specific phenotype, verified beyond mere coexistence.
- Falsifier: lineages merely cohabit; group dissolves when mixed and has no independent daughters.
- Connection: [AlChemy 2024 L2 negative/rare cohabitation](41-pass9-alchemy-organization-barriers-full-review.md) and [snowflake yeast 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06052-1), but **not** experimental proof of a combined mechanism.

## Most useful neutral hypothesis (original)

**Niche-opportunity feedback can increase the *reachable space of ecological functions* without increasing the universe of primitive reaction rules.** One must measure both (i) organism-generated environmental artifacts and (ii) the subsequent heritable *use* of those artifacts. An expanding observer-generated taxonomy, a new resource added by developers, or evolution of a single exploit trait does not establish a causal self-expanding niche feedback.

Prospective quantitative readouts:
- (A_t): count of preregistered **causally usable affordances** accessible to a lineage at t; not just distinct products.
- (E_t): accessible affordances **depending on inhabitant-produced artifacts**; provenance and knockout/rescue evidence required.
- (D_t): independent descendant lineages **that inherit use** of an affordance; replicate at the world/lineage level.
- (K_t): **cost ledger** of construction, material/compute consumption and maintenance, including collapses.
- Stability under changed seeds and hidden post hoc-free external probes.

All quantities require an operational definition and measurement error; no one score constitutes "life", conscious experience, or indefinite open-endedness.
