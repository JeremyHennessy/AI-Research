# Pass 10 full-primary review — Chen et al. (2025): selection pressure and ancestral genotype constrain heritable multicellularity

**Reviewed 2026-10-08 | Full primary article E2, NOT independently reproduced.** The openly accessible main publication and methods, results, figures/captions, discussion and data-availability information were read. **178.7 MB supplementary data archive and deposited sequencing reads were NOT downloaded or analysed.**

**Primary:** I Chen Kimberly Chen, Shania Khatri, Matthew D. Herron, Frank Rosenzweig (2025). *Genetic Predisposition Toward Multicellularity in Chlamydomonas reinhardtii.* Genome Biology and Evolution 17(6), evaf090. DOI [10.1093/gbe/evaf090](https://doi.org/10.1093/gbe/evaf090), [open PMC full paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12124119/), Creative Commons **CC BY-NC 4.0** indicated in PMC.

## Exact question

Which matters to the transition from **unicellular algae to heritable, clonal multicellular phenotypes**: external selective regime, inherited starting genotype, or mutations accumulated during the experiment?

The paper deliberately begins two selection regimes with the **same mixed pool of 10 founders**, allowing a much sharper comparison than previous experiments initiated from different ancestors. It does **not** fully isolate causal mutations, because competitive filtering of initial genotypes and downstream changes are not directly resolved.

## Design

- Green alga *Chlamydomonas reinhardtii*. Founder stock mixed 10 phenotypically and genomically differentiated strains (2 laboratory strains, 8 field isolates) in equal volumes. This is a **mixed starting population**, not ten independently evolved origins in each arm.
- **24 independently propagated populations**: 12 exposed to *Paramecium tetraurelia* predation and 12 selected for rapid settling by mild centrifugation. Both in COMBO medium with periodic fresh supplies, ~22.5°C, 14:10 light:dark schedule.
- **40 weekly transfers, estimated ~15 generations per transfer (~600 asexual generations)**, and transfer bottlenecks of ~1–2 million algae cells. **No unselected control arm**: the two arms compare distinct selection mechanisms, not simply selection vs no selection.
- Screening every eight weekly transfers: small-colony phenotype and microscopy, then **three serial platings** to assess persistence in a new medium. Figure methods distinguish temporary clusters from stably inherited clusters.
- Morphology: repeated imaging and DAPI-stained nuclear counts; **four biological repetitions per evolved strain, two per ancestors** for growth time courses; technical time-lapse wells: 72 hours, five technical replicate wells per strain. These are **not** 24× multiple new evolution runs.
- Ancestry: mating-type PCR plus filtered whole-genome variants of **four representative evolved strains** and comparison to founder genomes; aligned sequencing depths approximately 14×–18× for those evolved strains. **Two additional isolates from one evolved population not independently sequenced**, because authors inferred likely similarity.
- Data: deposited sequencing under **PRJNA820479** / accessions **SAMN27009621–24**. Supplementary file linked through publisher (178.7 MB), not retrieved.

## Actual results and negatives

1. **4/12 independent predator-selected populations** yielded **stably heritable multicellular lineages** after the 40-transfer observation window. **0/12 settling-selected populations** yielded stably heritable multicellularity by the same test. Other clusters appeared in BOTH treatments during earlier screens but **did not persist** through serial platings: these are **transient**, not stable evolutionary-individual successes.
2. The authors isolated **six stable multicellular strains** from the four successful predator-exposed populations, not six independent successful population histories. Three of those six came from *one* population (PS02).
3. Evolved strains form daughter cells that stay within **clonal clusters**, observed with 72-hour time-lapse microscopy; inherited structures arise from divisions rather than unrelated cells aggregating. **This establishes developmental formation**, not a complete multigenerational causal graph of independently reproducing cluster-level daughters.
4. Most inherited clusters contained roughly 2–10 cells at characteristic sampling, but one isolate, **PS09-32-A**, reached a **156-cell median at a particular maximum sampling time** (not the typical size of all descendants). Daily size trajectories differed markedly.
5. Genome-wide founder markers assign **three** representative evolved multicellular isolates to **founder CC-2936**, and the fourth sequenced evolved isolate to **CC-1690**; the authors infer all six isolates arose from those **two ancestral strain backgrounds**. They did **not** pinpoint/validate the causative mutations by knockout, backcrossing or allele swap.
6. Some early apparent successes, including cases from PS05/PS11 at 16 weeks and selected settling lines, **failed the permanence filter**. This is strong **counterevidence** against equating an observed cluster with inherited new individuality.

## Important caveats from paper's own discussion

- Differential competitive fitness among the ten starting founders is not measured across time; **purging of poorly competing founders could explain part of the concentration of successful ancestry**. The authors explicitly propose future founder competition experiments and temporal genotyping.
- Predation and settling differ in many specific microenvironment dynamics; the study **does not** establish that settling cannot ever select multicellularity. Earlier work had produced multicellularity via settling with different genetic histories.
- Heritable multicellular **structure** need not imply stable group-level reproductive individuality or sustained adaptive group evolution. Differentiation, endogenous resource-maintenance, repair and cognition were not established.
- Genomic fingerprints are source-tracing evidence, not direct causality for the transitions or a proof of the exact mutations.
- An observation window of 600 generations and selected isolates cannot establish open-ended evolution; **zero** observations of subjective experience, neural cognition or digital organization.

## Prospective mechanism tests (original research directions; NOT run)

- **Founder-competitiveness confound:** repeat *in principle* with one founder per population and a matched starting mixed population; serially sequence founder frequencies. Without this, genetic 'predisposition' and population sorting remain partly confounded.
- **Heritability vs appearance:** preregister persistence after multiple environment transfers and count *independently produced daughter clusters*, not merely large aggregate sizes or repeated frames.
- **Causal mutation test:** reconstruct candidate alleles one at a time and demonstrate retained collective reproductive life cycles in both ancestral and evolved genetic backgrounds.
- **Ecological generalization:** compare descendants with/without predators and novel disturbances, while keeping external nutrient refresh explicit.
- **Negative outcome preservation:** report all 24 evolution populations and all temporary failed clusters, not only attractive surviving isolates.
