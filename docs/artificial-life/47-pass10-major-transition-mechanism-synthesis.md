# Pass 10 synthesis — when a group becomes an evolutionary individual

**2026-10-08 | Original evidence comparison, not an experiment or living-system recipe.** This synthesis draws from separate biological evolution cohorts and a computational-organization counterexample. Treat physical reproduction, digital replication and philosophical individuality as **non-interchangeable** evidence classes.

## Central finding: the full life cycle, not the visual group, is the candidate individual

A stable collection can persist, look complex and cooperate yet fail to **reproduce as a distinct lineage**. Even a group that reproduces can have component-level competition swamp group-level selection in its reproductive phase. The strongest informative distinctions are:

1. **Group appearance:** clustering or a stable interaction graph (weak).
2. **Group parentage:** a collective produces a daughter collective with traceable causal ancestry (stronger).
3. **Group heredity:** parent differences predict descendant **collective functions** across a bottleneck and novel conditions (stronger).
4. **Group-level selection:** among-collective heritable variance predicts group descendants *after controlling for component fitness and externally provided selection machinery* (stronger).
5. **Endogenous individuality:** the collective maintains its own interaction/reproductive prerequisites and transmits them through descendants after outside scaffolding is removed or critically changed (still mostly unproven in our digital comparisons).
6. **Consciousness:** independent scientific question, *not* implied by any level above.

Do not turn this into a single numerical aliveness score. Report each level alongside test method, negative control, uncertainty and scope.

## Strongest counterexamples to the naive 'more complexity means life' claim

- **Mixing breaks group selection despite groups still existing (Rose 2020).** Protection of population boundaries during reproduction may matter more than visual cluster stability.
- **Many apparent clusters are transient (Chen 2025).** Images and temporary coexistence are false positives for heritable collective individuality.
- **Initial genotype biases possible outcomes (Chen 2025).** Without measuring ancestry and competitive founder sorting, environmental pressure alone is insufficient explanation.
- **Two already functional organizations may destroy each other (AlChemy 2024).** Interoperability between lower-level functioning units is not automatic or necessarily advantageous.
- **Community breeding can fail from poor collective heritability (Salazar/Mitri 2025).** Independent cells achieving good aggregate function is weaker than transmitting that function as a whole.
- **Scaffolding-induced stability may be model-specific (Doulcier 2024).** 'Scaffold removed' within theory can mean changing one parameter while keeping the external reproductive patch system intact.

## Original research thesis: reproductive organization as a maintained causal interface

**New hypothesis (untested):** sustained new individuality requires an **interface that preserves causally necessary organizational relationships across reproduction**. It may be a single-cell germline, inheritable interacting-program network, compositional chemical set, propagule bundle, or something presently unknown. It need not be a rigid physical shell.

**Competing explanations:**
- A. **Selection-structure only:** environmental partitioning and fresh-founder protocols determine whether group-level traits evolve; organism-built boundaries are unnecessary.
- B. **Heritable interface:** lineages acquire an internal, transportable reproductive protocol that permits retention of collective function after some external partitioning disappears.
- C. **Stable dependence without heredity:** collections persist as attractors or symbioses but never acquire distinct group descendants; no new evolutionary individual.
- D. **Observer artifact:** grouping metric follows appearances while causally distinct lineages merge or are manually reconstructed by the experimenter.

Only evidence that separates these causes can justify claiming a new individual has arisen.

## Research-only preregistration outline (FUTURE, no authorization for implementation)

**Question:** does a multi-component group transmit collective function through reproduction *without relying on a fixed externally supplied group copier/selector*?

1. **Identity before behavior:** define group as a causally maintained organizational process, not a single pixel cluster, entropy score or name assigned by an observer.
2. **Bottleneck modes (controlled):** lineage-separated propagules; equal-sized globally mixed propagules; deliberately disrupted lineage handoff; externally forced copy API; no-copy sterile comparison. Equalize costs and populations where comparability permits.
3. **Parent–daughter evidence:** trace multiple independently viable daughters and grandchildren; freeze state genealogy before analysis; destructive interventions assess causal dependency of descendants on parent group.
4. **Group vs component selection:** measure both, normalized for reproduction time, boundary survival and resource expenditure; include extinct lines in all denominators.
5. **External scaffold audit:** tag every supplied action: carrying capacity, resource refill, boundary generation, forced death/rebirth, copy/replicate operations, and fitness advantages. **Selective external weakening ≠ complete scaffold independence.**
6. **Resilience vs heredity:** distinguish returning to the *same* collective function after disturbance from shifting to a new equilibrium or re-seeding from a surviving ancestor.
7. **Blind ecological challenge:** test new functional interaction in descendant groups under preregistered held-out environments without feeding target scores back to selection.
8. **Stop/negative criteria:** no independent group offspring; no functional heredity above shuffled groups; dependence exclusively on copying API; group fitness effect explained entirely by cell growth; results vanish across independent seeds/substrates.
9. **Statistical unit:** whole world/independent evolution population, not frame, colony picked from same population, individual member, or repeated evaluation of the same archived data.
10. **Consciousness firewall:** no life/cognition/conscience/consciousness classification follows from positive outcomes; any welfare or experience hypothesis requires independent ethics and theory-discrimination review.

**No new executable experiment plan or AL experimental registry entry is created by this outline.** This is a human-readable preparation for separate future approval.
