## Scientific conclusion with uncertainty

**Strongest author-reported intervention:** In the bacteria comparison, reproductive-lineage mixing alone changes the direction of selection on group versus component traits. **Independent lineages and multi-generation functional inheritance, not merely stable components, are the unit to prioritize.**

**Most persuasive negative:** 2025 algae experiment shows transient multicellular structures that **failed serial-passage inheritance**; a compelling video or snapshot can be scientifically misleading.

**Most important unresolved condition:** The life cycle, selection scheme and physical compartment are still imposed by experimenters. Published success is not proof that a collective can originate, maintain, export and evolve its complete reproductive interface by itself. Computational AlChemy's interactions likewise do not yet show independently viable collective daughters.

**Important distinction:** organismhood, learning, predictive intelligence, moral conscience and phenomenal consciousness are different evidentiary questions; none established by this tenth pass.
