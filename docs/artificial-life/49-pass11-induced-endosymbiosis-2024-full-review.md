# Pass 11 E2 — Giger et al. 2024: induced endosymbiosis and its loss after selection ends

[Complete openly available PMC article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11560845/) is **CC BY 4.0**. ## Question and design

Can physical intracellular pairing, followed by positive selection, establish **vertically inherited** host–symbiont association—and does this association survive once outside selection is withdrawn?

1. Experimenters used fluidic force microscopy to inject bacteria into *Rhizopus microsporus* fungal hyphae, including a natural endosymbiotic bacterium *Mycetohabitans rhizoxinica* and an outsider *E. coli*. A natural endosymbiotic fungus (EH) and a non-host fungus (NH) offered controlled comparisons.
2. **E. coli negative outcome:** rapidly growing bacteria formed local clumps; the fungus constructed septa confining them, while uninfected hyphae persisted. No injected *E. coli* detected in spores with an assay detection boundary **1/1,000,000 positive spores**, three measurements. Neither absence beyond detection nor failure of every conceivable bacterial strain follows.
3. **M. rhizoxinica new-host pairing:** a small fraction of spores from injected non-host fungus inherited bacteria, initially **≤4%** of spores in classified samples. Some infected spores germinated and produced bacteria-bearing fungal offspring.
4. **Directed propagation:** spores from **one injected founder germling** were sorted for bacterium positivity by fluorescence-activated cell sorting (FACS); initially partitioned to **ten derived selection lines**, then three selected strong lines and one pooled line continued three additional rounds after the first seven. These are **not ten independent introductions/origins of endosymbiosis**.
5. **Separate assays:** germination, percentage of positive spores, crossover reinjections of evolved/ancestral host and symbiont, fluorescent-bacterial load, host/bacterial resequencing, and a **five-round no-FACS-selection washout** to test stability. Several figure measures report biological vs technical replicates separately.

## Results (author-reported)

| Observation | Primary figure/method | Key limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Initial bacteria-positive spores germinated ~6.3%, versus 63% bacteria-free spores | Fig. 3b | Strong host cost; assay specific to fungus/medium/selection |
| In selected populations, infected-spore germination increased to ~75% by round 10; positivity increased from **0.01% to up to 24.1%** | Fig. 3b–c | Positivity and germination are distinct measures, and many lines share one founder |
| Mean **fitness index** (positive fraction × germination success) reached **14.0%** by round 10, with best line ~25.3% | Fig. 3d | Engineered/observational index, not proof of autonomous reproduction or ecosystem robustness |
| Both bacterially produced WF-1360F and derived rhizoxin were detected in all ten round-7 lines | Fig. 3f–g | Demonstrates transferred *metabolic function*, not a beneficial fitness effect of rhizoxin in this selected lab environment |
| Crossover reinjection with evolved fungal host + ancestral bacterium retained high germination/colonization; genomic changes enriched on fungus side | Fig. 4–5 | Mechanism inferred from crossover and allele frequencies; **no allele-specific causal reconstruction**, epigenetics/structural variation not excluded |
| Without FACS selection, ancestral pairing fell below propagation threshold within **one** round; evolved pairing survived longer but fell below threshold after **four to five** rounds | Fig. 5b | **Most decisive negative:** evolutionary gains had not made the association independently stable |

## What this study DOES and DOES NOT demonstrate

- **Demonstrates, as published evidence:** experimentally induced intracellular compatibility, some vertical inheritance, adaptation under strong laboratory sorting, a metabolically novel host association, and experimentally observed **loss when selection is lifted**.
- **Does not demonstrate:** endogenous formation of initial host–symbiont encounter; fully self-produced intergenerational reproduction; permanent obligate mutualism; inherited *new primitive chemistry*; indefinitely open-ended evolution; spontaneous software organism; cognition, morality or subjective consciousness.
- **External scaffold inventory:** direct bacterial implantation, founder selection, FACS enrichment of bacteria-positive spores, fresh plates and media, investigator chosen propagation schedule, fixed fungal spore-making infrastructure. These remain indispensable for interpreting 'independent individuality.'
- **Scientific independence:** selected-line populations descend from one original injected germling; replicate lineages are independent **propagation outcomes after the founder**, not independent biochemical origins. The model predicting washout is not another experimental cohort.

## Prospective causal test design — paper only, not authorized to execute

- Compare **selection-withdrawn paired daughters** across multiple generations with matched resource/host density, avoiding re-sorting.
- Distinguish changed host physiology vs partner adjustment through genetic reconstruction and crossover; include no-bacterium/symbiont-disabled controls.
- Replace observed 'symbiont present' with **co-heredity of its ecological function** and partner-dependent descendant fertility, not just fluorescent positivity.
- Prospectively record all extinction/washout trajectories, not only selected survivors.
- Distinguish an evolved **reproductive interface** (partner delivery into viable spores) from externally preexisting fungal reproductive apparatus.
- Test whether reduced symbiont load alone can account for increased host germination, rather than inferred new reciprocal cooperation.

## Connection to Pass 11 competing theory

**Positive signal:** evolving a cost-reducing host–symbiont **interface** can make a newly imposed association heritable and less fragile.

**Direct falsifying counterexample to a naive 'obligate mutualism becomes autonomous life' theory:** when positive external selection ended, even improved inherited associations disappeared over four to five rounds. Future digital claims must include **selection withdrawal**, long daughter-lineage persistence and causal functional dependence, not simply a visually maintained group.
