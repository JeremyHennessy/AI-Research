# Pass 11 — Original combined path: Endogenous Ecological–Reproductive Closure (EERC)

**Created 2026-10-08 | UNVERIFIED ORIGINAL SCIENTIFIC HYPOTHESIS, not demonstrated organism, software architecture or deployed system.** The user-directed research goal is to discover foundations from which living-like organization and perhaps later **intelligence** could develop along a new path, without assuming transformers, LLM prompting, human-set cognitive tasks or current AI-agent designs. **Phenomenal consciousness and moral conscience are independent unanswered questions.**

**Scope boundary:** paper-only hypothesis, architecture comparison, and preregistration sketch inside AI-Research; **no new code, simulator, world, organism, trained learner or Ora/AgentTest modifications**, and no authorization for future execution inferred. The researcher can separately authorize bounded experiments later.

## The central open question

Why does a promising ecology so often stop at **coexistence**, collapse into **single-partner escape**, or require experimenters to reconstruct its collective reproduction? Under what causal conditions can a system **generate ecological opportunities and carry the relationships that created them into descendants**?

This combines seven reviewed threads but does **not** assert that their simple combination would succeed:

1. [AlChemy 2024](41-pass9-alchemy-organization-barriers-full-review.md): internally stable computational organizations rarely combine into inherited higher-level ones.
2. [Stringmol 2017/2021](27-semantic-closure-2017-full-review.md): copying and interpreting codes can coevolve in partially viable seeded chemistries, yet parasitism/semantic incompatibility is frequent.
3. [Rose et al. 2020](45-pass10-pseudomonas-life-cycle-2020-full-review.md): **who shares a propagule** and when controls group selection independently of component replication.
4. [Giger et al. 2024](49-pass11-induced-endosymbiosis-2024-full-review.md): selection-improved transferred metabolic function and vertical symbiont inheritance still **wash out in 4–5 unselected rounds**.
5. [Krishnan et al. 2026](50-pass11-ectosymbiosis-2026-theoretical-review.md): self-produced inhibitory metabolite can make a direct *physical association* beneficial; the mutant attachment and reproduction arrangements are **assumed in the model**.
6. [Fisher et al. 2017](51-pass11-host-symbiont-dependence-2017-full-review.md): vertical transmission correlates with greater host dependence across many studied symbioses, but correlation is not spontaneous origin of a higher-level unit.
7. [Pauli et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-27630-9) and [Melero-Jiménez et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-58742-1): obligate cross-feeding can **reduce evolvability**, or yield rescue through **loss of cooperation**. Cooperation is not automatically a one-way ladder toward life.

### Original EERC proposal

**Hypothesis EERC-H1:** The missing intermediate between ecological cooperation and a new evolutionary individual may be an **endogenously maintained, heritable reproductive interface**: a causal organization that (a) produces/maintains a new usable environmental opportunity, (b) constrains partners into a locally beneficial interaction, and (c) **transmits the necessary relationship** to independently viable daughter organizations, with no externally installed *collective copying* or human-ranked novelty reward.

Such an interface could be a **material package, regeneration cycle, partner-delivery mechanism, interpreter linkage or dispersed causal protocol**. It must not be identified merely with a cell wall, shared memory address, image boundary or named 'agent'. **This is an open search target**, not a proposed implementation of a new 'life module'.

**Core falsifier:** the surviving descendant can perform the same function after randomly mixing components or having the parent interface disabled; then the identified interface is not necessary, and the particular EERC causal claim fails. Other falsifiers: descendants cannot re-create the interface, the benefit disappears under externally matched nutrient flux, or no independently reproductive daughter groups appear.

## Five proposed causal transitions — each optional, independently testable

| Transition | Required observation | Negative / false positive |
|---|---|---|
| **T1. Constructed opportunity** | A population produces a metabolite/interaction opportunity, documented by a material or computational resource provenance ledger; another lineage can *functionally* use it | Designer newly supplies target resource; larger external nutrient budget alone explains benefit |
| **T2. Relational stabilization** | Interactions suppress a self-generated cost or produce complementary viability that favors *maintaining a particular relation* | Mere free-rider proliferation, ephemeral commensalism, or same effect under any generic nutrient replacement |
| **T3. Endogenous interface** | Components change interactions to create a persistent **reproductive transfer mechanism** needed for a functional daughter | Explicit group-copy API, externally enclosed habitat automatically duplicates every member |
| **T4. Collective daughter heredity** | Two or more independently viable daughters retain relation-dependent function and reproduce further descendants *without parent intervention* | Re-clustering by observer, source container mixing, only a single founder reproduction, selected offspring reassembled by researcher |
| **T5. Conditional ecological memory** | Same continuing individual changes later regulation after experience of delayed perturbations; causal memory ablation removes the advantage while sensory and budget controls match | Reflex, evolved population sorting, stored experimenter-provided target or model weights optimized for a human grading objective |

**Consciousness/agency firewall:** T1–T4 say something about *organization and evolution*, T5 says something about *within-life learning*. None says anything conclusive about phenomenal experience, human-like intelligence or moral judgment. The latter demand separate theories and safety/ethics review.

## A formally checkable study object without choosing a substrate

State at time t (conceptual only): 
- \(R_t\): bounded resources and external flux ledger (no negative or untracked grants).
- \(X_t=\{x_{i,t}\}\): individual component internal states and lineage identifiers.
- \(J_t(i,j)\): observed partner-specific causal exchange rates/interventions, not only adjacency.
- \(M_t\): **candidate reproductive interface state** (if it arises), represented as measurable relations and resources, not privileged 'organism' class in physics.
- \(D_t\): ancestor→daughter causal reproduction DAG, including independently reproducing grandchildren.
- \(H_t\): within-lifetime history variable used to regulate behavior; **not** a preloaded language model memory.
- \(A_t\): previously absent but newly accessible **testable functions** under the fixed substrate rules; include controls for observer taxonomies.

A resource-coupled transition rule (NOT a software specification):
\[
(R_{t+1},X_{t+1},J_{t+1})=F_{\phi}(R_t,X_t,J_t,\xi_t)
\]
where \(F_{\phi}\) is an explicitly **fixed** finite substrate law with stochasticity \(\xi_t\). Components may evolve or locally compose effective semantics, but researchers must not quietly change \(\phi\) mid-run and call it endogenous novelty.

**Candidate interface dependence** for an inherited function \(f\): 
\[
\Delta_f=\Pr(f\mid \mathrm{intact}\ M,\ \mathrm{held\!\!\!\!out})-\Pr(f\mid \mathrm{causal\ knockout}\ M,\ \mathrm{matched\ conditions})
\]
Both terms are prospective quantities with independent unit/CI and recovery controls. Neither a positive \(\Delta_f\) nor a complex causal graph proves life.

**Collective transmission:** report the fraction of parent groups whose *independent daughters/granddaughters* maintain \(f\) beyond each preregistered horizon, together with 95% uncertainty intervals and **extinction-included** denominators. Never redefine 'population' from pixels or treat repeat observations of one world as replicates.

**Self-constructed opportunity:** record counterfactual causal contribution of each produced resource/protocol to a subsequent lineage's feasible functional transition. Count a new *reachably usable relation*, not a new fundamental physics operator.

## Three neutral substrate families (competing, NOT a forced combined software design)

1. **C — Finite resource reaction/constraint network.** No language-model requirement, reactions have conservation and stochastic costs. *Best for:* material feedback, repair, producer/consumer coupling. *Weakest:* transmissible genotype-like specification and external-specified chemical grammar.
2. **S — Executable artificial chemistry with evolving expression.** Molecules can participate in copying/interpretation and partner-dependent reactions. *Best for:* lineage, heritable functional semantics. *Weakest:* seeded copier, fixed interpreter base opcodes, implementation loops and parasitic collapse.
3. **F — Spatial process field with local inheritable rules.** Components interact by propagating states/transport under explicit mass/resource budget. *Best for:* endogenous geometry and local boundary responses. *Weakest:* convincing phenotype without causal daughters, externally normalized field rules and absent child fertility.

**Decision criterion:** pick the first **scientifically discriminating contrast**, not the most sophisticated, compelling visual, or apparently anthropomorphic architecture. In the first future experiment, stay within **one** foundation; do not deploy all three together. Later matched cross-substrate comparisons can reject an overfit EERC result.

## Differentiating against the past and current AI

This is **not** 'Ora Phase 42 renamed', not a scaffolded virtual agent tasked to collect tokens, not prewritten competing NPCs, not a pretrained LLM with a memory database, not an optimizer rewarded for novelty, and not a simulation with permanent engine-protected group identities. An initial laboratory comparison should not even need natural language, symbolic logic tasks, or an OpenAI API.

The research object is **an organizational lineage over an ecological life cycle**, not the prose or 'choices' of a chatbot. Language or reflective intelligence, if it ever becomes scientifically justified to pursue, is a much later and separable hypothesis.

## What would justify changing the research program?

A multi-seed matched study showing **producer-specific ecological change**, **interface-dependent inherited group functionality**, **independently viable grandchildren**, and **resilience when externally required sorting/re-pairing is reduced**, all without a task novelty reward, would support EERC as an informative *hypothesis*. It would **not** alone demonstrate indefinite open-ended evolution, a conscious mind, moral status, or 'living' under a universally accepted definition.

Conversely, if groups only persist with external copy or forced partner reassembly, **reject the higher-individuality claim** for that design. If independent components escape cooperation under changed conditions, report a **mutualism-breakdown negative**, following the 2022/2025 bacteria results. If an apparent new niche only reflects greater externally supplied food, reject the niche-construction claim.
