## Core scientific findings (and why they matter)

**Actual new-host inheritance does not establish stable independence (Giger et al., 2024).** A lab-injected fungal/bacterial pairing acquired selected spore inheritance and transferred rhizoxin metabolism, but positive association disappeared after **4–5 rounds once FACS positive sorting ended** (one round for ancestral). Positive selection can produce a promising association that does not form a self-sustaining collective lineage. Ten selected lineages trace to **ONE injected founder**.

**Beneficial metabolic contact is not evolved reproduction (Krishnan et al., 2026).** Analytical model chooses particular fixed inhibiting waste chemistry and pre-existing host mutant able to bind partners. Conditions can make the mutant ecologically favorable but do not produce organismal daughters autonomously, evolve attachment mutations endogenously, or demonstrate subjective consciousness. The authors explicitly say physical vertically inherited higher-level transitions remain to be shown.

**Dependence and transmission should be measured separately (Fisher et al., 2017).** Across **106 published host–bacterial pairing records** (58 symbionts, 89 hosts, overlapping ancestries), removal of vertically transmitted symbionts correlates with larger host fitness loss than horizontally transmitted ones (~52% vs ~21% in the applicable **subset**). This is a phylogenetically adjusted **correlational** comparison, not 106 new lab transitions or proof of causality. Intracellular location alone was not a significant predictor in the study.

**Ecological diversity can persist without emerging organismhood (Al-Tameemi & Rodríguez-Verdugo, 2024).** A producer leaked benzoate used by another species; consumer morphotypes coexisted about 150 generations in a co-culture. The hypothesis of frequency-dependent maintenance **failed the authors' direct invasion assays**, and no matched exogenous benzoate flux control was run. Their **four co-culture evolution lines were inherited from previously reported evolution work**; follow-on measurements do not constitute four additional independent evolutionary origins.

**Strong independent counterevidence:** [Pauli et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-27630-9) report obligate cross-feeding reducing ability to adapt under antibiotic stress, and [Melero-Jiménez et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-58742-1) report recovery of most stressed cross-feeding communities **by losing one partner**, not by rescuing the partnership. Both stay **E1** in the canonical catalog pending complete figure/supplement audits. [Athreya et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1086/737588) is a copyrighted, partly inaccessible *theoretical* study showing dependence may develop earlier than reproductive cohesion; no E2 claim. [Black et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41559-019-1086-9) is an ecological patch-scaffolding *model*, not spontaneous microbe-built grouping.

## Original combined path: EERC

**EERC is a hypothesis about transgenerational *relationships*, not a proposal to train a conscious LLM.** Candidate chain: (1) inhabitants produce new usable ecological opportunity → (2) relation becomes adaptive via cost or reciprocal buffering → (3) participants internally maintain an interface that carries necessary components/relations across reproduction → (4) independently viable daughters transmit **group-specific functions** → (5) contingent later within-life state/history can make regulation predictive.

It may fail at every arrow. The path's real scientific value is its **orthogonal nulls**: externally matched nutrients, partner re-pairing, interface knockout/rescue, positive *preinstalled group copy* false-positive, complete extinct lineages, selective scaffold withdrawal, plus delayed cue/history tests. **Life, intelligence, moral conscience and subjective consciousness are explicitly separate claims.**

EERC's newness is a proposed integrated **causal test program**, **not** a discovery that no prior researcher considered these principles. Keep three rival substrates (finite chemical networks, expression-based molecule programs, mass-conserving local fields) **independent** until a specific test justifies combined implementation.
