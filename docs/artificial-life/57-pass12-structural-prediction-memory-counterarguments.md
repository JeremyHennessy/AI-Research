# Pass 12 — Structural memory, inherited regulatory anticipation, and the false-learning traps

**Research comparison 2026-10-08; selected primary methods and author reports, E1 unless separately audited.** No experiments, source program, datasets or statistics independently reproduced. We deliberately separate within-life habituation, inherited cell states, horizontal transfer and genotype-selected anticipatory regulation.

## Evidence strands with distinct origins and controls

### 1. Physarum encodes a nutrient-location trace in its own tube network

[Mirna Kramar & Karen Alim 2021, PNAS, DOI 10.1073/pnas.2007815118](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2007815118). A giant slime mold's tubular network shifts its **relative tube diameters** after localized nutrient stimulus. A transport/softening theoretical model reproduces the directional diameter changes and predicts which existing thicker paths are reinforced by later stimuli. The organism's physical configuration itself is an environmental-history-dependent variable that influences later transport/migration.

**Important limits:** chemical softening agent is a **model inference** rather than conclusively molecularly identified; mechanical transport history and altered flow need not demonstrate the organism makes an internal conceptual map. The original authors say identical-network randomized histories are **not experimentally available**; evidence for retrieval rests partly on observational network differences and a model, not a randomized knockout of exactly the memory-bearing tube hierarchy. It does not show vertical passage into daughter lineages or indefinite new function. Large supplemental figures and numerical data were not inspected in AI-Research.

### 2. Physarum habituation and recovery are stimulus-specific changes inside an individual

[Boisseau, Vogel & Dussutour 2016, Proc R Soc B, DOI 10.1098/rspb.2016.0446](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2016.0446), reports avoidance of quinine/caffeine diminishing with repeated exposures and reappearing after removal, with a **stimulus-specificity** control to distinguish mere locomotor fatigue from habituation.

**Source independence trap:** [Vogel & Dussutour 2016 fusion](56-pass12-physarum-fusion-memory-complete-review.md) is a different experimental paper from some of the **same researchers**, studying sodium-chloride habituation and then fusion. The later fusion experiment uses an already characterized organism/paradigm; it is not an independent cross-species validation of all prior 2016 results.

### 3. Stentor behavior may encode a history variable, not a continuous population score

[Rajan et al. 2022 (journal issue 2023), DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2022.11.010](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2022.11.010) measured **individual** Stentor contraction probabilities under repeated mechanical taps. Each cell appeared to transition sharply between high/low responding; the graded population curve could reflect heterogeneous *switch times*, not each cell smoothly learning the same way. Parameters depend on **stimulus intensity and timing**. A two-state stochastic model describes data; it is a **phenomenological fit**, not a proven molecular circuit.

[Rajan & Marshall 2025, DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2025.05.071](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2025.05.071) proposed an explicit **receptor-inactivation model**. Alternating strong/weak and sustained-training experiments supported some model predictions; its hidden receptor count has **not** been biochemically validated as the single unique memory storage variable.

[Escobedo et al. 2026, bioRxiv DOI 10.64898/2026.06.06.730631](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.06.06.730631) is an **unreviewed preprint** reporting a **fractional-order ~−30 dB/decade** frequency-response roll-off and finding none of the earlier habituation models in their then-present form accounts for that result. Their proposed parallel-timescale inhibition model is a scientific alternative, **not a settled new biological memory mechanism**. It may reflect distinct stimulation protocols; head-to-head preregistered independent replication is missing.

[Rajan et al. 2026](55-pass12-stentor-molecular-memory-and-daughters.md) explores calcium-dependent molecular processes and reports state persistence into progeny; because this is an overlapping Stentor research program, the 2022/2025/2026 studies must not be counted as **four independent species-level demonstrations**, despite having distinct measurements.

### 4. Bacterial anticipation may arise through selection over generations, NOT learning in one cell

[Tagkopoulos, Liu & Tavazoie 2008, Science, DOI 10.1126/science.1154456](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1154456): computationally evolved biochemical networks with **designer-defined dynamic environmental correlations** can acquire anticipatory response; in natural E. coli the authors identified co-regulation of temperature and oxygen response, then **evolved bacterial populations under inverted temporal environmental ordering** and observed gene-expression decoupling.

[Mitchell et al. 2009, Nature, DOI 10.1038/nature08112](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08112): early cues that predict a later condition can trigger readiness in E. coli/yeast. Laboratory evolution with different sequence statistics can degrade that anticipatory regulatory relationship.

**Scientific distinction:** expression that predicts a future environment can be **a hard-wired genetic adaptation to repeated ancestral ecological conditions**, not **within-individual learned** adjustment to a newly discovered correlation. Both are biologically useful. Neither is evidence for a reflective inner world.

## Six competing memory substrates — not a predefined software architecture

| Candidate stored history | Published analogy | What could imitate success | Necessary future intervention |
|---|---|---|---|
| Receptor/protein state | Stentor 2026 | drug toxicity/response exclusion; population sorting | state perturbation with intact motor response and matched physiology |
| Temporally distributed interacting molecular states | Stentor 2026 preprint | fractional curve fitted after observing task | novel unseen frequencies, frozen model, independent preparations |

| Genetically installed anticipation | E. coli 2008/yeast 2009 | species sorting, selection across many generations | same clone within-life cue reversal and genotype-controlled challenge |
| Intergenerational persistent cell state | Stentor 2026 daughter report | shared media, inherited static protein amounts, mutant selection | separately reared daughters and multi-generation fading/retention tests |

## Scientific consequences for EERC

- **Memory need not be a centralized database or text**, but the proposed physical carriers and dynamics **must be measured** and distinguished from response fatigue or equilibrium relaxation.
- **Within-life update** requires persistent, individual-specific changes after different histories while genome, current cues and external resource availability are matched. A population mean curve does not qualify by itself.
- **Transmissibility** requires explicit mechanisms and distinct tests: copying/daughter inheritance, fusion/horizontal transfer, and rediscovering the same response through shared environment are scientifically different.
- **Adaptive benefit** requires a separately measured consequence under held-out ecological perturbation and a measured resource/time cost. Even strong habituation is not yet a generic learning-to-predict mechanism.
- **Emergent agency** does not follow from habituation; regulatory history may be useful without subjective experience, moral conscience, task-general intelligence or language.
