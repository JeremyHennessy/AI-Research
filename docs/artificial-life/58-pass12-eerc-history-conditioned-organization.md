# Pass 12 — EERC extension: history-conditioned organization, not a preinstalled memory module

**2026-10-08 | Original unverified theoretical research; NO implementation.** This extends the independent [Endogenous Ecological–Reproductive Closure (EERC) version 1](53-pass11-new-path-ecological-reproductive-closure.md), preserving its original [machine-readable baseline](../../data/alife/combined-path-eerc-v1.json). A separate version 2 describes alternatives and falsification only, not authority to build a living organism.

## The precise gap in EERC v1

EERC already posits an ecological opportunity, a stabilizing relation, a reproductive interface and inherited group function. None of those proves **an individual changes its own future behavior based on personal experience**. Evolutionary adaptation of a lineage, a persistent attractor and instantaneous response to environmental cues are different from *within-lifetime learning*.

**New working hypothesis (EERC-M):** If a self-maintaining organization contains biochemical, mechanical, or informational states whose persistence depends on its own interactions, **the organization may be able to condition later regulatory responses on a trace of its past without a separately authored memory component**. Whether this offers a viability advantage, persists through reproduction or broadens adaptive opportunities is untested.

The claim must survive an important null: **a fixed filter/hysteresis circuit can appear to remember**. An environment-conditioned reaction network is not thereby conscious or intelligent, and any result may be mechanistically equivalent to ordinary engineering feedback. What matters is whether memory-bearing causal structure is *generated, maintained, modified and inherited or transferred under ecological selection*, not whether the observer calls its state "memory".

## Four separable properties that an EERC organism would need to earn by evidence

1. **Encoding**: two preparations with identical current cues/genome/resources but different histories have different internal measurable states and later responses.
2. **Persistence/recall**: after cue removal/washout, prior state affects a **different future challenge**, with recovery/decay and negative-control measurements.
3. **Causal utility**: disabling the proposed history carrier changes held-out ecological performance; restoring it causally rescues the function without supplying a new reward.
4. **Transmission / reconstruction**: a daughter (vertical), interacting cell (horizontal) or reconstituted collective inherits **functional dependence on the state**, not only similar appearance. This need not happen for learning to be real, but would strengthen the path toward higher-level individuality.

**Separation of mechanisms:** A receptor phosphorylation state in Stentor (molecular **candidate**), adaptive network tube geometry in Physarum (physical), time-dependent fusion transfer (horizontal), and evolved microbial pre-emptive gene regulation (phylogenetic) are **not one shared memory implementation**. They suggest alternative substrates and intervenable causal analogies.

## Competing models — NOT to combine prematurely

| Hypothesis | Main memory mechanism | Falsifier |
|---|---|---|
| H-M1 Molecular-state memory | Reversible modifications of already-maintained components encode training history | Effect disappears after matching viable cell states and controlling sensory fatigue/drug side effects |
| H-M2 Morphological-flow memory | Transport pathways change their own future response after local history | Matched physical geometry causes equal performance regardless of prior training; carrier ablation does not change behavior |
| H-M3 Exchangeable relational memory | Temporary fusion/interaction transmits a learned ecological response | No transfer after re-separation or effect depends only on donor mass/salt carryover |
| H-M4 Evolved predictive regulation | Local ecological temporal correlations select a pre-emptive response scheme | Cue decoupling does not change phenotype over selection and matched naive organisms behave identically |
| H-M5 History-conditioned EERC (original) | Internally maintained history *plus* collective reproduction yields lineage-stable, individually plastic ecological regulation | No within-individual plasticity above fixed-reflex controls, or benefits depend entirely on installed external memory/copy API |

H-M5 is the **actual candidate new synthesis**; H-M1–4 are alternative scientific mechanisms, not proof that H-M5 exists.

## Preregistration design sketch (future and separately authorized ONLY)

Use the **same physical/computational substrate** with matched state capacity, resource inflow, selection, runtime and hidden probe. Compare:
- **S0 Reflex only:** no persistent history-carrying reaction state; match immediate sensors and response capacity.
- **S1 Passive hysteresis:** chemical/field equilibrium has persistence, but no inherited functional novelty or endogenous upkeep.
- **S2 Stateful organism:** candidate history arises as a side-effect of normal maintenance/interaction, with original copy/repair/routing rules frozen.
- **S3 State-ablation/rescue:** break one candidate carrier only, retaining sensory/motor function; restore carrier without adding reward.
- **S4 Environmental inversion:** history cue predicts the *opposite* future outcome in withheld conditions; test plastic adaptation of same continuing individual separately from later generations.
- **S5 Vertical daughter:** descendants isolated from trained environment, with lineage ancestry and functional state assays.
- **S6 Horizontal transfer:** donor/recipient exposure separated; then short/long contact, re-separation, sham mix and transferred-resource matched controls.
- **S7 Selection-withdrawal:** track extinction and retained function when external enrichment/re-pairing cues reduced.
- **S8 Shortcut positive control:** an externally installed memory array or rule is permitted as **false-positive detector**, not candidate endogenous evidence.

Define pre/post within-individual state and held-out functional benefit. Report **independently seeded worlds or lineages** (not thousands of observations of same cell) as replicate units. Fitness score/task reward must not have direct access to hidden tests. Freeze intervention timing, latency, stimulus dose, physiological equivalence, dropouts and cost ledger before observing outcomes.

## Anticipated negative results that still matter

- The same phenotype appears when donor material is directly mixed without integration, undermining learned information transfer.
- Only stereotyped stimulus-specific habituation arises; no history-conditioned transfer to a second unfamiliar ecological use.
- A designed bistable switch outperforms all evolvable chemistries at matched resource budgets (then no evidence that emergence helps).
- Within-cell 'learning' disappears when selection history is separated from current environment or when original genotypes are re-tested.
- Memory causes resource drag; populations with no historical storage outperform memory-bearing candidates under ecological change.
- After ecological support is removed, adaptation remains in an individual but functional collective reproduction fails.
- The original fixed two-state model and fractional-order model disagree because measurements are incommensurate; postpone mechanistic claims instead of choosing favorite.

## Why this is a distinct path from typical AI

The putative organism's 'memory' is an observed **property of causal material/interaction history**, not a text conversation database, scripted goals, preset reward model, externally copied 'brain', or a Transformer. Cognitive skills are eventual open scientific questions, and **their emergence is not guaranteed** by available cell-habituation findings. EERC-M measures a process that could be falsified, not a recipe that automatically creates awareness.

**Consent & safety:** no experiment, neural model, software generator, live digital ecology, or Ora/AgentTest change is authorized by this document. If later approved, use isolated, reversible, measured and rights-respecting experiments. If credible consciousness concerns arise, perform a separate welfare/ethics assessment instead of treating any new score as proof of experience.
