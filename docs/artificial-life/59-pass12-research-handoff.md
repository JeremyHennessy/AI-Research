## Key research distinctions

- **Within-life memory**: history-conditioned reversible response in the *same* individual after exposure, vs simple fatigue and passive time decay.
- **Intergenerational state**: a daughter inherits a changed physiological response after separation from experienced parent and original environment, not automatically DNA mutation.
- **Horizontal exchange**: cytoplasmic fusion passes a conditioned phenotype from a previously experienced cell to a naive one, but does not establish daughter-group heredity.
- **Physical trace**: network tube diameters embed a transport history and influence subsequent motion; this may be an ordinary dynamical state rather than internal modeling.
- **Evolved ecological anticipation**: population genetic selection can wire anticipatory responses across generations. This is distinct from individual learning and consciousness.
- **Original EERC-M**: hypothesized causal memory mechanisms arise as by-products of self-maintaining ecological organization. Requires separate nulls, resource budgets and lineage-tracked utility; no system shown to meet these combined conditions.
