# Pass 13 — associative protozoa, anticipation analogs and designer-installed cognitive shortcuts

**2026-10-08 | Critical primary-publication comparison; E1 for selected full/abstract methods, not independent reproduction.** All studies link to primary articles, versioned manuscripts or original authors' material. This document DOES NOT assert that a new learned ecological skill, predictive consciousness, or evolved digital mind exists.

## A. 2026 Stentor associative learning — promising, **unreviewed preprint**

**Source:** Nhi Doan, Austen Theroux, Tejas Ramdas, Samuel J. Gershman, *Associative learning in the protozoan Stentor coeruleus*, bioRxiv DOI [10.64898/2026.02.25.708045](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.02.25.708045). **v1 2026-02-25**, **v2 2026-09-10** explicitly says **new experiments, new data analysis and revised discussion**. [Version v2 primary preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.02.25.708045v2). Version changes matter: **do not use v1 numerical models to claim v2 replication**. Unreviewed/author-reported; v2 full figures/supplements/code not independently audited in AI-Research.

- Authors paired weak and strong **mechanical taps** to an individual ciliate, with strong tap following weak. They report **transiently enhanced contraction in response to the weak stimulus**, rather than just reduced response to repeated taps.
- Controls manipulated temporal stimulus contingency and included some mismatched/unpaired, weak-weak or altered-stimulus controls to distinguish **nonassociative sensitization/arousal** from contingency-related learning.
- Their time interval manipulations yielded a **nonclassical dependence** on inter-stimulus/inter-trial times. An aggregate model combines habituation and associative potentiation. As indexed in original v1 detailed methods, classification depends on a running-response threshold; authors tested sensitivity to thresholds. **Model-fitting is not unique identification of internal learning chemistry**.
- **Major methodological boundary:** weak vs strong are the **same sensory modality** and differ by intensity; temporal conditioning is externally designed. Response tested is short-lived same-modality **contraction**, not prediction of a new food source, novel spatial environment or multi-generational lineages.
- The authors' report of controls is valuable, but a skeptical independent replication with pre-registered *novel cue modality and ecological fitness endpoint* remains absent. It is **not** yet peer-reviewed evidence of a generally intelligent protist.
- Distinct investigator group from the Rajan/Marshall Stentor habituation publications, but same organism and broadly similar mechanical stimuli: an independent research *program*, not a replication of identical 2026 daughter-memory results.

**Most important candidate question:** can transiently learned cue contingency improve organism survival under a **held-out ecological reversal** when fixed sensitization and damage are controlled? No published proof in this study.

## B. Periodic Physarum anticipation — original 2008 demonstration but alternative models

**Source:** Toshiyuki Saigusa, Atsushi Tero, Ryo Kobayashi, Yoshiki Kuramoto (2008), *Amoebae Anticipate Periodic Events*, Physical Review Letters 100:018101, DOI [10.1103/PhysRevLett.100.018101](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.100.018101), [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18232821/). Only abstract and original author article sections screened in this pass → E1.

- Under three periodical unfavorable temperature/humidity episodes, *Physarum* slowed locomotion during episodes and sometimes **spontaneously slowed** near the next expected episode even when conditions remained favorable.
- Authors proposed collective oscillatory dynamics and simulated different frequencies. The demonstration is **anticipatory timing under imposed periodic physics**, not learned causal knowledge, new adaptive niche or a transfer into daughters.
- An independently published **mechanical/periodic oscillator** could recreate similar phases without mental prediction. Without testing new irregular frequencies, reset/rand-period controls and blind challenge, cannot distinguish "smart agent" from resonant/hysteretic dynamics.

## C. Two independent theoretical counter-explanations (NOT living organism experiments)

1. **Bruna & Gyllingberg 2025**, *Cognition without neurons: modelling anticipation in a basal reservoir computer*, [arXiv:2505.02114](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02114), E1 author preprint abstract. A **preconstructed** hexagonal allostatic energy network with no neurons or trained readout produces periodic pattern completion after periodic inputs. Its network physics, graph size, stimulus choice and regulation laws are investigator-designed. No biological lineage, spontanously evolved energy circuit or organism self-maintenance demonstrated. Success would illustrate **memory in dynamics** and emphasize **easy false-positive 'intelligence' claims**.
2. **Solé & Pla-Mauri 2025**, *Cognition as least action: the Physarum Lagrangian*, [arXiv:2511.08531](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08531), E1 preprint abstract and publicly indexed figure descriptions. Adaptive graph conductance paths follow a variational trade-off minimizing dissipative transport under **predefined source/sink boundary conditions**. The physics can yield apparently goal directed paths, without a learned symbolic map or awareness. Not an independent biological experiment.

**Supplementary skeptical comparator:** Andrew Adamatzky (2017), *Maze solvers demystified and some other thoughts*, [arXiv:1712.04681](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.04681), E1 primary preprint summary. Several "maze-solving" physical systems simply follow **diffusion gradient or least resistance**, so a clever visual output cannot itself prove remembering a novel task. No evidence in this review that it directly falsifies Reid et al. 2012 controlled **slime trail** effect; that study's masking/geometry/chemotaxis controls remain stronger for a specific route-memory claim.

## D. Engineered cellular learning circuits can mimic adaptive responses

**Source:** Jordi Pla-Mauri & Ricard Solé (2026), *Engineering Basal Cognition: Minimal Genetic Circuits for Habituation, Sensitization, and Massed–Spaced Learning*, ACS Synthetic Biology 15(2):716–727, DOI [10.1021/acssynbio.5c00766](https://doi.org/10.1021/acssynbio.5c00766). [Public original journal and methods summary](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acssynbio.5c00766). **E1** indexed source and sections, full supplementary PDF and versioned code not reproduced in this pass.

- Authors **designed and simulated** genetic circuits with repressors, activators, fluorescent reporters and quorum molecules that can express **habituation**, **sensitization**, and **massed–spaced** dynamical differences under external stimulus schedules.
- Their own abstract treats them as designs **amenable to experimental realization**, not newly generated, independently self-maintaining wet-lab organisms nor evidence of evolved novel consciousness. An open Zenodo source-code archive is cited, but was not executed or license-verified by AI-Research.
- Their circuits are intentionally designed to reproduce a chosen functional readout. In EERC, this must be used as a **positive control showing a designer shortcut can fake success**, not as evidence the same mechanisms evolved without guidance.
- Distinguish time courses that *look* like learning from evidence of adaptive benefit under unseen ecological conditions, endogenous construction, energy cost, and lineage survival.

## E. Practical anti-overclaim decision matrix

| Claim | Strongest published signal | Cheapest meaningful null | Unverified leap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weak tap acquires learned predictive value | Paired vs unpaired Stentor preprint | New sensory modality, backward/blocked correlation and response integrity under matched tapping | New ecological or general intelligence |
| Organism anticipates cycle | Physarum periodic slowdown | Equal frequency distribution but randomized timing; oscillator-only model | Conceptual future representation |
| Internal dynamics stores history | Minimal allostatic hex-grid reservoir | Phase-locked passive oscillator at same state/compute budget | Evolved memory mechanism |
| Externally built genetic circuit learns | 2026 ACS simulations | Preinstalled vs emergently evolved circuit, resource-matched held-out novel targets | A digital organism spontaneously created its own brain |
| Physarum navigates new obstacle | [2012 full audit](60-pass13-externalized-navigation-memory-2012-full-review.md) | Inert coating, own vs third-party marks, timed food refresh | Task-general spatial cognition |
| Individual remembers while daughter resets | [2022/2023 full audit](61-pass13-whi3-memory-barrier-2022-full-review.md) | Gate deletion/rescue and descendant ecological/mating success | Adaptive self-chosen memory inheritance |

## Research priority

**Do not prematurely fuse an autoregressive LLM, an evolved slime mold, a designed mnemomic gene circuit and a biochemical reaction network.** One coherent scientific hypothesis is instead: **which interactions can create useful, inspectable ecological traces, and which systems can alter reading, erasing and transmitting those traces under truly unseen conditions?** That is evaluated in [new EERC-T synthesis](64-pass13-eerc-external-trace-selective-inheritance.md), not experimentally implemented.
