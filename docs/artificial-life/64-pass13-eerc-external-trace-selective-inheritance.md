# Pass 13 — New combined path: EERC-T, ecological traces and selective memory transmission

**2026-10-08 | Original UNVERIFIED hypothesis.** This is an extension to [EERC-01 v1](../../data/alife/combined-path-eerc-v1.json) and [EERC-02 v2](../../data/alife/combined-path-eerc-v2.json), preserving both original documents unchanged. It does not implement an artificial world, learner, organism, training schedule or consciousness test. No changes to Ora, AgentTest or any other production repo.

## New question

**Can an evolving organization create a reliable informational trace in its physical/ecological world, interpret that trace to adapt in a new situation, and selectively retain or transmit its learned state—without a developer supplying a memory API, task reward, or special reproductive individual?**

This asks for a potential *causal bridge* between three independently studied properties:

- **E1 Externalized trace** (Reid et al. 2012): deposited material can causally influence navigating an unanticipated obstacle; these trails are pre-existing biological traits and do not prove their evolutionary origin.
- **E2 Selective daughter partition** (Lau et al. 2022; Akhtar et al. 2023): yeast Whi3 state can be confined mostly to an experienced mother, with measured leaky first-daughter passage; experimenter-disabled barriers spread an altered phenotype.
- **E3 Temporally conditional adaptation** (Doan et al. 2026 bioRxiv, E1 *paper evidence*): weak/strong timed tap pairing reportedly produces transitory enhanced response under trained contingencies; its peer-reviewed validation and ecological generalization remain absent.

**These are three different species/physical systems, NOT a published integrated successful architecture.** Merging their motifs may fail completely. EERC-T is an *original candidate testable synthesis*.

## Why more and more memory could be maladaptive

1. **Noise and deception:** false or third-party environmental markers might drive a forager away from actual food.
2. **Resource renewal:** an explored location may become nutrient-rich after a delay; a rigid 'avoid visited' rule becomes harmful.
3. **Daughter opportunity:** a mother's prior failed mating episode may not predict daughter's future; duplicating the mother's refusal state can harm her descendants.
4. **Replication burden:** persistent marks, repaired sensor proteins, protected chemical states, and copying/transport consume material/energy/time.
5. **Contagion:** a self-propagating memory state may spread into populations irrespective of current ecology, making memory beneficial in one time slice and deleterious at another.
6. **Cheap physics baseline:** an ordinary diffusion gradient, damped oscillator or hand-designed bistable circuit can perform well enough that there is no benefit from evolving a distinct memory mechanism.

**Original hypothesis EERC-T1:** under changing environments, a system that can modulate *whether to trust external traces and whether to pass internal state to descendants* might outperform perfect-retention and perfect-erase controls, at matched budgets, when both trace accuracy and future ecology shift.

**Critical falsifier:** if permanent marking plus an inherited fixed response rule performs as well as or better than a history-conditioned, endogenously maintained gate on all held-out contexts, then this particular adaptive memory-gate advantage is rejected.

## Distinct research axes — cannot collapse into one 'intelligence' score

| Axis | Mechanistic evidence required | False success/shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| **Trace authorship** | An inhabitant produces a physically conserved external modification from accounted resources | Developer writes a globally accessible visited-cell array |
| **Trace reliability** | Conditioned response discriminates usable/costly traces in novel environments | Always avoid a specific residue independent of truth |
| **Temporal history** | Same individual/genotype with identical current cues but different prior contingencies responds differently after washout | Population mutant selection or sensory fatigue |
| **Controlled partition** | Causal gate changes mother, daughter and horizontal transfer without changing other resource/motor functions | Engine copies entire organization each update |
| **Adaptive daughter outcomes** | Daughters' independently measured new-function success and grandchildren differ under transmission interventions | Mere inherited phenotype with no fitness relevance |
| **Novel condition** | Performance on previously sealed resource renewal, obstacle and partner contingency, not exposed in selection reward | Optimizer knows hidden condition score |
| **Resource burden** | Full memory construction, maintenance, forgetting, copying and readout costs measured | Unlimited/free global memory |
| **Mechanistic causality** | Knockout/rescue of mark or gate with matched substrate; alternative explanation controls | Post-hoc entropy/novelty metric or visually convincing maze path |

## A formal conceptual separation (NOT executable algorithm)

For a process at t, describe (i) evolving internal organization **X_t**, (ii) environment **E_t**, (iii) externally produced trace **Z_t**, (iv) internal history carrier **H_t**, and (v) parent–daughter causal relation **G_t**. Each is a measurement, not a preinstalled privileged 'organism object'.

Hypothesis: within fixed ecological laws F, an internal process may alter its local world and its own later response:

- Environmental accounting: \`E(t+1), Z(t+1) = F_env(E(t), Z(t), processes, finite resources)\`.
- History/readout: \`H(t+1) = F_local(H(t), current signals, physical maintenance)\`.
- Daughter passage: \`G(parent,daughter)\` specifies actually transferred material, relations, functions, and origin of any gating *after measurement*, not as an automatic simulator's copy API.
- Ecological effect: \`Delta(T) = performance(intact organism-produced trace and gate; novel test T) - performance(counterfactual trace/gate ablation; budget matched)\`.
- Measure the sign of Delta under **environment reversals** and include all extinct and censored lineages.

If researchers *preprogram F_local to remember* and *preprogram the gate* (e.g. an explicit parent-copy instruction), they may still make a useful comparative baseline, but **must not claim those features emerged themselves**.

## Future-only preregistration menu (choose ONE contrast at a time)

**First informative comparison, no need for an AI model:**
- T0 **no-trace**: fixed physics and finite material budget, trace deposition suppressed but resource costs matched.
- T1 **trace-exists**: local deposited mark; response genetically/reflexively fixed.
- T2 **trace masked**: same physical residue, information unavailable; inert viscosity/gradient matched.
- T3 **false-trace**: misleading third-party mark vs useful true marker, precommitted probabilities.
- T4 **resource refreshed**: previous visited location gains nutrition; compare ability to stop avoiding older trace.
- T5 **gate-off/inherited all**: candidate internal state is copied to every descendant (positive false-success control).
- T6 **gate-on selective**: potential state-dependent transmission (if it arises; record whether preinstalled).
- T7 **erased state**: no inherited memory plus same genetics and environment.
- T8 **oscillator/bistable-circuit engineered**: manually designed temporal response with the same compute/resource burden (strong cheap competitor).
- T9 **blind new geometry/ecology**: U obstacle rotated or extended, cue contingencies inverted and partner changes unseen in selection; no feedback of held-out scores.

**Prioritization by information:** explicitly **do not start with T6** until research identifies whether any substrate can give rise to an informative trace in a fixed world. First establish the T1-T4 ecological-trace causal chain and compare T0/T8; then examine inheritance only when daughter reproduction is independently verified. A single mechanism at a time; no forced combined system.

**Scientific unit:** independent world/lineage for any future computational evolution; a frame, cell nucleus, daughter from same mother, clone isolate or simulation-timestep is NOT an independent evolutionary replicate. Record all extinctions, partial failures and negative ecological effects. A separate regulator for consciousness cannot be inferred from all scores becoming positive.

## An honest measure of 'new path' success

A meaningful future EERC-T result would require observable adaptive function absent in ancestors and absent from externally installed controls, tracked through ecological change, with:
1. internally **generated** usable external mark;
2. energy/resource **cost** and counterfactual benefit measured;
3. evolved or at least causally established **state reliability/gating** beyond a designer's fixed rule;
4. retained function across independent new-world challenges, plus possibly descendant transfer;
5. every preinstalled operation documented, including whether feedback, identity and copying are developer-defined;
6. negative runs counted.

**It would still NOT prove consciousness, conscience, subjective suffering, autonomous personhood, or unlimited open-endedness.**

## Competing hypotheses we should preserve

- **Physics-only:** no 'learning' needed, gradients/transport explain successful routes.
- **Pre-existing biological reflex:** Physarum has innate slime-aversion; novel ecology only makes a fixed trait advantageous.
- **Population selection:** variant survival changes population means rather than a single individual updating state.
- **Resource-cost dominance:** erasing memories increases fitness in unstable worlds; no adaptive gating added value.
- **External-scaffold dependence:** fixed group compartments and automatic reproduction, not true higher-level unit.
- **Historical contingency:** only founder genotypes with special inherited response primitives can exploit traces.
- **Model overfitting:** optimization to maze shape or stop-time choice creates artifact gains that disappear on sealed challenges.

## Main decision

Pass 13 recommends **scientific criteria and readings, not implementation**. The EERC-T hypothesis broadens the search beyond conventional AI and beyond cognition-as-spectacle by treating ecological information as part of the organism–world relationship. The approach remains a candidate to falsify, with three separate substrate families [chemistry, program interactions and conservative flow fields] preserved from EERC v1, *not* a newly selected winning architecture.
