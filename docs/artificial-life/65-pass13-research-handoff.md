## New scientific combined path, without forcing an AI architecture

**Question:** Might an evolving self-maintaining process modify its environment with information-bearing traces, make reliable choices when those traces become misleading, and selectively pass some history to descendants?

This is NOT a claim an LLM, software model, microbial organism, conscious experience or substrate-winning architecture was created. It is a *newly documented falsifiable synthesis* of **different** biology and theory papers. Unlike an implanted memory database, an endogenous environmental trace must be produced under fixed conservation constraints and its use be causally shown under blind ecological conditions.

**Critical nulls:** fixed chemoattraction, inert mark, universal slime avoidance, false third-party trail, resource renewal reversal, physical gradient/least resistance, readout habituation/sensitization, developer-installed genetics and auto-copy reproduction. Daughter interventions must control for septin/cell-cycle impairment and be scored by independent lineage fitness, **not fluorescent aggregate presence alone**.
