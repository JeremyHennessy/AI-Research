## Scientific opening

Four fully examined primary sources establish distinct parts of the problem:

- [Lambert & Kussell 2014](66-pass14-lambert-kussell-2014-timescale-memory-full-review.md): glucose/lactose shifts produce *temporary* protein carry-over and continued gene expression; lag is reduced after previous exposure, yet storing unused protein carries a cost.
- [Abreu et al. 2024](67-pass14-abreu-2024-environmental-fitness-reversal-full-review.md): yeast mutant fitness under condition A can depend substantially on which condition preceded A, and overall fitness may conceal large opposing effects.
- [Wolf et al. 2008](68-pass14-wolf-2008-microbial-information-memory-full-review.md): current bacterial reporter dynamics reveal past media/density states in information bits; authors did **not** prove such information is an evolved survival strategy.
- [Yi et al. 2000](69-pass14-chemotaxis-integral-feedback-2000-full-review.md): a **fixed pre-existing integral feedback circuit** can yield seemingly clever history-sensitive adaptation without newly learned strategy.

Together they justify **investigating**, but not asserting, a new route to adaptive intelligence.

## New hypothesis EERC-R1

**An evolving ecological organization may generate and preserve useful physical traces of its own history and evolve their *retention/erasure relationship to environmental reliability*, without any developer-written policy, dedicated memory API, human fitness target or automatic group-copy primitive.**

The word *may* matters. A passive biochemical relaxation, a genetically fixed feedback integrator or a designed stochastic-switching population may suffice and defeat the hypothesis. The newness lies in an **integrated falsifiable causal comparison**, not in claiming these pieces were never previously studied.

### Five separate requirements

| Property | Evidence required | Critical false signal |
|---|---|---|
| H — history information | Distinct pasts under identical current cues yield distinguishable internal/interaction state | Reporter carries history but state is useless |
| U — utility | Carrier-specific intervention affects performance on a **previously unseen** ecological task | Output label improves but resource use/survival does not |
| R — reliability modulation | Functional response changes after unexpected cue–resource relationship **reverses** | External rule already calculates a fixed 'forget' parameter |
| O — endogenous origin | The causal carrier/retention process developed from ordinary substrate resources/mutations | Hardcoded memory class or reward is installed in simulator |
| T — lineage transmission | Daughters/independent granddaughters inherit **functional** control only when ecological circumstances favor it | Model copies all state each birth, no causal independent daughters |

**No property by itself establishes organismhood, intelligence, conscience or subjective experience.** Do not merge them into a single aliveness score.

## A measurement framework — NOT source code

Record observation-only objects:

- **E(t)**: current environmental input and researcher-known cue/resource reliability, including false traces, renewals and unexpected permanent shifts.
- **X(t)**: measurable intracellular or relational state and physical source of its persistence (state need not be a database).
- **C(t)**: full resource/time/compute cost of writing, preserving, transferring and erasing X.
- **Y(t)**: functionally relevant behavior and **viability/offspring**, including extinct and censored lineages.
- **G(t)**: parent–daughter causality, independent daughter and grandchild performance with ancestor identity.

A descriptive history score **I(past;X)** quantifies evidence of trace information. A separate *counterfactual* benefit **ΔY = Y(intact carrier) − Y(selective carrier knockout)** under matched ecology and plausible damage controls is required before calling it adaptive.

When an environment alternates A and B, compare **A→A**, **A→B**, **B→B**, **B→A**, *A after unexpectedly long B*, *B after environmental cue inversion*; **do not average away fitness reversals**. The researcher may select physics and observation windows, but may not secretly feed the answer or held-out score back into evolutionary dynamics.

### Prospective null and challenge arms (entirely future-only)

1. **No-history / resource-matched:** same chemistry/observation budget with no persistence.
2. **Permanent state:** constitutively prepared, paying expression/store costs even after resource disappearance.
3. **Fixed short/long decay:** two preinstalled time constants; **reference comparators**, never emergent winners.
4. **Engineered feedback integrator:** Yi-like built-in controller with matched sensing/energy cost.
5. **Stochastic phenotype switch:** slow and fast switching as Acar-like alternatives, with no adaptive controller.
6. **Unassigned chemistry:** ordinary resource-limited components may carry physical history as a consequence of interaction, **without privileged memory API**.
7. **Causal memory knockout/rescue:** remove candidate process while matching general sensor, growth and death effects.
8. **Unseen reliability reversal:** previously informative trace becomes misleading, or a resource returns to a marked/exhausted area.
9. **Permanent shift:** previously advantageous state becomes consistently costly; count failure and residual resource loss.
10. **Independent offspring test:** compare full-copy/zero-copy/mixed or physically emergent transmission, matched daughter viability and lineage costs.

**Order of implementation if *separately* authorized:** first compare one physical memory mechanism with no-memory and cheap fixed control. Only if it produces an uninstalled positive and withstands intervention should later work test evolving erasure or offspring transmission. No forced all-in-one software design.

## Falsification and likely failures

The EERC-R claim fails for a given substrate if a preinstalled fixed feedback rule, passive metabolite retention or stochastic phenotype diversification entirely explains future benefit; if novel state only correlates with *past* but does not affect future viability; if any advantage vanishes when memory storage energy is charged; if performance collapses under altered cue statistics; or if group reproduction only succeeds through externally installed copy/sort operations.

Negative findings remain permanent scientific data, not defects to hide with architectural changes.

## What to read next

Highest-value follow-up is a **genuine causal intervention on retention cost/decay** followed by fitness comparison under both *frequent reversals* and a *surprising sustained shift*. The existing 2008 Acar, 2014 Lambert, 2024 Abreu and 2025 Jain sources cover complementary slices, but they do not demonstrate the full EERC-R chain.

The long-range research goal includes a possible living and intelligent digital organization, but **this is only a research hypothesis** and does not establish any route to subjective consciousness or normative conscience.
