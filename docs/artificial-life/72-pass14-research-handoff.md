## New original scientific theory, NOT a settled discovery

**Hypothesis:** under some physically finite and resource-accounted ecological environments, a lineage may evolve a usable *cost-effective retention/erasure relationship* that generalizes when past cues become unreliable. The mechanism must arise from ordinary substrate interactions and heredity, not from a prewritten memory API, explicit goal reward, external copy function or hand-tuned decay rule.

**Four independent lines of evidence must not be conflated:** detecting a history state, producing adaptive benefit under **novel** resource conditions, establishing causal necessity and material costs, and inheriting the functional relationship through **independent daughters and grandchildren**. None of these constitutes a direct digital consciousness test.

**Decisive prospective nulls:** permanent expressor, zero memory, passive molecular carryover, preset methylation integrator, stochastic fixed fast/slow switch, cue inverted mid-run, resource permanently withdrawn/renewed, genuine within-life state selection versus population genotype sorting, carrier knockout/rescue, time-matched compute/energy and all extinction outcomes.
