# Pass 15 — Evolution of switching versus individual physiological memory: competing historical experiments

**2026-10-08 | E1 publisher original abstracts and selected main method/figure text only; NO full supplemental-method certification or independent replication.** This report uses antibiotic-associated studies as **published examples of selection and time-scale dependence**, not as actionable instructions to generate drug resistance/tolerance or run biological experiments.

## Four separate but easily confused claims

### Beaumont and colleagues 2009 — switching *originated* through population evolution

Hubertus J. E. Beaumont, Jenna Gallie, Christian Kost, Gayle C. Ferguson, Paul B. Rainey, *Experimental evolution of bet hedging*, *Nature* 462:90–93 (2009), DOI [10.1038/nature08504](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08504). **E1** publicly accessible publisher/PubMed abstract, main figures/metadata and author-linked full-paper PDF lead identified but full main methods not audited here.

A research-imposed regime repeatedly reversed which *Pseudomonas fluorescens* colony states had future reproductive opportunities. Only **2 of 12 independent evolving populations** developed heritable high-rate stochastic phenotype switching; most instead followed different mutational/state paths. Genetic follow-up in one lineage found historical contingency: the final switch-associated mutation's **adaptive effect depended on the earlier evolutionary background**. This is stronger than engineered fast/slow switchers for the **origin** of a switching phenotype, but **does not** prove each individual predicts or learns ecological statistics in its own lifetime.

**Independence caution:** [Gallie et al. 2015 E2](75-pass15-gallie-2015-latent-bistability-and-evolution-full-review.md) is a *molecular mechanism follow-up on a Beaumont switching lineage* with new biochemical assays. Treat distinct **causal interventions** as such, but do **not** count the original switching event twice.

### Acar and colleagues 2008 — researchers *installed* different switching rates

Murat Acar, Jerome T. Mettetal, Alexander van Oudenaarden, *Stochastic switching as a survival strategy in fluctuating environments*, *Nature Genetics* 40:471–475 (2008), DOI [10.1038/ng.110](https://doi.org/10.1038/ng.110). This study is **already catalogued in Pass 14 at E1**, do **not** add another entry. Researchers engineered switching galactose-utilization phenotypes in yeast, then imposed rapid versus slow environmental alternations. Relative population fitness reversed. Engineered response rates are **not independently evolved adaptive memory policies**. Acar provides a crucial engineering counterfactual to Beaumont's heritable *origin* experiment, not an independent Pseudomonas switching event.

### Fridman and colleagues 2014 — evolution of latency timed to stress exposure

Ofer Fridman, Amir Goldberg, Irine Ronin, Noam Shoresh, Nathalie Q. Balaban, *Optimization of lag time underlies antibiotic tolerance in evolved bacterial populations*, *Nature* 513:418–421 (2014), DOI [10.1038/nature13469](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature13469). **E1** primary publisher abstract, PubMed and published high-level figure descriptions; complete source/appendix not audited. The paper reports that *E. coli* **genetic variants** selected over generations displayed a delayed regrowth distribution calibrated to past periods of antibiotic exposure, without immediately increasing ordinary antibiotic resistance. Genome comparison and genetic restoration supported genotype-level causal contributions.

**Critical scientific separation:** A distribution of descendants delaying growth is a population **bet-hedging/tolerance** trait, not proof that one bacterium learned a new drug schedule. Study interventions were designed and applied by scientists. No use of the original drug selection procedure or resistance optimization is proposed here.

### Van den Bergh and colleagues 2016 — strategy can be slow to *reverse*

Bram Van den Bergh and collaborators, *Frequency of antibiotic application drives rapid evolutionary adaptation of Escherichia coli persistence*, *Nature Microbiology* 1:16020 (2016), DOI [10.1038/nmicrobiol.2016.20](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmicrobiol.2016.20), E1 primary abstract and figure synopsis. Authors found persistence frequency evolved rapidly during repeat treatment, while reducing persistence after drug exposure ceased was **much slower and incomplete** under studied conditions.

This is directly relevant as a **counterexample** to the belief that a high-scoring memory or state switch can be automatically reversed when environmental conditions change. But it is **evolutionary reversal across generations**, not demonstrated individual cognitive erasure. Treatment frequency and persistence are specialized biological conditions; generalize only as a scientific analogy.

### An additional negative control: assay-dependent awakening

A further original observational source, [The frequency of persisters in Escherichia coli reflects the kinetics of awakening from dormancy](https://doi.org/10.1128/JB.00056-10), *Journal of Bacteriology* 2010, PubMed abstract/E1, reports that identical stationary cultures can produce **different apparent persister fractions** depending on the fresh medium used to measure awakening. That warns even the **measurement** of a 'memory fraction' may depend on assay timing and environmental context. Authors/original full method comparison and denominators are **pending**; do not inflate E2 catalog without whole-paper review.

## Research methods accounting

| Result category | What changes | Genuine independent unit | What it does NOT prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acar 2008 | Engineered phenotypic switching rate | Independent yeast culture competition | Endogenous origin of switch regulation |
| Beaumont 2009 | Inherited stochastic-switching phenotype evolved | 12 independent original selection populations, 2 successes | New within-lifetime learning or unconstrained novelty |
| Gallie 2015 | Existing 2009 lineage biochemical mechanism reconstructed | New molecular assays nested in historical lineage | A second independent evolution of that initial switch |
| Fridman 2014 | Genetic selection for lag-time distributions | Original treatment-selected bacteria lines (complete denominators pending) | Conscious foresight or generic transfer across environments |
| Van den Bergh 2016 | Persistence frequency evolved with treatment regime | Original bacterial evolution groups (exact full methods pending) | Fast adaptation of a fixed individual controller |
| Awakening 2010 | Assay medium and time alter apparent dormant fraction | Dormant source culture × measurement context | Universal fixed memory fraction |

The literature supports **multiple evolutionary ways of coping with change**, but **no continuous sequence** from stochastic switching → cognitive generalization → higher-level organism → consciousness has been demonstrated.

**Future research boundary:** focus on mathematically and ecologically harmless abstract memory/dormancy trade-offs in separately approved digital scientific studies, not operational drug-tolerance selection. No biological protocol, lab run, organism, digital simulation, or production system was modified.
