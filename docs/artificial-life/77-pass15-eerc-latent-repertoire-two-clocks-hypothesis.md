## Gap exposed by Pass 15

Our previous EERC memory papers mainly ask **how long a state persists** and whether earlier ecological cues remain useful. New source comparisons instead expose a different question:

**How can evolution obtain a new usable dynamical behavior from an existing finite network, and how does selection across many generations interact with the physical adaptation of one individual within a lifetime?**

Three separate evidence levels:
- [Beaumont 2009](76-pass15-stochastic-hedging-and-antibiotic-history-comparators.md) reports new **stochastic switching** in only two of twelve independently evolving bacterial populations. Genotype-level history matters.
- [Gallie et al. 2015](75-pass15-gallie-2015-latent-bistability-and-evolution-full-review.md) finds a later inherited perturbation exposed a **preexisting metabolic decision pathway** that can sustain two phenotypic states, with a resource/growth cost. Equivalent genetics in another species did **not** reproduce the phenotype.
- [Kratz et al. 2026](74-pass15-kratz-2026-multiscale-bacterial-memory-full-review.md) reports minutes-to-hours nutrient pulse history shaping growth-rate adaptation; the proposed distribution of ribosomal dynamics and adaptive gating is **partly model-construction**, not observed de novo genetic emergence of a memory code.
- [Basan et al. 2020](73-pass15-basan-2020-growth-lag-metabolic-tradeoff-full-review.md) directly manipulates metabolic readiness, changing later lag while altering other resource objectives. Pre-existing pathways and externally chosen nutrients are essential.
- [Fridman 2014 and Van den Bergh 2016](76-pass15-stochastic-hedging-and-antibiotic-history-comparators.md) document evolved population lag and persistence strategies under specialized antibiotic selection; these are **not** within-lifetime learning, and rapid selection can have slow reversal.

No primary source shows all these properties arising together from simple digital chemistry, let alone producing conscious life.

## The new candidate hypothesis — EERC-L

**In some finite resource-conserving ecologies, a population of interacting processes may acquire a new heritable *control over the accessibility of latent dynamical states* through evolutionary modification of existing interaction/repair pathways. Once accessible, those states may provide different within-individual physical response times to changing conditions, without any installed memory or mutation rewards.**

What is new here is the **specific multi-level causal comparison**, not the claim that no one has studied bistability, evolutionary adaptation or distributed systems.

The distinction:

- **Clock 1 — within-life:** the continuing system changes its state and local response during exposure on physical relaxation times `τ_1, τ_2, …`. Memory is detectable only if different pasts change current states under identical current conditions.
- **Clock 2 — across generations:** heritable changes shift **which state transitions are possible**, their probabilities/thresholds, and their carrying cost. This could expose a useful previously latent behavior.
- **Ecological bridge:** selection need not reward an experimenter's intelligence task. A naturally produced opportunity or cost (resource exhaustion, collaborator by-product, return after deprivation) can affect which variants reproduce.
- **Potential higher-level integration:** only if organizations produce **independent daughters that inherit the functional relationship** and remain viable after externally supported sorting/auto-copying is reduced does this inform evolving higher-level individuality.

**No generically optimal memory rate is presumed**, and the physical substrate always contains fixed foundational laws. Emerging functionality means new *reachable combinations and ecological uses* within those laws.

## Observational tests that cannot be collapsed into one life/intelligence badge

| Dimension | Required measurement | False positive |
|---|---|---|
| Preexisting latent repertoire | Show reaction/interaction possibilities before the claimed evolutionary innovation, and record what was initially inaccessible | Researcher adds a new opcode/reaction/resource in the middle |
| Heritable accessibility shift | Compare ancestor, evolved variant, causal reversion/rescue of altered interaction without changing substrate physics | Daughter receives copied full state through a privileged engine API |
| Within-life responsiveness | Same genotype + identical present cues, distinct randomized history → distinct future response with washout | Allele sorting across a population or instrument averaging |
| Spectral flexibility | Multiple measurable relaxation/transition times change under context | Programmer supplies a fractional-order kernel and calls it emergent |
| Ecological function | New use improves held-out material access or independent survival when natural opportunity arises | High entropy, visually compelling phenotype, test score embedded into world |
| Cost/fitness reversal | State switch carries measured material/time/compute price and can be harmful after a reversed regime | Free memory or always-safe dormant state |
| Generalization and reproducibility | Multiple independently seeded lineage histories, blind ecological challenges, negative outcomes and confidence intervals | 30,000 correlated cell frames counted as independent evolving worlds |
| Functional inheritance | Causally reproduced daughters/grandchildren share a group-level function, not just genotype or image shape | Instant group cloning performed by an external simulator |

## Competing models — do not force one winner

1. **Passive relaxation hypothesis**: ordinary physical chemistry and available resources explain all observed behavior; no evolution of a new control interface is necessary.
2. **Genetic sorting only**: heritable changes cause population shifts but the *same* individual cannot update a useful strategy based on personal experience.
3. **Preinstalled controller**: a fixed feedback integrator, bistable gene circuit, stochastic bet hedge or explicitly provided memory kernel replicates all performance at matched cost.
4. **Context specificity**: evolution uncovers a switch only because a particular initialized program/chemical pathway contains a latent attractor; it fails under independent starting conditions or transferred substrate.
5. **Higher-level false positive**: coexisting stateful components never produce independently self-maintained descendant organizations.
6. **EERC-L hypothesis**: a new **evolvable access interface** emerges and causally modulates a useful within-life response repertoire at constrained cost.

The winner must be determined by matched experiments, not by aesthetic preference for 'living AI'.

## Paper-only future experiment ordering (unimplemented)

**Don't build a full artificial life world and then chase a novelty score.** In a separately authorized sandbox, choose *one* minimal substrate and pre-register:

- Fixed starting physical laws/resource conservation and complete installed-primitive inventory; matched equal budget throughout.
- Unmodified ancestral populations, passive chemistry controls and a **researcher-installed switch** comparator intentionally labeled as a *shortcut positive control*.
- A known latent bistable or distributed-timescale possibility **without a prewritten evolutionary target**; observe whether spontaneous heritable variation actually makes it reachable.
- Mechanistic mutation/reversion tests, matched resource/feedback ablation, direct state transition readouts **in the same continuing individual**, and fully traced descendants.
- Encounter frequency held out, unexpected **permanent** resource reversal, selection removal, false external marks, new collaborators or resources. Do not leak the held-out scorer to the dynamics.
- A full loss account (energy, time, storage, copying, death) and all extinction failures.

**Reject** claims for a substrate if an installed feedback filter is sufficient, if only evolutionary genotype sorting occurs, if switching never causally affects external ecological success, if the effect disappears under changed conditions, or if daughters exist only because an automatic group copy instruction was provided.

## Why this could matter for a path beyond LLMs

This theory does **not** choose a Transformer, language memory store, neural network, cell automaton, artificial chemistry or digital brain as final architecture. It asks whether the first useful computational building blocks can be **emergently recruited** from a process' ordinary resource economy and later supported by lineage selection. Their capacity to support intelligent behavior is **unknown**. A language model is not ruled out as future tooling, but none is required to formulate or test the present scientific question.

**Consciousness remains separate**: even positive function/lineage tests would not prove subjective experience or a moral conscience.
