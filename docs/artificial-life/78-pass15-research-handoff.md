## Most important scientific distinction

- **Evolution clock (many generations):** heritable changes in available component interactions can expose latent transitions/bistability already possible under fixed biological/physical rules.
- **Individual clock (minutes–hours):** a continuing cell can respond to recent environmental history through already-existing protein/molecular relaxation, without genetic change during each switch.
- **Ecological bridge (conditional and unproven in digital life):** if such heritable access modifies organism-level future fitness at genuine material/energy cost, then selection could preserve it. None of our sources demonstrates all three evolving spontaneously in a digital chemistry, nor emerging higher-level daughter reproduction or consciousness.

**Avoid the deceptive “RNN organism” claim:** mathematical equivalence between a fixed physiological memory kernel and a recurrent network is an explanation/computational analogy, not evidence that bacteria are trained LLMs or that artificial life has been produced.

**High-value negative evidence** includes two-out-of-twelve switch origins, dependence on ancestral genetic context, failure of one analogous switch in E. coli, delayed nutrient recovery despite fast growth, an empirically unconfirmed candidate ribosome-subsector model, incomplete persistence reversal, and assay-dependent dormant classification.
