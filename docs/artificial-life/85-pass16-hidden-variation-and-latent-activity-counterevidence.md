# Pass 16 — Competing mechanisms of 'hidden' novelty, functional insufficiency and selection dependence

**2026-10-08 | Critical E1 primary abstract and selected-method research.** Not complete paper E2, not independent scientific reproduction. Two additions to canonical catalog are E1: **Zheng et al. 2019** and **Copley et al. 2022** (review).

## Zheng, Payne & Wagner (2019) — cryptic variation can change accessible adaptive peaks

Primary: Jia Zheng, Joshua L. Payne, Andreas Wagner, *Cryptic genetic variation accelerates evolution by opening access to diverse adaptive peaks*. Science **365**:347–353 (2019), DOI [10.1126/science.aax1837](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aax1837). [Original PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31346060/), copyright AAAS; full main paper/method appendix not independently audited.

Authors used a **human-chosen protein fluorescence phenotype**—yellow-to-green fluorescence—and prepared populations carrying different prior neutral/cryptic variants. Under researcher-directed laboratory evolution and performance measurement, previously diverse populations accessed **more varied and higher-performing green fluorescent protein variants**, while less diverse pools converged on narrower routes. This supports context-specific access changes through epistasis, **not** spontaneous origin of organic reproduction or human-like cognition.

**Distinctions:** a fluorescent color switch is an externally scored property, not an ecological niche invented by the protein. Experimentally added variation and researcher-run mutation/selection govern the accessible paths. Different engineered candidate genotypes are not independently reproducing multi-component organisms. Do not extrapolate to arbitrary self-sustaining digital ecologies without parent–descendant and resource accounting.

## Copley, Newton & Widney (2022) — a promiscuous activity can exist but be too weak to matter

Primary: Shelley D. Copley, Matilda S. Newton, Karl A. Widney, *How to recruit a promiscuous enzyme to serve a new function*. Biochemistry 62(2):300–308 (2023 issue, published online 2022), DOI [10.1021/acs.biochem.2c00249](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.biochem.2c00249), [public accepted original review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9881647/). **E1 contextual paper/review**; original underlying enzyme experiments from many laboratories not each independently audited here.

Across a curated survey of **108 enzymes**, authors report a median promiscuous catalytic efficiency `kcat/Km` around **31 M^-1 s^-1**. Many side activities are too weak, at ordinary enzyme expression and substrate concentration, to contribute meaningful metabolic flux. Recruiting such a function may require a sequence of changes to expression, catalytic parameters, substrate abundance or network context.

**Scientific consequence:** **possible molecular reaction** is weaker than **reachable function**, and **reachable function** is weaker than **selectable viable behavior**. Merely cataloguing many latent enzyme functions, RNA paths or opcodes is not evidence that any actually contribute to survival. Their material costs and environmental availability are decisive.

Copley is a review, not another independent wet-lab lineage experiment. The 108 component measurements are cross-study source observations, not 108 new experiments carried out by AI-Research, and their data quality/assay comparability should be assessed before cross-enzyme generalization.

## Other source counterevidence from Pass16

- [Beaumont 2009 E2](79-pass16-beaumont-2009-history-dependent-switch-full-review.md): a mutation can **create a switch in an ancestor** while remaining **deleterious** there; earlier mutations make it selectable. A 2/12 event can vanish after an external novelty-exclusion reward is disabled.
- [Karve & Wagner 2022 E2](82-pass16-karve-2022-complex-environments-novelty-full-review.md): more mutation count does not always produce more novel viability; environmental complexity is confounded with longer experimental generations (authors partially adjusted).
- [Hayden 2011 E2](81-pass16-hayden-2011-cryptic-rna-variation-full-review.md): a component **inactive alone** may regain activity with a partner, yet not construct an independently reproduced consortium.
- [Rotrattanadumrong & Yokobayashi 2022 E2](83-pass16-rotrattanadumrong-2022-ribozyme-neutral-network-full-review.md): a measured 65,536-path subnetwork can be partly discovered with researcher-directed machine learning/search; **neutral** is threshold-specific.
- [Graham/Smith/Simons 2014 E2](84-pass16-graham-2014-neurospora-dormancy-fitness-full-review.md): geometric-mean persistence can select seemingly slower phenotypes; environmental sequences artificially prohibit consecutive losses, and lineages become extinct.

## Anti-claim taxonomy

| Assertion | Defensible evidence | Key counterfactual |
|---|---|---|
| A new reaction is physically possible | Biochemical promiscuous activity or specified computational primitives | Compare actual flux/cost at endogenous expression and substrate supply |
| New phenotype appears | Parent–descendant molecular mutation or state change | Revert relevant change, control background/measurement |
| New phenotype is **selectable** | Survival/reproduction relative to comparator in specified ecology | Compete without designer imposed novelty exclusions/bottlenecks |
| New collective is individually reproducible | Parent collective forms independently viable daughters/grandchildren | Remove group-copy API, test lineage fidelity and partner ablation |
| New ability remains functional under unfamiliar conditions | Blind held-out resource and partners with cost constraints | Reverse external cue, match baseline inputs, include extinction |
| New cognitive ability / consciousness | Independent mechanism/theory-specific tests, not currently established here | Strong non-conscious/reflex, physics-only and statistical alternatives |
