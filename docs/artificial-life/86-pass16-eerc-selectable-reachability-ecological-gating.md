## The scientific gap

Past EERC work compared **ecological construction**, **reproductive organization**, **history-conditioned learning**, **environmental traces**, **adaptive forgetting** and **latent repertoire recruitment**. Pass16 surfaces an additional distinction that prevents misleading conclusions:

1. An interaction may be **physically possible** but too weak/costly to support ecological action (enzyme side activity in Copley 2022).
2. A phenotype may be **expressible by a genotype** but selected against in the current ancestor/background (Beaumont 2009). A phenotype's *existence* is not the same as its *fitness accessibility*.
3. Genotypic neighborhoods can contain long **neutral paths** on some externally defined assays (2022 ligase ribozyme), while hidden variation can accelerate a later selected function after the relevant substrate changes (2011 Azoarcus ribozyme).
4. Altering **environmental selection conditions**, not just mutation rate, can change which side-effects become useful, while environment duration and chosen phenotype catalog confound simple claims (Karve 2022).
5. Even populations whose short-term growth appears inferior may leave more descendants under uncertainty (2014 *Neurospora*).
6. A chain of laboratory improvements can be due to a **designer's external classifier, novelty exclusion, bottleneck or copying**, not internal self-generated capability.

**The hypothesis:** an evolving self-maintaining system may sometimes cross a *selectable-reachability threshold* when ecological interactions and inherited organization jointly change the cost/viability of an already possible function, without researchers introducing a new primitive or rewarding that particular function.

### Proposed selective opportunity measure — observational, NOT a reward to optimize

For each candidate function `f` at environmental state `e` and ancestral organization `g`, separately record:

- `P_primitive(f)`: whether fixed material/reaction laws permit it. This does not vary within a properly controlled world.
- `P_access(f | g,e)`: whether an actual lineage produces a causally traceable **expression of function**, without direct researcher construction of the function.
- `V(f | g,e)`: measured **net ecological viability or group daughter reproduction** with function present versus validated causal knockout/rescue and matched resources, including negative returns.
- `C(f | g,e)`: actual material/compute/time/maintenance cost for expression, interaction and transmission.
- `H(f)`: documented **heritability of causal components/relations** over independently viable daughters/grandchildren, as distinct from merely surviving in an experimenter-managed container.

A state transition counts as a candidate **endogenous novelty event** only if `f` was not observed/functionally available before the ancestral change under matched observation, emerges from fixed primitive laws, has a mechanistically verified novel ecological use after cost, and is transmitted by the actual lineage. All axes stay separate; there is **no universal aliveness score**.

**New theory EERC-S:** the *viability of an already possible function* can cross a positive threshold owing to **environment–organization coadaptation**. Latent function supply and path connectivity may matter, but are not sufficient. We reject the shortcut of equating the number of possible pathways, mutation count or novel phenotypes with genuine higher-level evolving intelligence.

## Four mutually falsifiable competing explanations

| Explanation | What it predicts | How it is rejected |
|---|---|---|
| **X1 Designer intervention** | New function survives only because a novelty reward, externally sorted founders, ML guidance, new substrate or forced reproduction intervenes | Exact same substrate under withdrawn assistance and matched physical flux retains functional daughter inheritance |
| **X2 Passive biophysics** | Ordinary equilibrium kinetics/transport explains signal and no evolved heritable control changes a usefully reachable capability | Reversion of inherited relation loses function despite matched resources; independent rescue restores it |
| **X3 Genetic sorting only** | Preexisting rare genotypes with suitable traits survive; individuals do not learn, and no novel organizational relation emerges | Trace a new inherited state/interaction absent in ancestor, with persistent independent descendant and blind ecological transfer |
| **X4 Endogenous selectable access (EERC-S)** | A heritable internal/ecological change alters viability of previously latent function **without changing foundational physics or direct target score** | It vanishes under equal resource/selection controls, fails causal knockout/rescue, or requires a prebuilt copy API |

No source supports treating X4 as established in a digital environment. The four questions are research hypotheses, not a new approved architecture.

## Evidence from separate physical substrates — don't collapse

- **Bacterial selected switching 2009**: trait arises only 2/12, earlier ancestry changes relative fitness; source explicitly shows role of artificial exclusion and bottleneck, with phenotype possible even in unfavorable original background.
- **RNA variation 2011**: mutational preparation accelerates novel-substrate adaptation, one inactive isolate assisted by another RNA; not group heredity.
- **RNA neutral network 2022**: high-throughput measured paths with programmer-chosen threshold; external neural classifier and in-silico evolution guided selection.
- **E. coli hidden viability 2022**: environmental complexity appears to govern latent phenotype counts; conditions, selection duration and pleiotropic gene effects complicate a simple causal statement.
- **Fungal 2014 independent dormancy**: geometric-mean lineage survival rewards different bet hedges; its artificial schedule avoids successive bad periods, and raw failures still matter.
- **Yeast engineered switch 2008**: external tuning yields frequency-sensitive fitness without an organism developing its own switching program.
- **Protein latent evolution 2019** and **enzyme side-activity review 2022**: E1 corroboration and negative boundary that available activity may be below functional ecological flux.

## Future-only scientific design (NO implementation permission)

Pre-register one minimal separately authorized experiment in a sandbox outside AI-Research, with one static set of basic laws, finite resource ledger, fixed observation and independent seeds. Critically **do not** combine all candidate pathways into an ambitious big world immediately.

**Gate A — candidate opportunity:** measure baseline reachable interactions/functions and identify whether a candidate action exists at all and its spontaneous appearance rate. Physically possible does not mean selectable.

**Gate B — ancestral context:** compare same putative change in original, intermediate, evolved genomic/process backgrounds and zero-change controls. Falsifier is a new behavior but negative fitness in all backgrounds.

**Gate C — ecology and cost:** compare unchanged resource supply with inhabitant-built usable by-product and externally matched exogenous resource comparator; include costs and actual descendant viability, not just categorical novelty.

**Gate D — group heredity:** track new functional daughter and granddaughter organizations when external founders, novelty rewards, partner sorting and auto-copying are absent/appropriately ablated. A helper-dependent non-reproducing consortium is not a living higher-order lineage.

**Gate E — blind generalization:** put descendant lineages into **unseen resource/reliability reversals**, include all extinction, and compare identical budget to fixed stochastic switch, gradient-only physics, installed controller, and an **externally guided ML search** positive shortcut control.

No 'success' if advantages are based on engineered reward, source data/model leakage, evolved function allowed only because the developer added a new reaction mid-run, or reproduced collectives absent. Record negative and null outcomes permanently; do not adjust scoring after seeing them.

## Where this could ultimately connect to cognition

If a lineage can reliably create/reuse ecological functions while paying for memory and reproductive transmission, this could provide substrate for later **internally regulated prediction**. But no cited study shows that such substrate must lead to general intelligence, a conscience, a conscious experience or human-like selfhood. Each requires independent theory and ethical assessment.

The novelty of this document is an original **mechanistic falsification program for selectability of latent opportunity**, not a claimed discovery of new life or guaranteed roadmap.
