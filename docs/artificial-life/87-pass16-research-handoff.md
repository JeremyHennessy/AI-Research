## Author-reported primary full readings (E2) and key findings

1. [Beaumont et al. 2009 full original](79-pass16-beaumont-2009-history-dependent-switch-full-review.md), **existing E1→E2**. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08504). **2/12** evolving bacterial selection lines developed heritable colony switching under a researcher-imposed **novelty exclusion and founding bottleneck**. Final change could produce switching **even in the original ancestor** but was not beneficial there; historical context altered **selectability**, not physical possibility. Replay **3/36** from potentiated precursor vs **0/138** from original; researcher removal of special novelty preference led to loss of switch. **Do not double count Gallie 2015 molecular follow-up as new independent switch origin.**
2. [Acar et al. 2008 full original](80-pass16-acar-2008-engineered-switch-full-review.md), **existing E1→E2**, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/ng.110). Fast/slow phenotype switching trade-off was compared in yeast, but **researchers built and tuned the switch and selected reward conditions**. A powerful intentionally installed shortcut comparator, not independently evolved adaptive memory.
3. [Hayden, Ferrada & Wagner 2011 full original](81-pass16-hayden-2011-cryptic-rna-variation-full-review.md), **new E2**. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature10083). Two prediversified RNA populations adapted roughly **8x/6x faster** than initially undiversified WT after new substrate was imposed, in defined authors' rate units. An individually inactive RNA **AzoDelta** regained function with another enzyme: cooperative catalysis, not an independently reproducing collective.
4. [Karve & Wagner 2022 full original](82-pass16-karve-2022-complex-environments-novelty-full-review.md), **new E2**. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-33634-w). Complex environmental selection correlated with more newly viable bacterial phenotypes despite ~22x mutation-rate elevation not doing so in simple conditions. **Major confound** complex environments ran ~600 generations vs ~100–200 in simpler arms; 52 previously inaccessible test environments researcher-chosen; gene pleiotropy plausible, not all causally reconstructed. This is source interpretation, NOT lab protocol or antimicrobial evolution instruction.
5. [Rotrattanadumrong & Yokobayashi 2022 full original](83-pass16-rotrattanadumrong-2022-ribozyme-neutral-network-full-review.md), **new E2**. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-32538-z). Experimentally assessed >120,000 RNA variants including **all 65,536 of 16-site interval**; many functional neutral paths, but **external experimental selection, researcher-chosen activity threshold and neural classifier with 100 in-silico rounds** guided part of discovery. Not an RNA organism training its own brain.
6. [Graham, Smith & Simons 2014 full original](84-pass16-graham-2014-neurospora-dormancy-fitness-full-review.md), **new E2**. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2014.0706). **88 independent fungal evolution samples from 12 initial genetic lineages** generally increased dormant offspring fraction as experimentally selected bad-year frequency increased. Geometric-mean long-horizon fitness favored over arithmetic mean with important constraints: **consecutive bad years prohibited**, 50% schedule alternating, many failures/extinctions, no causal evolving memory mechanism isolated.

**E1 comparators**, not full-paper reviewed: [Zheng et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aax1837) cryptic fluorescent protein paths and [Copley/Newton/Widney review, 2023 issue](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.biochem.2c00249) on weak promiscuous enzyme side-activity. [Critical boundary note](85-pass16-hidden-variation-and-latent-activity-counterevidence.md).

## New original untested synthesis

[**EERC-S: selectable reachability and ecological opportunity thresholds**](86-pass16-eerc-selectable-reachability-ecological-gating.md), [v6 versioned manifestation](../../data/alife/combined-path-eerc-v6.json), and [eight-source experimental independence/counterevidence register](../../data/alife/pass16-selectable-reachability-evidence.json).

Four separate axes **cannot be reduced to a single innovation or intelligence score**:
- **Physically possible** under immutable substrate laws, even if rarely expressed or weak (promiscuous enzyme).
- **Reachable in an ancestral lineage** via mutation, combination or environment; distinguish genotype/phenotype connectivity.
- **Selectably useful** at real current ecological flux and cost, not merely visible, high novelty score, or dependent on programmed exclusion rewards.
- **Inherited in independently viable functional daughter and granddaughter organizations**, not copied by the simulator or reassembled by a technician.

An original hypothesis is that changing organism–environment relations could shift an existing weak or deleterious possibility into a **viable, heritable ecological function** without evolving new laws. It is **untested** in digital life and not guaranteed to create language, general intelligence, conscience or phenomenal consciousness.

### Most important negative and false positive controls

- The same phenotype can be expressed in an ancestral genotype but remain **deleterious**.
- Researcher rewards for novelty and restrictive founder bottlenecks can cause survival and apparent open-endedness **that vanish when those aids change**.
- RNA neutral paths can be **ML-guided search results**, not independent endogenous exploration.
- Catalytic help from another component may restore reaction **without reproductive co-transmission**.
- More mutation count is not necessarily new selectable opportunity; complex treatments may carry duration and selection confounds.
- A lineage can persist with low short-term performance, making geometric-mean population persistence relevant—but imposed environmental sequences and extinctions count.
