# Pass 17 — Contrary source evidence: hidden variants, enzyme substates and selection bias

**2026-10-08 | Three separate primary studies reviewed at E1 (abstract plus selected original Results/Methods sections; not full-method certification).** These publications involve different organisms and/or the *same previously selected enzyme trajectory*, which must not be pooled as repeated independent demonstrations of new life. No molecular engineering or source-code/data reproduction undertaken.

## A. Routh et al. 2025 — standing cryptic variants are not always useful

**Primary**: Shreya Routh, Richard J. Lindsay, Ivana Gudelj and Riddhiman Dhar, [Metabolic remodeling and de novo mutations transcend cryptic variation as drivers of adaptation in yeast](https://doi.org/10.1093/evolut/qpaf019), *Evolution* 79(4):650–664 (2025), **E1**. Complete original figures/appendices not independently accessed, although public journal abstract and indexed original methods/results were screened.

The authors evolved **three related yeast lines** in rich nutrient conditions for about **1,000 generations** and then examined responses to unfamiliar stressful environments. The source reports:
- elevated fitness in the familiar rich condition accompanied by metabolic remodeling and some apparent loss-of-function changes;
- generally **worse initial fitness/adaptability** in new stress conditions, with some line extinctions;
- later stress adaptation largely associated with **new mutations after challenge**, **not** detectable beneficial cryptic variants amassed during the prior stable regime;
- salt stress showed a **particular exception** plausibly affected by changed lipid metabolism.

**Negative against an overly rosy hypothesis:** long residence in a predictable setting does **not** guarantee that hidden variants accumulate in a way that helps new environmental functions. Some histories make future adaptation harder. The three original starting lines share a common stock; stress assays and sequenced mutations are not independent three-species origins.

## B. Sakuma et al. 2026 — an enzyme's preexisting physical substate distribution can move with selection

**Primary:** Morito Sakuma, Dhani Ram Mahato, Ferran Feixas, Colin J. Jackson, Eiji Nakata, Sílvia Osuna and Nobuhiko Tokuriki, [Single-molecule kinetic exploration of functional sub-states in an evolving phosphotriesterase](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-72534-1), *Nature Communications* 17:5945, published 2026. **E1 selective complete-accessible main Results/Methods screening**, supplemental structural/biochemical data not independently analyzed.

- Authors **measured** catalytic reaction-rate distributions for the original enzyme and **18 previously evolved laboratory variants**, studying native and other weak catalytic activities.
- Across the selected series, changing function correlated with **redistribution of single-molecule catalytic substates**. Authors used structural analysis and molecular-dynamics models to offer an explanation involving conformational changes, **not direct proof of a uniquely causative evolutionary conformational controller**.
- They found a measurable **metal/cofactor heterogeneity** that can itself generate a high/low catalytic peak. Careful source interpretation must distinguish biochemical preparation differences from evolution of conformational state variability.
- Variants and trajectories are drawn from **earlier directed-evolution studies**; these are **new single-molecule measurements** of an existing trajectory, *not 18 fresh independent evolutionary origins*. Raw molecular measurements and model calculations are not reproduced.
- A weak side-reaction measurable in a prepared assay is **not** an ecological fitness advantage, heritable new collective organism, or a learning strategy.

**Research-only interpretation:** selection can alter which physical substates dominate without changing underlying chemical laws; but a virtual ecology needs its **own** evidence of benefits and independent descendants.

## C. Kaltenbach et al. 2016 — native-function mutational robustness theory was contradicted

**Primary:** Miriam Kaltenbach, Stephane Emond, Florian Hollfelder and Nobuhiko Tokuriki, [Functional Trade-Offs in Promiscuous Enzymes Cannot Be Explained by Intrinsic Mutational Robustness of the Native Activity](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pgen.1006305), *PLOS Genetics* 12(10):e1006305 (2016), **E1** primary publisher original abstract and key result sections, full supplementary mutational assay statistics not reanalyzed.

The authors characterized previously selected enzyme function transitions, measuring old versus new reaction activities in different genetic backgrounds. They found that the native activity was often **less**, not more, robust to mutation than the new weak activity—a direct contradiction of one prominent simple explanation for weak observed trade-offs. Selection for new activity while not penalizing the old one can produce a **biased observable sample of apparent generalists**, without any universal biochemical buffer for useful side functions.

**Important double-count hazard:** Kaltenbach's enzyme study and Sakuma's 2026 report both involve previously selected catalytic-function trajectories from a related enzyme research field. Exact per-variant independence needs provenance checks; do not count article number or assayed mutants as new independent origins.

## Cross-paper comparison to retain in canonical evidence

| Attractive claim | Source that challenges it | Appropriate conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Every stable ecological history stores helpful cryptic functions | Routh 2025 | Some histories reduce adaptability; new variants dominate new-environment evolution |
| Same unusual phenotype implies same genetic mechanism | [Taylor 2016 E2](89-pass17-taylor-2016-cryptic-yeast-genetic-architecture-full-review.md) | 8/17 mutation-dependent vs 9/17 cryptic variant recombinations |
| Alternative routes must be low-fitness or nonexistent if not previously seen | [Lind 2015 E2](88-pass17-lind-2015-hidden-evolutionary-routes-full-review.md) | Many routes comparable fitness, suppressed by accessibility constraints |
| Evolution always makes future mutations less harmful | [Johnson & Desai 2022 E2](90-pass17-johnson-2022-mutational-robustness-landscape-full-review.md) | Mutation-effect distribution changed in one environment but not another |
| An enzyme weak side reaction constitutes an ecological niche | Sakuma 2026 / Kaltenbach 2016 | Biochemical measurability, resource-dependent utility and collective reproduction are distinct |
| Stable alternative catalytic substates mean neurons or awareness | Sakuma 2026 | Protein chemical kinetics is not experience, intelligence or self-generated organismhood |

**Best future research priority:** the counterfactual first point where a **previously low-fitness but physically possible** activity becomes **selectable through an organism-produced environment** rather than through a scientist adding the target nutrient/reward. This is the next EERC hypothesis, not an established result. No experimental activity authorized.
