# Pass 17 — EERC-C: ecological constraint redistribution and contingent route accessibility

**2026-10-08 | Original falsifiable scientific proposal; neither implemented nor independently supported as a working artificial-life system.** Builds on EERC-01…06 as **immutable** historical proposals. The present document authorizes **no world, organism, simulation, AI model, synthetic biology experiment, API use or changes to Ora/AgentTest/Ora2.0**. It is an attempt to formulate better causal comparisons rather than declare a winning architecture.

## Newly exposed gap in Pass 16's selectable-reachability hypothesis

EERC-S v6 separates **physically possible**, **accessible**, **selectably useful**, and **independently inherited**. Pass 17 adds a difficult counterfactual:

**Can the evolving environment itself change which functional paths are accessible and competitively worth following, instead of the experimenter *deleting the easy genetic routes*, *adding a novel resource*, or *picking an impressive visible phenotype*?**

Evidence from several distinct sources:
- [Lind et al. 2015 E2](88-pass17-lind-2015-hidden-evolutionary-routes-full-review.md): externally eliminating three common routes revealed thirteen alternative routes in mature bacterial cells. 91/200 independent populations yielded a selected morphology; this is **exogenous route suppression**, not organism-built ecological gating.
- [Taylor et al. 2016 E2](89-pass17-taylor-2016-cryptic-yeast-genetic-architecture-full-review.md): 17 independently isolated yeast colony morphologies arose from **different gene interactions**, and nine needed no new mutation. External visual category = similar morphology, **not shared function or advantage**.
- [Johnson & Desai 2022 E2](90-pass17-johnson-2022-mutational-robustness-landscape-full-review.md): 91 fixed test disruptions across 144 historically linked clones indicated environmental differences in how previous adaptation changes the future mutation-effect distribution; **more adaptation does not universally widen the future viable set**.
- [Routh et al. 2025 E1](91-pass17-cryptic-variation-and-enzyme-substate-counterevidence.md): long stable environmental evolution often worsened transfer to new stressors; adaptation mainly used **new** mutations, not prior accumulated cryptic variation.
- [Sakuma et al. 2026 E1](91-pass17-cryptic-variation-and-enzyme-substate-counterevidence.md): previously selected enzyme variants redistribute measurable catalytic substates; **enzyme side reaction ≠ ecologically selectable resource**.

No source demonstrates our integrated EERC-C chain; cross-domain analogy is *original inference* only.

## New original hypothesis

**EERC-C1:** In some finite-resource ecologies, an inhabitant-generated change to local constraints (consumed resources, secreted harmless products, cooperative costs, blocked pathways, partner-generated sinks, material boundaries) can make **previously improbable but physically possible** organizational transitions both **reachable and fitness-enhancing**, with the novel function remaining causally inherited by independently reproducing descendants **without** rewards for novelty or automatic group copying.

**Difficult falsifier:** after neutralizing the inhabitant-generated ecological change while matching material resources, the alternative path is *not* less accessible/selectable, or any observed benefit is entirely explained by generic nutrient subsidy/engineer-installed switching, rather than the proposed ecological relation.

This is **not** a claim organisms alter primitive computational physics. They may shift ecological *affordances within fixed physics*. Selection is still driven by physical viability and resource costs, not a new "consciousness score."

## An independent scientific ladder (do not sum dimensions)

| Level | Causal question | False-positive outcome |
|---|---|---|
| P — physical possibility | Can fixed substrate laws already produce the candidate state and reaction? | Developer introduces a new opcode or hidden custom special-case |
| A — accessibility | Could ancestral vs changed ecology/genotype reach it at matched mutation/time budget? | Researcher deletes dominant route or injects the desired state |
| E — ecological causation | Does an inhabitant-produced condition alter accessibility over inert/matched resource controls? | Environment change comes from researcher timer or untracked free material |
| F — fitness | Does candidate state improve blind net resource use/viability, counting all costs and extinctions? | Observer classifies "new" morphology while no survival gain occurs |
| H — heritability | Is the altered organization/function passed to independent daughters, not copied in engine? | Asexual genome copying alone or developer re-pairing reproduces false group |
| R — robustness | Does function persist across unseen resource/partner reversals and independent histories? | Only original training ecology produces positive score |

**Zero inference to consciousness** from positive values on any axis.

## Three competing explanations, each could win

1. **Installed access**: programming a novelty reward, disabling dominant routes or awarding resources creates all apparent innovation.
2. **Generic metabolic subsidy**: partner chemical release merely increases total available resource; no particular route/network is favored beyond biomass.
3. **Endogenous constraint migration (EERC-C)**: a causal organism-generated ecological change reshapes comparative mutation accessibility and downstream heritable utility, within immutable primitive laws.

Fourth skeptical possibility: **ecological route contraction**. Organism-modified conditions may *remove* most routes instead of expanding them. A more constrained ecology may be evolutionarily limiting. Include full extinct unsuccessful lineages and report this as a negative outcome, not as a defect to patch.

## Future-only counterfactual template (NO experiment authorized)

In a separately authorized, isolated minimal chemistry:
- Freeze primitive reaction grammar, material/CPU conservation, mutation supply, initial ancestor, reproduction/selection rules and measurement time.
- Compare (i) no inhabitant ecology modification; (ii) actual inhabitant-produced condition; (iii) matched exogenous material; (iv) inert/false environmental marker; (v) manually forced knockout of the easy route **as explicit shortcut positive control**; (vi) engineered novel reward baseline.
- Use ancestry/barcodes for candidate reactions and **causal ablation/rescue** of the ecological constructor; separate frequency of appearance, realized rate, growth/reproductive benefit and offspring survival.
- Hold out new resources/partners; test reversal, external scaffolding withdrawal and **daughters/granddaughters**; record every unsuccessful world, timeouts and extinction.
- Compare original easy route when restored, and whether alternative routes remain beneficial **without investigator removal**.
- Predeclare negative nulls: route never appears under endogenous conditions; appears but is neutral/deleterious; works only when scientist replenishes product; selected marker requires a morphology scorer; no independent group offspring.
- **Do not optimize a model to a desired novelty benchmark or treat designed neural readout as evolved cognition.**
