## Primary reading list and evidence levels

1. [**Lind, Farr & Rainey 2015 full original E2**](88-pass17-lind-2015-hidden-evolutionary-routes-full-review.md), [eLife DOI](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.07074). After *researchers* blocked three prevalent Pseudomonas routes, familiar colony morphology appeared in **91/200** independent microcosms (109 nonpositive by six days); 10 single-step and 3 multi-step alternative classes characterized. Frequently similar fitness among variants, **but externally deleted pathways/selected environments** and mature bacterial physiology supplied. This does not establish endogenous novel ecological function.
2. [**Taylor et al. 2016 full original E2**](89-pass17-taylor-2016-cryptic-yeast-genetic-architecture-full-review.md), [Nature Communications DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms11669). **17** independent reporter-phenotype instances from 106 genetic crosses/more than 100k screened progeny: **eight** with de novo mutation interactions, **nine** from combinations of standing cryptic variants alone. **Morphology is not measured novel ecological fitness**.
3. [**Johnson & Desai 2022 full original E2**](90-pass17-johnson-2022-mutational-robustness-landscape-full-review.md), [eLife DOI](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.76491). 91 gene-disruption perturbation effects in **144 nested clones** from historical six + six already evolving yeast populations, across two environments and six timepoints. Average effect became more deleterious in **one** environment, not detectably in the other; previous evolution-history data reused, not 12 fresh independent 10,000-generation experiments.
4. [**Three distinct E1 primary reports and counterevidence**](91-pass17-cryptic-variation-and-enzyme-substate-counterevidence.md): [Routh 2025](https://doi.org/10.1093/evolut/qpaf019) reports rich-environment preadaptation often harmed new-stress adaptation and *new mutations*, not standing cryptic variation, dominated subsequent adaptation; [Sakuma 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-72534-1) characterizes native/alternate catalytic **substate distributions** in 18 *previously selected enzyme variants*, with metal/cofactor confounds, not 18 new evolutionary origins; [Kaltenbach 2016](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pgen.1006305) contradicts simple assumptions about high robustness of native enzyme activity. Each remains E1 pending complete source-depth confirmation; none proves new functional organism in ecological conditions.

## New research path: EERC-C (original, unverified)

Six axes must remain independent: physics possibility, pathway accessibility, inhabitant-caused ecology, net survival/resource fitness, functional daughter heredity, and novel-environment generalization. **Strong prospective null controls:** matched exogenous nutrients vs constructor, environmental mark without usable content, manually knocked-out common route as explicitly labeled shortcut benchmark, no-new-function founder, unexpected resource/partner reversal, full costs/extinctions and repeated blinded independent daughter lineages. **No experiment is approved or implemented by this plan**.
