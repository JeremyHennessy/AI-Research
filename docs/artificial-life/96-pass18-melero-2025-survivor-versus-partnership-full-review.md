## Design and unit of survival

The study starts from an experimentally assembled complementary bacterial pair, exposes replicate populations to two environmental challenges, and follows decline and recovery. Unstressed controls retain both partners. This study is a distinct engineered strain system, not a continuation of Pauli's original consortium. Neither publication should be counted as an independent spontaneous origin of cooperation.

The main Results report **37/48 populations surviving under salinity and 41/48 under PNP**. The recovered populations tested for composition contain only one detected original strain, and evolved isolates can grow without the previously required exchanged nutrient. Thus the measured population can survive while its original obligate relationship disappears. Recovery of abundance is not recovery of collective organization. Do not summarize the two conditions as each having “over 80%” recovery: the first reported fraction is below that threshold.

This review preserves each statement at its source location. We have not resolved whether they describe different analysis sets or typographical errors. Do not compute a pooled success rate, silently replace a denominator, or claim an independent reanalysis. The qualitative partnership-loss finding is reported separately from these uncertain denominators. An archive audit would be needed for precise rate reconstruction; the linked source-data workbook, author repository and Zenodo 14992670 remain unexamined.

## Mechanistic strength and remaining alternatives

Composition testing, autonomous-growth assays and sequencing support escape from the imposed dependency. Isolated clones with single detected metabolic changes strengthen a causal genetic interpretation. Proposed redirection through promiscuous enzyme activities is still a biochemical explanation informed by earlier knowledge; the paper does not directly measure every proposed flux or reconstruct every mechanism. Numerical dominance and differences in mutational accessibility are plausible explanations of partner asymmetry, not proof that the missing partner is inherently incapable of rescue.

External nutrient supplementation does not universally reproduce the phenotype of evolved autonomy; the Discussion notes a supplementation failure to relieve sensitivity. Material rescue is therefore an intervention whose physiological consequences require measurement, not an automatic substitute for an evolved metabolic state.

## Consequence for the research direction

A relation-preserving objective would classify these surviving endpoints differently from a biomass or component-survival objective. That is a measurement distinction, not a reason to hard-code forced cooperation into an open-world system. A future study should report component survival, partner composition, exchange function and descendant reconstruction separately. The authors do not demonstrate autonomous co-dispersal or reproduction of the original pair as a collective, nor any digital-life or consciousness result.
