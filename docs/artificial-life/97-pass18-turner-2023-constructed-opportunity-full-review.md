## Source history and causal comparisons

The paper studies archived clones from the **Ara−3 LTEE population**, where citrate use had evolved in earlier work. Multiple sampling times, clones, metabolite measurements and repeated competitions remain nested within that historical population. They are not independent new origins of the innovation. The study extends that history with new ecological and genetic assays.

Citrate-using cells release metabolites that create a resource opportunity for a coexisting non-citrate-using lineage. Chemical measurements identify candidate exchanged resources, growth assays examine substrate use, and competitions with and without the producer test whether the producer changes consumer fitness. Reconstruction of a candidate consumer allele in the ancestor strengthens causality for a substrate-use phenotype; it does not reconstruct the complete evolved history or every growth-rate change.

The adjacent-time competition in the Results reports relative fitness **1.14 ± 0.04 with the producer** and **0.94 ± 0.02 without it**, for the later consumer clone against an earlier clone (means ± 95% confidence intervals). This sign reversal supports environment-dependent benefit of the evolved consumer. It is stronger than observing correlated abundance alone, but it measures differential growth, not arrival of a novel mutation or reproduction of a collective.

## Sampling and source discrepancies

Targeted time-course measurements of the exchanged metabolites use **one culture per clone**. Separate assays have their own replication; these must not be used to inflate the replication of that chemical time course. The growth assays comparing the two clades use different basal media, limiting a direct between-clade growth comparison.

The **Figure 6 caption reverses the filled/open symbol mapping relative to the Results prose** for producer presence and absence. Keep this unresolved source discrepancy visible. The adjacent-time numeric contrast quoted above comes from the explicit Results text. Do not infer a numeric trajectory from the disputed symbol legend without checking the original data. This review does not silently repair the figure or claim that its plotted mapping was independently verified.

## Why this supports opportunity generation, not obligatory mutualism

The producer also consumes released products and competes with the recipient. Cross-feeding need not be reciprocal cooperation or obligatory dependence of both partners. Citrate and the broader culture environment remain supplied and maintained by researchers; the exchanged metabolites are produced by the organisms within that environment. Endogenous ecological modification does not mean independence from external energy and matter.

The extended coexistence and consumer specialization support construction of an ecological opportunity. Subsequent loss of the consumer lineage is also part of the historical boundary. Neither persistent coexistence nor exchange demonstrates that parent collectives produce independently functioning daughter collectives without researcher pairing, sorting or repair.

## Consequence for the research direction

Retain a positive causal benchmark: actor presence changes the benefit of a recipient trait, and measured actor-generated resources offer a mechanism. Add a separate inheritance question instead of treating ecological benefit as a life badge. Partner removal and matched material rescue can diagnose mediation; they cannot alone establish or refute endogenous opportunity generation, and they do not demonstrate relational reproduction.
