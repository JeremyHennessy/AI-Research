# Pass 18 — Separate the creation of ecological opportunity from inheritance of the relationship

**Original research interpretation, 2026-10-08. Untested; no organism or scientific simulation implemented.** This pass refines how to interpret EERC-S and EERC-C. It does not add a new named theory or overwrite the seven archived EERC manifests. The [Pass 17 recovery audit](94-pass17-recovery-causal-audit.md) remains the qualification of v7's overly strong material-rescue falsifier.

## What the evidence changes

[Turner 2023](97-pass18-turner-2023-constructed-opportunity-full-review.md) supplies a positive case: an evolved actor produces resources, and its presence changes whether a recipient trait is beneficial. [Pauli 2022](95-pass18-pauli-2022-dependence-and-evolvability-full-review.md) shows that an established relationship can restrict subsequent adaptation, with history-dependent benefits and important exceptions. [Melero-Jiménez 2025](96-pass18-melero-2025-survivor-versus-partnership-full-review.md) shows recovery of a component after loss of its obligate partnership.

Together these studies support **two distinct research bottlenecks**: generating a useful ecological opportunity and transmitting enough organization for descendants to reconstruct it. The first does not establish the second. These are not three independent origins of collective individuality, and none tests the full proposed digital path.

## Record what continued

| Observed event | Classification | Additional evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Biomass recovers after one partner disappears | Component rescue; original relation lost | Autonomous-growth and composition checks distinguish escape from hidden persistence |
| Both partners remain in one continuing culture | Co-presence | Causal exchange and benefit; exclude common external support |
| Removing a producer harms a recipient, and supplying its product restores function | Material mediation supported | Matched intervention effects, direct resource measurement and alternative physiological explanations |
| Function returns using different partners or a different exchange route | Functional reconstitution; compositional change | Track the new route and ancestry; do not relabel it preservation of the old pair |
| Separated descendants restore the causal exchange without partner-specific sorting, repair or re-pairing | Candidate relational inheritance | Repeated independent descendant lineages, lineage tracking, failure retention and environmental controls |

No row is a sufficient criterion for consciousness, general intelligence or open-ended evolution. Failure at a collective criterion may accompany successful individual evolution.

## Define inheritance before looking at successful endpoints

A future design should track **compositional ancestry** (which components descend from which) and **functional continuity** (which material exchange or organization is rebuilt). Fix in advance whether a target claim requires the original partner identities, the original functional relation, or a new self-maintaining relation. An open-ended system can legitimately replace partners and functions; the record must show the replacement rather than moving the success definition after observing it.

Distinguish continuing parental material from a daughter process capable of rebuilding. Co-dispersal alone, residual shared resources, or researcher copying of an assembled community can each mimic relational inheritance. Remove partner-specific sorting, repair, re-pairing and group-copy assistance when testing endogenous reconstruction. Continue to allow ordinary environmental energy and material inflow; demanding a thermodynamically closed organism would be an invalid requirement.

## Future-only discriminating comparisons

1. Establish a candidate actor-generated opportunity using presence/absence and measured material mediation. An external-product rescue can strengthen this causal account rather than refute it.
2. Ask separately whether descendants regenerate the relation after ordinary dispersal or a bottleneck. Keep imposed transfer structure visible; continuation of the same mixed culture is insufficient.
3. Track survival, component ancestry, exchange function and autonomy escape independently. Retain extinct and relationship-losing descendants in the denominator.
4. Compare matched resource availability and ordinary environmental change without adding novelty rewards or compulsory cooperation. Resource addition is not assumed physiologically equivalent to evolution of autonomy.
5. Separate mutation arrival, establishment and detection. Endpoint novelty and persistent biomass do not identify these mechanisms on their own.

These comparisons refine interpretation of the existing AL11 future design; they add no executable experiment, approved organism architecture or successful scientific result. The prediction still at risk is whether internally generated opportunity can become reliably reconstructed organization without the investigator supplying the relation.

## Next evidence that would change the conclusion

The priority is a complete primary review with actual parent-to-descendant transmission, dispersal and reconstruction assays, paired with a failed-transmission case. Start from the already reviewed [induced endosymbiosis withdrawal failure](49-pass11-induced-endosymbiosis-2024-full-review.md), then examine an independently evolved host-associated transmission system. *Primitive agriculture in a social amoeba* (Nature, 2011, DOI 10.1038/nature09668) is a prospective reading target only: its full methods were not reviewed or added to the catalog in this pass. Check whether microbes are inherited, reacquired or selected externally, and whether function is tested in descendants rather than inferred from continued association.

Resolve the Melero denominator and Turner legend discrepancies against deposited data before using those figures for a pooled quantitative analysis. This archive work is a separate outstanding task, not a prerequisite for acknowledging the qualified main-text findings.
