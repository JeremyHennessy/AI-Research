## Interpretation and next pass

Useful ecological relations can arise and shape adaptation while remaining vulnerable to individual escape. Evidence that an actor creates opportunity does not establish that descendants reconstruct the relationship. Material rescue after producer removal can support mediation; it is not by itself a falsifier of endogenous ecological construction or evidence of collective reproduction.

Prioritize a full original transmission/dispersal study with descendant function assays, alongside the existing induced-endosymbiosis failure after external sorting stops. *Primitive agriculture in a social amoeba* (2011; DOI 10.1038/nature09668) is a prospective lead only, not a reviewed/catalogued result. Separately reconcile Melero's denominators and Turner's Figure 6 legend with their deposited data before any quantitative pooling or figure reconstruction. Do not rerun organisms or source experiments under this research-only authorization.
