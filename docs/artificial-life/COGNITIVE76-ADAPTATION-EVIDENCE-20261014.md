## One useful literature finding

Miras (2024), [Exploring the costs of phenotypic plasticity for evolvable digital organisms](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-50683-3), studies simulated modular robots with environmentally regulated body/controller plasticity. It isolates genetic costs from other plasticity costs and reports beneficial and neutral cases. The environment supplies a desired-direction signal; trials reset starting position between two behavior periods. Therefore it does not demonstrate Ora-style personally learned hidden mappings, native VM affordability or autonomous discovery.

Inference for Ora: plastic behavior, useful personal learning and evolutionary advantage are separate measurements. The paper motivates decomposing costs and environmental assistance; it provides no numerical forecast or acceptance threshold for Cognitive76. Do not replace digital cognition with robot/body construction.

## A compact evidence ladder

| Evidence | What it supports | What it does not support |
|---|---|---|
| Generic update scaffold exists | Expressive supplied machinery | Acquired knowledge |
| Blank-state actor changes predictions after own outcomes | Experience-linked updating | Useful learning without causal controls |
| Intact beats matched disrupted/disabled histories on common actual opportunities | Scoped useful own-history dependence | Generalization to unrelated tasks |
| Same source relearns a prospectively changed mapping | Conditional adaptation | Open-ended discovery |
| Blind inherited capacity yields greater paid descendant success | Evolutionary contribution of capacity | Inheritance of acquired knowledge |

Every rung requires actual costs and unconditional failure/death denominators. Missing control decisions are not wrong answers. None changes a completed experiment's claim scope.

## Smallest later adaptation test

Only after interpreting Cognitive76, decide whether a new adaptation question is justified. Register a finite changed-mapping task with a genuine no-change arm before fresh samples. Reuse context sensing and source architecture; change only the prospective task schedule. Keep hidden response changes private to the evaluator. The actor receives current context and its own subsequent outcomes, never a change label or a reset of acquired state.

Measure first-choice accuracy, paid work, cap-adjusted energy and completion before and after the change. Preserve both affected and unaffected contexts. Include matched fixed and own-history controls, plus a declared sparse observation-noise condition if that question is being tested. Never treat a failed action caused by real rules as fabricated sensor noise.

No outcome-picked source, learner insertion, optional stopping or horizon extension. Select the source enrollment mechanism and number of opportunities prospectively. This is a possible next experiment, not a prerequisite to completing today's comparison.

## Route to evolving intelligence

If useful learning is demonstrated, compare inheritance of blank-state learning parameters or architecture variants while keeping acquired models separate. Descendants must obtain their own experience. Establish full paid reproductive viability and ordinary admission only for that reproductive experiment; reproduction remains unnecessary for within-life learning.

Measure actual descendant success, not merely prediction scores or acquisition counts. Ora's cap can dissipate additional intake, so improved decisions need not create an evolutionary advantage. Genetic length, instruction/update expense, information acquisition, maintenance and mistakes should be logged separately. No hidden reward for matching a developer-selected algorithm.
