October 14, 2026. Research-only; no new execution authority or change to Cognitive76.

## Finding and scope
Kozielska and Weissing's [2024 neural-network model](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011840) supplies a local prediction-error learning mechanism. Their simulations link useful learning to lifespan, resource distribution and exploration cost. Environments are constant within a generation and change between generations; gathered energy directly determines offspring. This is conceptual evidence, not Ora's causal result or its actual resource accounting.

Inference for Ora: more global simulation steps do not necessarily give one entity more useful learning opportunities. Its personal lifetime, informative feedback, later returns and affordable actions must be measured. A large population cannot replace personal experience in a within-life claim.

A useful setup offers experience that predicts later outcomes after immediate feedback is insufficient. A completely stable inheritable mapping can favor a fixed policy; a mapping changing faster than observations can support may make memory useless. Neither is automatically a runtime defect.

Pick one measured limitation for a separately prospective follow-up. Do not silently lengthen personal lifetimes, reprice instructions or select the best historical genotype.

Later evolution experiments must quantify a real link from learned decisions to retained usable resources and paid descendant success. The paper's direct energy-to-offspring rule cannot be imported as Ora evidence. This reproductive question is not a prerequisite for useful within-life learning.
