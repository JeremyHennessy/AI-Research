## Question, finding and decision

**P1 question:** Does any **single**, independently assessed original system in the audited cohort renew *all* indispensable protein-translation, information, assembly and supporting roles after researcher-supplied finished active machinery is withdrawn, while retaining functional output and ancestry?

**Decision — C1 EVIDENCE GAP / NO COMPLETE CLOSURE IN AUDITED PRIMARY COHORT.** The component experiments demonstrate genuine and diverse positive biochemical functions, but **none of these audited original studies jointly measures complete active-constructor renewal in one self-sustaining system**. This is not an impossibility theorem for synthetic life or an assertion that the successfully reproduced biological descendants of existing living cells fail to renew their parts. A cross-paper combination of successful components is a *proposed integration*, not a demonstrated organism.

## First open causal construction loop (do not conflate papers)

A minimal translation-based proposal needs active ribosomes **R₀**, templates/instructions **G₀**, functional factors/tRNAs **F₀/T₀** and assembly support **A₀** to make active replacements **R₁/F₁/T₁/G₁/A₁**. The *first observed quantitative obstruction in the audited cell-free pathway* is that R₀ remains supplied and **R₁ is functionally measured but insufficient to replace R₀** (limiting large-subunit equivalent: 3 nM from an 80 nM native-ribosome seed). That calculation concerns **Kosaka's conditions only**, not universal rates or an estimate for a combined system. Further simultaneous obstacles include ~10-fold inadequate non-ribosomal factor capacity in Mottaghi, continued supplied S150/processing machinery, operator purification, externally renewed tRNAs/protein feed and template rescues in other *non-identical* experiments. Therefore neither an **R₁ → R₂** supporting cycle under initial-machinery withdrawal nor the full joint constructor loop has been shown in the audited cohort.

This ordering names **the first measurable open loop in this particular proposed construction route**; it does not assert that translation is universally required for nonbiological life, that all repairs are impossible, or that living descendants already propagating from functional cytoplasm lack genuine ongoing maintenance.

## Measurement that would change the decision

A distinct original primary study could reopen C1 if it **jointly** reports: initial quantities and physical provenance of each already active machine; new identity/functional assay of **every** indispensable replacement after a specified, meaningful loss; a time series showing sufficient active **R₁ → R₂** and simultaneous F/T/G/A replacement on measured finite raw feed; explicitly controlled withdrawal of finished active translator/constructor, prepared genome rescue and operator assembly (ordinary food/energy feed retained); proper failure-inclusive **independent founding units**, not wells, droplets or repeated transfers from one founder; chemical/material/waste accounting and independent controls against old-machine carryover. Verification of C1 alone would **not** establish positive paid work, a profitable simpler control (C2/C3), endogenous descendants (C5), or learning (C6).
