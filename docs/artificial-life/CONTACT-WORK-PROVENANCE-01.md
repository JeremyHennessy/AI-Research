## Primary evidence A — substrate-consuming autonomous contact motion

**Source:** Korosec CS et al., *Motility of an autonomous protein-based artificial motor that operates via a burnt-bridge principle*, *Nature Communications* **15**, 1511 (2024), DOI [`10.1038/s41467-024-45570-y`](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-45570-y), [indexed full original main text and Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10891099/). **E2-partial:** original Methods, Results and negative controls assessed in accessible HTML; supplementary data/raw tracks not independently checked. No independent replication established. Open primary article; exact redistribution permissions for individual third-party material not audited; only new analysis and source links are provided.

**What actually worked:** Protease-coated microspheres cut peptides immobilized on a prepared surface. Cleavage leaves a depleted track, making movement toward fresh peptide sites statistically favored without externally clocking each forward step. Reported maximum directional speeds reach **80 nm/s**. There is a mechanistically informative bare-lawn control. Importantly, the full Results report motility for **59 devices** (52% under the authors' motility rule) and prolonged immotility for **55 devices** over the observation window; selecting only motile trajectories would exaggerate reliability. On patterned one-dimensional tracks, comparison with peptide-free tracks supports substrate-coupled motion rather than a universal constant-speed motor. Small background flow is disclosed and incorporated in the authors' model.

**What remains unsupported:** The Lawnmower starts from manufactured protein catalysts, prepared microsphere hubs and a laboratory-patterned peptide lawn. The track's chemical potential is spent during cleavage; worn peptides are not regenerated by the motor, and disabled trypsins are not replaced. Useful external load work, chemically and mechanically **paid return to an already depleted contact**, track rebuilding, total heat/waste ledger, complete raw-to-role constructor renewal, an attainable paid static competing converter, and independent reproduction after withdrawal are **not** demonstrated. Lack of extra solution fuel while moving **does not** make the peptide track free fuel. The authors themselves flag immotile devices, dwells and the inability to easily replace inactive enzymes.

**Decision changed:** This is a concrete positive example of a local, unclocked *forward* contact transition coupled to a consumable substrate. **Reject a burnt-bridge/one-use lawn as the complete reversible either a closed AUTONOMOUS-CONTACT-01 or COHERENT-CONTACT-01 contact-and-return mechanism.** Keep only its evidence that contact motion can couple to local chemistry. Any future mechanism using that effect must first show an independently accessible track-renewal route and charge it; do not promote speeds or selective trajectories into useful work/surplus.

## Primary evidence B — fuelled directionality with manufactured molecular machinery

**Source:** Liu H-K et al., *Chiral catalysis-driven rotary molecular motors*, *Nature Chemistry* **18**, 1178–1185 (2026), DOI [`10.1038/s41557-025-02050-0`](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41557-025-02050-0). Original journal abstract and article metadata verified; publisher main-text access was unavailable in this review. An institutional accepted manuscript is listed but was not audited. **E1 primary abstract only; no E2 upgrade.** Publisher abstract is © authors under exclusive licence to Springer Nature; paraphrase only, no figures or long excerpts. No independent replication established.

**Reported demonstration:** A synthesized chiral catalyst/motor promotes carbodiimide hydration, linking fuel turnover and directional rotary bias. The publisher reports an **8:1** directional preference in one motor, an **up-to-30:1** preference with one hydrolysis promoter, and **1:2** reversed bias with another. This is useful to distinguish direction-selective catalysis from externally commanded clockwise/counterclockwise control, but direction ratios are **not** a mechanical load-work measurement and do not constitute a paid complete cyclic ledger.

**Missing:** No audited accessible constituent-level rate/potential table, finite reservoir depletion and waste account, synthetic motor construction cost, raw-to-catalyst renewal, damage replacement, profitable static converter, measured whole-life surplus, or reproduction with intervention withdrawn. Chemical promoters and supplied reactants must appear in the resource bill. Publication of a journal article is not proof of self-maintenance.

**Decision changed:** Retain as a *distinct mechanism worth checking for a physically causal direction-bias transition*, not as a closed constructor or available priced engine. **Do not implement** from 8:1 / 30:1 ratios alone; the full rate/potential/chemical-potential/force-load ledger and fabrication provenance are prerequisites. No currently verified advantage over a paid simpler comparator.

Abstract-level original study reports an externally operated optical-tweezer work measurement of RNA transitions. **E1 context only in this pass:** useful for distinguishing mechanically imposed forward/reverse protocols and measured work, but externally supplied optical trapping apparatus means it does not show a self-made molecular engine or autonomous retained-work cycle.

These two studies are not E2-reviewed in this work and must not increase completed-main-text source counts.

**Decision changed by these E1 screens:** require a stated apparatus-inclusive energy/work boundary and an independent rate interpretation before using any observed optical-pulling hysteresis or transition ratio as the complete autonomous contact/return law. Unknown costs remain UNKNOWN.

**Future-only falsifiers (not experiments authorized here):**
1. If a candidate contact-return cycle cannot close its heat/work/material balance without a free reset, the claimed autonomous return mechanism is rejected.
2. If forward displacement disappears when track cleavage is disabled while raw feed and environment remain otherwise matched, the mechanism is substrate-consuming; if it remains, an alternative bias/flow explanation must be evaluated.
3. If a proposed renewal path has no raw identity lineage for spent substrate and constituent enzymes, all-role renewal fails even with continued motion.
4. If a fully costed stationary converter with the identical feed has greater net positive useful work than the advanced candidate, comparative admission fails.
5. If the reported motor advantage disappears when nonmotile devices and all failed attempts are counted, the claimed reliable advantage fails.
