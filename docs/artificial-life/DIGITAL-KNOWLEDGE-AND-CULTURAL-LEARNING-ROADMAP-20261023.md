# Digital knowledge access and cultural learning — prospective research roadmap

**Date:** 2026-10-23  
**Status:** Future-facing research and design only. No new world, library service, ingestion job, organism instruction, grant, scientific registration, stage acceptance, or persistent activation is authorized by this note.

[K0 feasibility draft and traceable example](KNOWLEDGE-CAPSULE-K0-FEASIBILITY-20261023.md) are now documented. Rights for redistribution and VM instruction/energy costs remain unresolved; no K1 runtime is authorized. Consult the [latest dated update](ORA-RUNTIME-LEARNING-UPDATE-20261023.md) for completed retention and failed-pilot outcomes.

## Goal

Allow future Ora digital organisms to benefit from **existing human knowledge** and, later, from discoveries made by other organisms, without expecting every fact to be rediscovered by trial and error. Knowledge access is not the same as learning, intelligence, or evolution: the important test is whether an organism acquires information, decides appropriately how to use it, and gains an independently verified benefit.

This should be developed alongside, not ahead of, the existing supervised-world recovery priorities. A continuously running universe is not a prerequisite to testing an isolated read-only information interface. Conversely, a library must not be used to bypass scientific source, survival, cost, or experiment-control gates.

- [Context90 closed results](ORA-INTELLIGENCE-CURRENT-20261019.md): the largest supplied memory setting met its scoped own-history retrieval gate but no tested setting met the full paid-value comparisons. Reusing earlier information is not proof of energetic advantage.
- [Existing AI technical handbook](../02-technical-handbook.md): retrieval-augmented methods have recognized provenance, retrieval-error, freshness and prompt-injection limitations. The literature catalog is *research material*, not an approved organism policy.
- Existing finite organism experiments demonstrate bounded learning, reproduction and copying through supplied mechanisms, but **not** sustained beneficial evolved cognition or a live persistent population.
- The contemporary Ora scientific organism has a small instruction language and bounded storage. It cannot simply understand free-form scientific articles. Any local-model interpretation or human-authored translation is **external assistance** and must remain separately attributable.

## Five knowledge channels — never collapse their provenance

1. **Human reference material:** scientific publications, databases, textbooks, technical specifications, public records and verified research notes. These are claims with scope and uncertainty, not guaranteed correct actions.
2. **Curated knowledge capsules:** concise, structured, versioned, source-linked, rights-reviewed observations or hypotheses prepared offline. A local LLM may suggest capsules but cannot silently approve its own extraction or give them scientific authority.
3. **Organism observations:** information directly obtained through its own paid sensing/actions in its existing world, with actor, time, energy, actual channels and provenance.
4. **Heritable program:** the copied code/capacity with explicit mutation and reproduction costs. This is distinct from an individual's acquired facts.
5. **Cultural/shared memory:** data another organism lawfully publishes or teaches through a future bounded in-world mechanism. It is neither inherited DNA nor personally discovered evidence.

A result must identify which channels made it possible. A human-supplied answer in a lookup table cannot be counted as an evolved discovery.

## Proposed staged research program

### K0. Catalog and provenance contract — can be designed now

- Inventory already available approved references in AI-Research and existing local assistant knowledge workflows. Reuse them instead of creating a second unrestricted scraper, database, agent or scheduler.
- Draft a compact capsule schema: stable item ID; fact / procedure / hypothesis / observation type; claim or encoded relation; primary source and retrieval date; exact version/content digest; scope and uncertainty; rights/access classification; human-reviewed status; translator/model identity if used; contradictions/supersession; permitted audience; and distinct estimated lookup/encoding/storage costs.
- Keep citation identity, evidence confidence and practical applicability separate. Human research catalog evidence labels do not turn into organism fitness scores.
- **Deliverable:** one draft schema, a source-to-capsule traceability example, and an explicit list of unresolved permissions, information channels and implementation choices. No unreviewed ingestion, no runtime connection, no organism experiment.
- **Stop if:** allowed sources or a trust boundary cannot be identified. Record the blocker rather than fabricate data.

### K1. Offline read-only librarian — first bounded software candidate

- In a separate reviewed future engineering change, test deterministic retrieval of a tiny curated fixed snapshot. Use a bounded catalog (for example SQLite or JSON) only if the current approved storage cannot meet the need.
- Keep the librarian outside the organism VM, with no code execution, shell, repository write, actor control, unrestricted network, privileged simulator truth or automatic human-source promotion.
- Validate bad/missing/stale/conflicting capsules, wrong hashes, access failures, adversarial document instructions and citation/provenance mismatch.
- **Deliverable:** independently reproducible source-to-response trace plus strict budgets. Retrieval and software tests alone provide **zero organism learning or stage credit**.

### K2. One priced information opportunity — only after prospective admission

- Specify a minimal organism-visible representation that its instruction set can actually read, such as a bounded encoded hint/relation or environmental observation. Compare candidate designs without silently altering any closed VM.
- The future organism must **choose and pay** to acquire, decode, store and act on information. Equal-capacity competitors get equal resource budgets and access to any generally shared affordance.
- Freeze a task where information is decision-relevant, not a direct hidden-target or reward leak. Pre-register a source-neutral query opportunity, resource/scheduling constraints, all channels and the first useful result.
- Compare: no library, irrelevant/wrong library entry, current-reactive/simple fixed strategy, memory-disabled or own-history-disrupted candidate, and a disclosed privileged-oracle **upper bound only**.
- Measure retrieval relevance, actual uses, correct decisions, survival, formation/computing/query/storage costs, resource cap losses and complete lineage/failed-opportunity denominators.
- **Stop before new Worlds if:** the organism cannot afford and physically encounter an informative read, current observables already suffice, or the answer leaks an evaluator-only target.

### K3. Learning **when and what to ask**

- Study whether the organism learns to request useful information only when the expected decision benefit exceeds query and memory expense.
- Use unfamiliar held-out problems, changed/unavailable knowledge, contradictory evidence and alternate environments. Do not let fixed source IDs, a predictable clock or trainer-curated answers make the task trivial.
- Separate genuine improved information-seeking from a supplied fixed lookup routine, an LLM behind the scenes, and mere retrieval accuracy.
- Require independently controlled changes in its *own subsequent actions and actual returns* before claiming useful knowledge-based adaptation.

### K4. Organism-to-organism cultural transfer

- Explore a future bounded in-world protocol for publishing, transmitting, accepting, testing, rejecting and forgetting observations. It must operate with explicit bytes/energy/time/availability and cannot execute sender instructions on the host.
- Compare a recipient with truthful teaching against no teaching, shuffled/uninformative teaching, direct personal learning, and an equally funded fixed/reference actor.
- Trace source organism's personally acquired evidence, transmission cost, receiver interpretation, new recipient behavior, reproduction and longer-term effects. Account for misinformation and nonviable recipients.
- Determine whether useful knowledge spreads **culturally** without incorrectly crediting genomic evolution. Separately test whether learning/communication *capacity* becomes beneficially heritable.

### K5. Long-horizon collective knowledge — speculative

- If K2–K4 succeed independently, investigate cooperative discovery, division of labor, knowledge accumulation, cross-task transfer and robust correction of obsolete information.
- Do not equate a large archive, copying, sophisticated dialogue, novelty count or increased simulation duration with emergent intelligence or consciousness.

## Scientific acceptance ladder

**Information available** → a valid lookup existed.  
**Information accessed** → the actor used the legal paid channel.  
**Information understood/used** → its own decision demonstrably changed because of the information.  
**Useful learning** → paid actions outperformed honest controls on independent cases.  
**Cultural transfer** → another organism gained useful competence through a traced transmission rather than direct privileged assistance.  
**Evolved learning capacity** → inherited variation produced replicated differential descendant success under complete resource/ancestry controls.

Each rung needs independent evidence and its own failure cases; none promotes the next rung automatically. Report all costs and unfavorable/null cases, and freeze acceptance criteria before any fresh target, source bank or intervention.

## Source selection and security

- Prefer authoritative metadata/abstract records or material whose permitted use has been checked; examples to evaluate include OpenAlex, Crossref, arXiv and Europe PMC, plus the existing AI-Research catalog. Source names are *candidates*, not configured connections or confirmed API/rights authorizations.
- Preserve original provenance and licensing. Do not copy restricted full-text collections simply because the project is noncommercial. Do not publish private chat, local memory contents, machine identifiers, credentials, or evidence archives.
- Treat all retrieved material as **untrusted data**, not instructions. External documents may never authorize tool calls, scientific experiment gates, modifications to organism genomes, repository pushes, spending, external communications, or execution.
- Favor bounded offline/local retrieval without metered services or OpenAI API keys. New external services, network access, persistent ingestion or accounts require separate explicit approval.
- Do not let the host/local assistant secretly select organism actions; keep cognition supplied by the translator distinguishable from what the organism acquired.
