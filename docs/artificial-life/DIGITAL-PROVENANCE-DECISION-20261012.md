## Decision: no causal promotion from provenance tags

A conservative feedback-origin tag tracks *possible information ancestry* of a state or action. It does not demonstrate that the attributed feedback **caused** an action, that the action was useful, or that access to older feedback improved any outcome.

The prepared kernel covers explicit register, memory, code, program-counter, movement/position, inbox/message, copy and inherited-state dependencies. Its limits are material:

1. **False positives are possible:** a no-op write or cancellation may retain an origin tag although actual numerical behaviour is unchanged.
2. **False negatives are possible:** energy depletion, food removal, scheduling, partner availability, nursery-size or other implicit control-flow dependencies fall outside the prepared component. An empty tag cannot prove the absence of experience dependence.
3. **Foreign provenance is not own-life learning:** a child's copied program or message can carry its parent's feedback ancestry; preserve actor ownership and birth-reset semantics rather than crediting the child with its own experience.
4. **The event is not the advantage:** a feedback read after endpoint completion, repeated reads of one attempt, an empty resource attempt, or a tag carried without subsequent beneficial action cannot independently satisfy learning admission.

**Therefore do not classify any candidate as learned, history-independent or causally improved from this kernel alone.** Keep unsupported classifications UNKNOWN.
