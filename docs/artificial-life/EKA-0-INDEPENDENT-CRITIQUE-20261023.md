# EKA-0 independent research assessment

**2026-10-23 — provisional verdict: UNKNOWN for Ora feasibility; NO-GO for a new population now.** This completes the literature and falsifier leg of [issue #62](https://github.com/JeremyHennessy/AI-Research/issues/62), conditional on the separate [Ora source/cost audit, issue #313](https://github.com/JeremyHennessy/Ora2.0/issues/313). It does not authorize a World, change physics, or reverse any closed result.

The ambition is scientifically coherent. The current evidence does not establish an affordable, mutation-accessible route from Ora's supplied ancestor to a useful new inherited information strategy. That is a missing feasibility argument, **not evidence that such a route is impossible**. GO for a population requires the source audit below; another elaborate supplied learner would answer a different question.

## What is already known, and what could be new

| Prior mechanism | What prior work already anticipates | Stronger interpretation to rule out in Ora |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Digital program evolution | Avida evolved complex logic functions through mutational stepping stones under designed rewards. A new useful instruction sequence alone is not a new general phenomenon. [Lenski et al. 2003](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature01568) | A cheaper fixed/reactive mutant, exploiting task economics, can explain improved reproduction without learning. |
| Baldwin effect / assimilation | Learning can change selection on inherited variants without copying acquired information into the genome. Hinton and Nowlan explicitly supply the within-life search and success recognition; this is a mechanism illustration rather than emergence of that machinery. [Author's complete article](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~hinton/absps/evolution.htm) | Descendants improve because selection fixes an answer or easier starting state, rather than evolving a learning rule. |
| Evolved learning mechanisms | Arnold, Suzuki and Arita report evolved local neuromodulatory learning and comparison with reinforcement-learning baselines. Full methodology was unavailable in this pass; their abstract is sufficient to reject a broad novelty claim, insufficient to certify a matched Ora comparator. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42518295/), [author code](https://github.com/sovvel/hbl) | Choosing between supplied learning modules, or inheriting a supplied updater, does not show a new endogenous updater. |
| Evolvability under change | The 2025 Avida study uses 100-instruction ancestors, substitutions, changing task regimes and later distinct tasks. Its measured mutational neighbourhoods and response to changing conditions anticipate claims about evolution shaping future adaptability. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2413930121) | Better performance after a switch can be a changed mutation distribution or cached fixed strategy, without personally acquired knowledge. |
| Mutable interpretation | Physis allows heritable processor/instruction definitions over fixed lower-level primitives. Its reported structural conservatism and inconclusive advantage over fixed processors caution against equating more mutable machinery with more evolutionary potential. [Existing full-primary review](28-physis-2003-full-review.md) | More flexible syntax increases breakage or search space without creating selectable useful variation. |
| Foundation-model program search | DGM's primary abstract explicitly describes a foundation model generating agent variants and benchmark validation. [DGM v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22954v3) | Externally authored mutations or scoring-generated code are engineering search, not organism-native discovery. |
| Communication / culture | Existing Stringmol and ecological reviews already distinguish inherited programs, interactions and functional dependencies. [Stringmol review](26-stringmol-2016-full-review.md), [roadmap](DIGITAL-KNOWLEDGE-AND-CULTURAL-LEARNING-ROADMAP-20261023.md) | Sending or copying a useful message does not show cumulative culture; a static lookup table can explain recipient benefit. |

**Potential Ora contribution:** a complete, independently audited causal chain in this particular resource-accounted substrate: an ordinary mutation introduces a previously absent information-use routine; an organism personally encounters informative feedback; that routine changes behaviour or legally transmitted program content; descendants gain fully paid useful performance in reserved contexts; targeted interventions remove that advantage. This would be a narrow new Ora result. Worldwide novelty remains unestablished. A fixed learned answer is weaker than an inherited ability to acquire new answers; both must be labelled separately.

## Closed Ora evidence constrains the proposal

The [merged strategy](ORA-ENDOGENOUS-LEARNING-EVOLUTION-DECISION-20261023.md) and existing research handoffs report:

- **Longitudinal01:** two finite 8,192-dispatch histories, eight generations and 35 paid births each, with supplied ancestry and mutation disabled. Continuity is evidence; evolutionary improvement is not.
- **Learning81:** scoped personally acquired cue knowledge under supplied machinery; the cited operating surplus of 480 lost to compact references at 664/661. Correct choices did not establish economic superiority.
- **Inheritance83:** variation and paid births occurred, but predeclared beneficial-selection criteria failed. Only two of eight q8 proposals reached mutant paid births. Counting selected survivors would conceal the main feasibility problem.
- **Context90:** q8 met its scoped retrieval criterion, while smaller variants and all paid advantage flags failed. Retrieval of personal history did not establish a viable new hereditary strategy.

These are existing closed findings, not independently re-executed here. Their original sample banks, negative results and acceptance decisions remain unchanged. Assistant retention, local-model prompt histories and PILOT74 shutdown qualification contribute no new organism-learning evidence. PILOT74 preserves experimental state; it does not establish EKA feasibility.

## Required source-only handoff and decisive falsifiers

1. **Information opportunity.** Exhibit two reachable situations with identical presently available cue/clock/location/last-feedback inputs but different profitable later actions. Earlier personally observed information must distinguish them. Otherwise a memoryless reference can solve the task and there is no identifiable memory claim. Confirm the opportunity occurs before death and before relevant information expires.
2. **Reachable expression.** Pin ancestor, legal mutation distribution, mutable addresses/opcodes, copying semantics and newborn state. Identify a finite legal route to an information-use phenotype and intermediate viability. A hand-written witness establishes possibility only; do not seed it, select a successful founder, or call it evolved evidence.
3. **Paid search opportunity.** Bound actual births, viable expression, encounters and transmission, rather than counting VM dispatches as mutation trials. If a particular route requires d specific independent substitutions with probability p each, p^d is only a route-specific illustrative probability: neutrality, alternate routes, selection and correlated mutation change it. Use exact source probabilities and a declared denominator. A loose bound is UNKNOWN, not permission for a large search.
4. **Cost coverage.** Show a possible positive balance after observation, processing, waiting, memory/code material, copying, division, cap heat and failed offspring. Keep nominal, operating and formation-inclusive ledgers distinct. Reject this assay if even its honest best-case benefit cannot cover unavoidable costs; do not subsidize it after seeing failure.
5. **Transmission mechanism.** Separate copied code from private memory, maternal resource transfer and persistent environment. SELFWRITE followed by COPY can transmit content, but that does not itself identify selection for a learning mechanism. Trace changed words and descendant expression, with the relevant private state absent at birth.
6. **Affordable causal design.** Predeclare matched intact, disrupted-information, effective-write-disabled, inheritance-disabled and no-mutation comparisons. A lesion that kills replication or changes available resources is not selective evidence for learning. Include sham lesions and report any unavoidable cost mismatch. Do not require an enormous factorial population before knowing which single path is plausible.

**NO-GO for the candidate assay** if no informative opportunity exists, useful expression cannot occur before death, or a source-backed upper benefit bound is below required cost. **UNKNOWN** if mutational accessibility or economical reproduction is not bounded. **Provisional GO to register EKA-1**, not execute it, requires all six items with exact source identities and independent scrutiny. No current evidence warrants rejecting the entire VM or immediately replacing it. If a specific bottleneck is proved, compare the smallest alternative that changes that bottleneck under matched resources; do not infer that Lenia, a neural substrate or a longer genome automatically solves it.

## Smallest credible EKA-1, conditional only

Ask first whether **one mutation-origin information-use routine** can improve fully paid descendant performance on a narrowly defined hidden-state task, relative to the actual ancestor and the best cheap reactive competitor. Do not initially demand simultaneous learning-rule evolution, self-rewrite, culture and open-ended novelty.

Freeze the source, neutral founder rule, mutation law, resource ledger, primary and unseen conditions, independent sampling unit, minimum worthwhile effect, confidence procedure and stop budget before admission. Use complete lineages, including unexposed deaths and incomplete copies. Require at least two independent mutation origins or label a single origin exploratory. Compare a minimal fixed policy, last-feedback policy, finite-state/one-bit policy where legal, and a supplied learner as an explicitly authored ceiling; pay their real materials and action costs. A supplied learner winning is a benchmark, not an evolved organism result.

Separate discovery and causal tests: retain candidate genotypes without outcome-dependent edits; assay them only under a separately authorized frozen protocol. Re-evaluate on new hidden assignments and erase private experience without changing genotype. Improvement that survives erasure but fails genotype reversion supports inherited fixed behaviour; improvement requiring new personal evidence and a specific routine supports inherited learning capacity. A self-write claim additionally requires word-level feedback-to-write-to-copy provenance. No new population or genotype assay is executed by this document.

## Claim ladder and source limits

Record five different outcomes: **developer supplied**, **acquired during life**, **mutation-origin useful change**, **inherited improvement**, and **new capability surviving independent causal tests**. Progress on one does not imply the next. Use the project's existing seven-stage roadmap without granting a stage for this review; persistent ecology, cumulative culture and open-ended intelligence remain unproven.
