# EKA-0 selective prior-art note — supplied heritable patterns versus naturally evolved information processing

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-31. **Evidence level:** direct reading of the public preprint text; no independent rerun, code reproduction, peer-review certification, or Ora experiment. **Decision:** EKA feasibility **UNKNOWN**; admission of a new scientific population **NO-GO**.

## Primary source and version

Nam H. Le, *Organisms Write Their Own Evolution: Direct Epigenomic Inheritance Accelerates Learning on Compositional Tasks*, arXiv:2602.00978 **v2**, revised **2026-10-09** (v1 submitted 2026-02-01), https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00978 and https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.00978 . This is a **preprint**, not evidence of independent replication. Method and comparative figures below are author-reported; see §§3–5 of the source.

## What was actually supplied and measured

Phenopoiesis (PHENO) is an *engineered* two-channel inheritance mechanism. The developer supplies the composition primitives, within-life exploration/evaluation rule, **successful-solution write-back**, genetic weight update, heritable pattern library, and offspring inheritance semantics (§§3.2–4.2). The author compares a gene-only model (GENE), a within-life-learning/no-write-back model (BALDWIN), and PHENO on 10×10 shape-composition tasks. The text reports mean post-switch recovery of **28.4, 14.7, and 8.3 generations**, respectively (§5.2.2), and describes further multi-task advantages (§5.3). These are *reported model outcomes*, not Ora measurements.

Two limitations matter for causal attribution:

1. **PHENO versus BALDWIN changes more than epigenome transmission:** PHENO updates inherited genetic weights **and** passes a learned epigenomic pattern library; BALDWIN does neither (§4.2). The reported difference does not, on its own, isolate the effect of the epigenome rather than genetic write-back or their interaction.
2. Equal numbers of model fitness evaluations for BALDWIN and PHENO (§4.2) do **not** establish equal paid storage, lookup, composition, copying, lifetime survival/reproduction or operating-system resource costs. The paper does not test Ora's native VM, exact COPY/division cashflow or an ordinary mutation path originating a useful information-processing rule.

This is relevant **prior art for developer-provided vertical transmission of personally acquired solutions**. It is **not evidence that such a transmission rule can evolve unaided from Ora's ordinary founder**. Do not repeat the source's broad novelty or agency claims as established facts.

## Smallest discriminating EKA question

The remaining Ora-specific question is not whether a supplied heritable-memory system *can* outperform a weak baseline in a composition task. It is whether **ordinary mutations in the existing compact VM** can introduce an affordable information-use/write rule absent from the founder, express it before death, legally transmit effective code to **paid offspring**, and improve **fully paid descendant fitness** across pre-registered held-out conditions.

For a future, separately authorized causal comparison, challenge any claimed win with: **(a)** a fair compact fixed/reactive and Baldwin-like within-life learner; **(b)** a cost-matched inherited-fixed-solution controller; **(c)** separate effective-SELFWRITE and inherited-information disabling/sham controls rather than changing two channels at once; and **(d)** full exposure, failed origin, birth/death, energy/material, formation, memory and execution accounting. A successful authored witness, supplied pattern store or post-hoc selected descendant cannot satisfy mutational reachability or natural-selection origin.

**Boundary:** this note neither proposes a new ecology nor changes ancestor/VM law, rewards, budgets, seed banks, closed Context90/noisy-credit results, PILOT74, native admission or Observatory. Source-only comparisons and ordinary-mutational path economics remain the cheaper next science work.
