## Distinct research question

Does repeated daughter DNA inheritance under programmed physical droplet fission demonstrate a constructor that independently reproduces, or does it demonstrate a useful externally operated division-and-replenishment testbed? What controls and cost/provenance measurements would discriminate these hypotheses **in future work only**?

This original study was new to the reviewed AI-Research main at preparation and is distinct from the previously published contextual sources. Minagawa et al. 2025, DOI `10.1038/s41467-025-56172-7`, already has primary critical review and structured evidence under **Pass 36**. Abil et al. 2024, DOI `10.1038/s41467-024-53226-0`, is reviewed under **Pass 35**. Both are contextual comparators, **not** newly counted studies and **not** independent replication of the precise Zhai apparatus.

## Observed positive result (exactly what happened)

A **programmed electrowetting-on-dielectric (EWOD)** array transported, fused and mechanically divided reagent-bearing aqueous droplets. The device had **112 electrodes** and reservoirs for fresh externally prepared reaction mixes, supported by externally driven heater, controller, oil-phase conditions and an assay readout. Daughter droplets could retain DNA while fresh recombinase-polymerase-amplification (RPA) enzymes, primers, dNTPs, ATP and reagents were added again to the returned daughter. The investigators ran **20 programmed rounds** of fusion, DNA amplification, division and daughter collection. In particular, fluorescence associated with DNA amplification could be maintained/modulated across 20 rounds; gel electrophoresis also supported DNA amplification in the separate on-chip RPA validation assay. This is a meaningful engineering demonstration of coordinated serial propagation of DNA material under external apparatus control, **not an observed spontaneously dividing organism**.

- Each round *externally* programmed droplet movement and equal daughter division. One daughter was taken for analysis; the other rejoined externally dispensed reagents for the next round. The chip used a resistive heater and a supplied silicone oil medium. The RPA mix included commercially prepared active biochemical enzymes and a fluorescent probe; the gene being copied was not shown to encode replacements for the RPA machinery.
- The 20-round feedback experiments deliberately alternated **Mg²⁺ additions (rounds 1–5, 11–15) and EDTA additions (6–10, 16–20)** in one series, or alternated 25 °C and 45 °C temperature conditions in another. Thus changes in signal were *operator programmed*, not a learned or evolved self-regulatory program.
- Under magnesium-addition conditions, reported fluorescence grew; it declined during EDTA inhibited cycles, consistent with serial dilution. At 25 °C the reaction could not outpace dilution. At ~40 °C apparent DNA amplification and halving were closer to balance under their tested conditions. These negative/weak regimes are informative, and should not be hidden behind the phrase `twenty generations`.
- The authors reported coefficient of variation **3.72% and 4.65%** in auxiliary droplet mixing/division contexts, and 3 independent replicates in their 20-cycle DNA assay. These are reproducibility measurements of a **machine-driven cycle**, not proof of heritable adaptive advantage or viable new daughter organism founders.
- Some detected products were DNA-amplification fluorescent signals; a fluorescence plateau, the configured PMT gain adjustments for saturation, and amplification in separate gel assays must be distinguished from verifying functional allele inheritance, newly synthesized enzymes and permanent daughter viability.

## Finite resource, exergy and material provenance

**Identifiable source and cost categories, amounts not totaled:** externally manufactured electrowetting chip, dielectric coatings, electrodes, controller and heater, regulated electrical power and heat flow, prepared silicone oil volume, fresh RPA enzymes and kits, ATP/phosphocreatine and dNTPs, DNA primers/probes/template, buffer and salts, new magnesium/EDTA feed, sample handling, analytical instrumentation, assay consumables, rejected/collected daughter volume, chemical waste, device wear, failed reactions, repair and operator time. These are **not zero** merely because no integrated joule/mole/economic budget was supplied. The source does not show daughter fabrication/replacement of the chip, controller, heaters, enzyme production capability or feedstock processing. Environmental fuel/food provision should not be confused with researchers deliberately **replacing the active constructor** every cycle.

**Missing evidence:** no whole-system finite exergy budget; no allocation of electrical work, caloric chemical input and discarded daughter material per retained useful offspring; no independent long-term chip-maintenance or degradation/repair accounting; no complete mass balance; no demonstration of a profitable simpler control after all specified costs; no intervention-withdrawal persistence. Unknown costs remain **unknown, not zero**.

## Specific fair controls needed in *hypothetical future work* (not performed here)

A fair interpretation would compare (a) fully costed instrumented DNA cycling under the published DMF apparatus with (b) stationary or passively partitioning, externally replenished isothermal-RPA conditions using **the same feedstock, probes, energy boundary, sample count and failure accounting**. The simpler route cannot satisfy daughter-fission criteria merely by copying DNA, so score both on separately preregistered outcomes: chemical copying throughput, daughter-compartment heredity, verified independently functional daughter system, total costs, and resource-adjusted viable descendants. Avoid a rigged control incapable by definition of meeting the endpoint.

For autonomy specifically, compare intervention-withdrawal conditions in which *ongoing ordinary finite energy and feedstock* remain available, but direct chip-operated partition, timed reseeding with active enzymes and externally programmed winner selection do not. The existing paper **does not demonstrate this withdrawal test**; the scenario is a falsifiable future hypothesis, not an observation or instruction to implement.

## Counterhypotheses and negative findings

1. The observed daughter succession might be fully accounted for by **operator timed droplet splitting plus external enzyme replenishment**; no intracellular construction loop is needed to explain the published results.
2. Fluorescent reporter signal, although supported by gel products in a separate assay, is not a functional organism readout; substrate/probe chemistry and adjustment of saturated fluorescence detectors complicate inference about long-run biological inheritance.
3. Dilution and magnesium/EDTA cycles produce predictable externally forced dynamics without sensing, memory, adaptation or environmental feedback learning.
4. Even with adequate DNA copying, viability and reproductive capacity of a descendant containing no fresh externally supplied active enzyme has not been demonstrated.
5. The platform's improved operator control versus older liposome approaches is a legitimate **assay-engineering** benefit, not comparative net-surplus proof after apparatus, power, chemicals and waste are charged.

## Eight *future-only* falsifiable research hypotheses (NOT implemented or approved)

- **EA-H1 — division provenance:** withholding direct externally imposed division while maintaining equal accessible feed/exergy will eliminate repeated identifiable daughter compartments; falsifier is multiple physically independent daughters with traced genomes that persist without that partition machinery.
- **EA-H2 — constructor dependence:** withholding fresh **active** RPA enzyme input but permitting raw building blocks will terminate multi-round amplification sooner than an active-enzyme-fed comparator; falsifier is demonstrated sustained replacement of the active constructor with independently measured activity over multiple generations.
- **EA-H3 — net reproductive gain:** after accounting for finite feed, electricity, heat, oil, reagent manufacture/disposal, collection and hardware wear, the published setup may not exceed a cheaper control on any common useful-output metric; falsifier is verified positive net reproductive/output surplus over an attainable simpler control under matched accounting.
- **EA-H4 — propagation fidelity:** equalized gene-copy output does not guarantee heritable functional diversity; falsifier is independently observed, beneficial, functionally assayed sequence variation in descendants beyond genotype-independent fluorescence changes.
- **EA-H5 — independence denominator:** 20 successive rounds represent a single mechanically relayed pedigree per replication run rather than 20 independent founder lineages; falsifier is multiple distinct unscreened founders with fully traced failure-inclusive descendants and comparable outcomes.
- **EA-H6 — observer/sensor learning:** externally scheduled Mg/EDTA and temperature patterns can account for response without internal sensing/history; falsifier is a paid internally maintained sensing/state/action/history mechanism that outperforms fixed-reactive, history-shuffled and memory-disabled controls on previously unseen challenges, with all read/write/energy costs.
- **EA-H7 — damage repair:** after multi-point damage to both genome apparatus and chip-actuator/chemical constructor, external replacement may remain necessary; falsifier is observed internal restoration of **each essential role including its constructor** without specialized human assembly beyond ordinary finite feed.
- **EA-H8 — materially accessible new function:** programmed cycles can transfer an already chosen DNA template without demonstrating a newly acquired useful phenotype; falsifier is independently replicated novel function, inherited by daughters with measured benefit and failures after external intervention withdrawal.

## Decision and handoff

**Retain as a positive physical inheritance benchmark and a strong negative autonomy comparator. Reject as evidence of complete organism/constructor self-maintenance.** This source changes the evaluation rubric by forcing a distinction between (i) DNA presence, (ii) usable inherited DNA-encoded function, (iii) machine-controlled daughter division, and (iv) independent renewal of the apparatus needed to make further daughters. It does not justify new Ora experiments, simulator changes, persistent activation, observer reactivation, new AI model, cost or new schedule.
