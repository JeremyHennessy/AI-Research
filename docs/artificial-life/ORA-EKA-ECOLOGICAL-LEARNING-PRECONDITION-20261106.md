## Decision: prove environmental information value before designing another learner

**Primary concern (hypothesis, NOT demonstrated impossibility):** the current renewed-food ecology may favor cheap location/port reflexes or reactive search sufficiently often that adding acquired memory gives no **fully paid** reproductive advantage. The immediate task is a **source-only ecological information-value audit**, not a new authored learning program, more population seeds, a larger simulation, or adjusted rewards.

### Source-observed mechanics, not a new organism result

- [SharedLineage68 source](https://github.com/JeremyHennessy/Ora2.0/blob/ff8eccf074f782b3d0a1a2f933fd5ae8c196a547/experiments/digital_sharedlineage68_population.py): `input()` offers **four packets per site at each global multiple of 64**, subject to a **32-packet per-site cap**. An offer is not necessarily an admission, and a refill is not proof that a packet remains at a later forage.
- [Pressure71 source](https://github.com/JeremyHennessy/Ora2.0/blob/ff8eccf074f782b3d0a1a2f933fd5ae8c196a547/experiments/digital_pressure71_population.py): for **renewed packets (ID >= 64)** the correct forage port is a function of the hidden seed, site, and the global `tick >= 2048` phase, **not the packet's displayed `kind`**. Consequently the correct port is stable for a site **within a phase** and switches at that phase boundary. Hidden seed/port are **not** free organism observations. An organism's own maximum is 256 dispatched turns; global ticks and organism turns must not be equated after births.
- Each instruction has a one-energy base fee. A successful forage adds up to 20, capped at energy 64. A 27-word nursery requires nine successful COPY dispatches (four energy each including base) plus a DIVIDE dispatch (31 including base), and 43 free material units (27 nursery and 16 new state). A daughter begins with 12 energy **already charged through formation**, not an extra gift or second debit. Do not ignore refused attempts, failed probes, heat/spill, round-robin scheduling, material occupancy, death or expiry.
- Mutation substitutes values in the inherited 27 words under the existing COPY rule; neither a code-length expansion nor an authored winner may be assumed. A newborn begins with blank experience state.

**One-action bound:** for one *available* packet with one stationary correct port, a free-to-stay first-correct-port forage costs one base dispatch; a wrong-port forage followed by XOR and a correct second forage costs three. Thus a perfect recalled first-port decision can save **at most two own dispatches** against that specific opposite-first paired reference, and **zero** against an already-correct first choice, before any acquisition/selection/retrieval costs. This is **not** a whole-lifetime fitness bound or a proof that remembered information is useless. A cheap acquired finite-state memory implementation is a **positive mechanism comparator**, not something a genuine learner must outperform just because it stores data differently.

### Independent scientific literature and its actual scope

1. **Pontes et al. (2020), _The Evolutionary Origin of Associative Learning_, _The American Naturalist_, DOI [10.1086/706252](https://doi.org/10.1086/706252).** In digital nutrient-trail environments, the authors report that stable patterns favor reflexive behavior while patterns differing across generations but predictable within an individual's lifetime favor the evolution of associative learning. They also report reflexive predecessors. This is direct relevant prior art, **not** an experimental reproduction in Ora. The [original Dryad data and configuration](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.f45gh6s) are available but were not downloaded or rerun in this review.
2. **_Evolution takes multiple paths to evolvability when facing environmental change_ (PNAS, 2025), [original paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11725885/).** Digital self-replicating programs can discover multiple pathways to increased evolvability in changing environments. This motivates comparing environment timescales and lawful mutational paths, **not** declaring any pathway present in Ora.
3. **Cicala et al. (2026), _Co-evolution of self-replication and function in a digital primordial soup_, [arXiv:2607.09211](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09211).** Random 32-byte assembly programs and task-weighted interaction produce reported coevolution of reproduction and computation. Its task/interaction selection pressure and aggregate outcomes are not evidence of personally acquired, causally useful memory or Ora's fully paid descendants. Existing [AI-Research draft #65](https://github.com/JeremyHennessy/AI-Research/pull/65) already records a more detailed prior-art interpretation; do not count this as a new replication.
4. **Existing internal full-text timescale-cost review:** [Lambert & Kussell (2014)](66-pass14-lambert-kussell-2014-timescale-memory-full-review.md). Reuse, rather than recreate, its distinction between memory benefit, switching cost and environment timescales.

## One bounded, falsifiable next pass — no World execution

**Step A — actual environmental information opportunity (first gate):** Establish from immutable source and already sealed records which actor-observable cues can predict a *later* food decision beyond (i) the current site, (ii) intact last successful port, (iii) a cheap fixed/alternating/reactive policy and (iv) a location-specific learned finite-state cache. Distinguish within-lifetime uncertainty from cross-seed variation; varying the world seed is not automatically within-lifetime learning. Include phase-2048 crossings only if a *single* lawful organism survives and acts across the boundary. For scarcity, require an *admitted* packet, actual competing removals and exact scheduler interleaving, not nominal offered stock.

**Gate A NO-GO:** All proposed informative histories reduce to an identical or cheaper current-state/reactive decision, no funded contestant can create the required packet asymmetry, or the observable cue cannot arrive early enough for the individual's later profitable decision. Close that *motif*, not the entire substrate.

**Step B — minimal control-cost comparison (only after Gate A):** For two fully matched lawful situations with identical present *observable* cues but different personally obtained histories, specify the correct different decisions, what bit is retained, how it remains valid, how it is retrieved, and *every* own dispatch, phase, energy/spill and movement cost. Compare against correct-first, two-probe, free-stay, latest-success, fully paid reactive and equally cheap location-specific memory controls. If a minimal finite-state cache ties the proposed memory mechanism, that is evidence for a simpler acquired-memory implementation, **not** evidence against learning. A fabricated history or free oracle cue is invalid.

**Step C — complete biological value (only after Gate B):** Require one complete founder-reachable, nine-instruction / 27-word paid find–remember–return–use–COPY–DIVIDE lineage, with *ordinary* mutation provenance, shared-stock competition, occupancy, 256-own-turn expiry, complete parent/newborn energy and material, all failures/deaths and living descendants at the **same preregistered global horizon**. Design intact, cost-matched sham, information-erasure, nonpredictive-cue and independent unseen-context controls. Do **not** execute these arms until new scientific and native admission is explicitly obtained.

**Gate C NO-GO:** Genuine useful information has no net costed descendant advantage against strong controls, or the purported phenotype cannot arise and survive by legal mutation/selection. If Gate A or B fails, do not build a learner for this motif.

**Stop-and-decide output:** one small table with source-pinned time/site/food/observation/energy/age/mutation/competitor/offspring columns, any impossible or unknown premises explicitly marked; one **PASS / NO-GO / UNKNOWN** verdict for the specific opportunity; and the *single* next proof obligation. A new draft, more hash counts or an engineered positive program is not milestone completion.
