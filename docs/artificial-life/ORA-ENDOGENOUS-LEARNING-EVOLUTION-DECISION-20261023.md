# Ora independent science decision — evolve learning rules, not just trained behaviours

**Decision date:** 2026-10-23  
**Decision type:** Strategic research direction; a falsifiable hypothesis, NOT a verified scientific discovery, world-first claim, new experiment admission, runtime activation, or authorization to change the live assistant.  
**Companion engineering handoff:** [Ora2.0 endogenous-learning strategy](https://github.com/JeremyHennessy/Ora2.0/blob/main/docs/ENDOGENOUS-LEARNING-EVOLUTION-STRATEGY-20261023.md).

## Independent conclusion

Ora should prioritize **endogenously evolved, useful information processing** in a bounded digital ecology, not the largest or most impressive provided learning algorithm. Maintain the existing supervised world-continuation engineering track, but treat uptime and observer graphics as infrastructure rather than evidence of self-evolving intelligence.

The initial research question is whether legal organism code variation plus ordinary feedback, memory access, code writing, paid copying and genuine ecological resource pressure can produce **a new lineage-relevant information strategy** that was absent from the supplied ancestor.

Our proposed signature experiment is **EKA-01: Endogenous Knowledge Allocation**.

> Under resource constraints and changing information lifetimes, can genome variation and organism-executed decisions produce a useful, heritable change in **where information lives**—a fixed encoded rule, private experience, or a code change transmitted to offspring—without a developer-installed solution, privileged reward labels or posthoc winner insertion?

This is an *original proposed combination and causal question for Ora*, **not certified worldwide novelty**. Prior work already studies evolved learning, the Baldwin effect, genetic assimilation, open-ended evolution and cultural transmission. Discovery would require evidence of a mechanism or result not already explained by those baselines.

## Why this decision, based on current evidence

**Ora substrate (published evidence, not new experiments):**

- Mobile54/Pressure71-style worlds have a compact interpreted program and bounded resources. Existing legal VM primitives include feedback, memory READ/WRITE, branch, self-write to the organism's own code, COPY, division, movement, messages, and local energy transfer. These are authored substrate affordances, not evolved learning.
- Longitudinal01 showed two **finite 8,192-dispatch histories**, each reaching 8 generations with 35 paid births. The ancestor was supplied and mutation disabled: engineering continuity, not improvement by natural selection.
- Inheritance83 observed paid inherited variation and complete population histories, but **failed** its predeclared beneficial-selection criteria. The q8 informative variant had very sparse paid offspring opportunities; six of eight candidate parents died before dividing.
- Learning81 independently reproduced scoped actor-acquired cue knowledge, but retained energy failed against efficient compact references (480 versus 664 and 661).
- Context90 q8 passed its specified own-old-history retrieval criterion; smaller variants and *all* full paid nominal/operational/formation-inclusive energy advantages failed. Its generic learner/source were supplied.
- PILOT74 prospective finite same-population recovery/Windows custody remains unqualified at full production scope. An authorized native qualification failure stopped *before any genome/world step* because of text output framing; repair is source-qualified but not a valid rescore.
- The separate persistent local Ollama **assistant** has approved signed memory and operating windows; it is not the evolving digital population. Model-answer/prompt-memory improvements confer no artificial-life stage credit.
- Older AgentTest is independent. Its Phase42 read-only prediction shadows and lossless snapshot/Observer mechanics are useful **measurement lessons only**. The original heartbeat watchdog reached its 50-attempt recovery limit; no live continual-evolution result transfers to Ora.

**What is actually limiting scientific progress:** A learned decision is not the same as an economically useful trait; a born mutant is not evidence of beneficial inheritance; a saved World is not an evolving ecology. New elaborate model gadgets have repeatedly improved local decisions without outperforming smaller competitors when all resource costs are counted. The q8 useful phenotype is not automatically reproductively viable.

**Novelty audit rule:** For each proposed new result, write both (1) which mechanism or result differs from the cited work and (2) the smallest already-known explanation or algorithm that could account for the observation. A new acronym, custom opcode, big performance number, or new arrangement of familiar modules is not automatically a new discovery.

## Research hypothesis EKA-01 — gradual evolution of where knowledge is stored

Ora already distinguishes:
- **G (germline/encoded behaviour):** paid organism program words copied into offspring;
- **E (experience):** private paid observations, feedback, registers and writable memory that a newborn does not automatically inherit;
- **P (program rewrite):** organism-executed self-write followed by legal paid COPY; a potential bridge from within-life information to heritable program variation. Its existing presence in the opcode set is *not* proof any population has discovered or used such a bridge usefully;
- **C (culture):** in-world exchanged information, with sender/receiver attribution, not yet established;
- **H (human references):** curated external information, not organism-created knowledge, also not yet a lawful VM input.

**Core test:** Can variants that use G/E/P differently arise from **neutral genotype mutation**, experience authentic feedback, survive ordinary costs, and produce descendants with a *measurable, causally attributable new advantage* on new environments?

The simplest candidate **does not install a new q8 trainer, a special programmer species, a winner genome, an oracle hint, a magic energy refill or a learned opcode**. Instead use the existing VM affordances and a task with measurable, realistically attainable information opportunity. Native genome code/learning-action changes must originate from actual mutation and organism-executed instructions.

This is risky: an accessible useful genotype may not exist under current fixed-length 27-word programs, mutation rate, energy and time limits. The research **must first screen physical and mutational attainability** from reviewed source without opening a new science bank.

A useful success would be an independently reproduced lineage showing a previously absent *functional* program/use pattern which arises naturally, is inherited, improves fully paid descendant success versus controls, and fails under the specifically relevant causal lesion. If only feedback-derived register values improve a same-life choice, that is Learning81-like scoped individual learning, not this claim.

## Required tests, in order

### EKA-0: zero-World feasibility and mechanism audit

1. Review exact current VM, code length/materials, opcode accessibility, altered-copy probabilities, paid food/action budgets, first viable reproduction, descendant contexts, actual feedback channel and post-copy newborn state.
2. Identify a feasible sequence of legal genotype changes leading to an optional *new use* of sensing/remembering/self-write/copy, without transplanting that solution into a scientific founder. Source-only combinatorial reachability or a hand-coded hypothetical phenotype is a **feasibility witness**, not evolved evidence.
3. Confirm the task supplies sufficient distinct personally observed information before mutation/selection; if unexposed q8 parents die or information becomes irrelevant before birth, stop. Check cheaper reflex/reactive solutions first.
4. Calculate full nominal N, operating O and formation-inclusive E bounds separately, including opportunity/exposure probability, cap heat, deaths, instruction fees, code copy and energy lost by reproduction. If no route can cost-cover its prerequisites, do not run.
5. Draft original-source hypothesis, contrary predictions and stopping rule. Preserve the existing source and closed scientific cohorts.

**Kill gate:** no physically reachable opportunity or no nontrivial, cost-covering candidate accessible under the honest mutation process. No arbitrary horizon, resource or mutation-injection adjustment just to manufacture a pass.

### EKA-1: first hereditary usefulness trial (newly preregistered only)

- Use **natural mutation and ordinary paid reproduction** with unselected starting ancestors, complete populations and both independent and unseen contexts. New family/seed/VM changes need their own prospective source+cost+qualification gate.
- Essential comparisons: natural-variation vs no-variation; inheritance enabled vs physically cost-matched blocked inheritance; program self-write path enabled vs matched write-disabled/disabled-effect control; private experience intact vs causal erasure, plus competitive fixed/reactive/short-code reference policies.
- Retain every dead, unexposed or incompletely copied entity. No outcome-selected q8 parent, no post-outcome repair, no prefilled child memory, no independent scorer-generated instructions.
- Track actual mutation birth and expression, actor personally earned information, self-write provenance, exact copied words, descendant code/feedback/newborn blank state, lifetime performance, material/energy accounting and descendants after founder death.
- Show **causal inherited functional value**, not just increased birth count or a visual new pattern. Test a genotype on reserved environmental variations without injecting the learned state.

### EKA-2: evolution of adaptive information routing (only after EKA-1 effect)

- Ask whether *organism-derived* code changes make information handling conditional on cost/information lifetime, not just permanently encode one profitable answer.
- Register alternating stationary/reversing niches in which different strategies have honest opportunity; include an independent feasible simple competitor and context-shift failure cases.
- Compare with developer-authored policies off to the side (as reference programs, **never organisms seeded into the population**). A model-generated code variant is explicitly externally authored, not endogenous.
- Do not call an arbitrary source rewrite, random novelty, entropy increase or self-instruction loop an evolved algorithm. Identify the line of descent and which functional routine was genuinely absent from the ancestor.

### EKA-3: ecological inheritance and culture (later, distinct admissions)

- Only after robust EKA-1/2 results, evaluate whether organisms can construct useful durable environmental artifacts and communicate observations to others through the existing bounded in-world message affordances.
- Every interaction pays bytes, instruction, material, opportunity and reproduction costs. Show recipient benefit versus no teaching, shuffled/inaccurate teaching and personal learning controls, and show whether the transferred knowledge genuinely accumulates.
- Curated human publications (K0 roadmap) remain reference baselines unless a separately controlled, paid knowledge-acquisition interface has independently passed. No privileged scientific truths in organism packets.

## Priority decision

**Track A — engineering gate, time-boxed:** finish the existing legitimate PILOT74 native/custody/finite same-population recovery qualifications. The goal is exact, safe bounded continuation; do not promote 24/7 uptime or animation into science. Preserve failure receipts, one-writer authority and separate startup approval. If progress stalls, stop expanding integration and write down the single blocking gate.

**Track B — principal research (parallel, cheap first):** EKA-0 source-only feasibility and the minimum EKA-1 prospective design. This is the **highest scientific priority**. It may conclude NO-GO. No huge population or broad random trials before the physics and mutation-accessibility screen.

**Track C — supporting knowledge:** keep AI-Research evidence and K0 capsule work available as a critical reference baseline; don't implement a direct research-to-organism library, LLM-mediated organism actions, full language understanding or multiple shared caches yet.

**Optional substrate exit:** if reviewed physical/finite mutational reachability establishes that the current VM cannot support meaningful evolutionary exploration under reasonable resources, compare one alternative substrate under matched conditions (e.g., a smaller combinatorial instruction set or mass-conserving local interaction rules). This is not authority for a broad world rewrite.

## Decision rubric

A claimed advancement must answer four separate questions:
1. Did a **previously absent useful behaviour** arise without a hand-inserted winning controller?
2. Was it **actually expressed and transmitted** to descendants through paid reproduction, with no privileged inheritance?
3. Did it provide a **replicated paid reproductive or survival advantage** over fair simple baselines on new contexts?
4. Did the advantage depend on the causal **information or structural mechanism** identified, rather than hidden labels, extra resources, posthoc selection or an external language model?

No claim of evolving intelligence, original scientific invention, or open-endedness if these remain NO/UNKNOWN. A failed experiment is valuable as a bound on the substrate.

## Operations and safety

Ora organisms remain abstract VM data and actions inside a bounded, isolated simulation. No host Python/shell/network/model administration, repo edits, external purchases or autonomous deployment. Do not automatically self-modify actual host code. Keep existing task/guardian/native constraints, archive immutability, independent referee and read-only observer boundaries; the human owner authorizes production launches.
