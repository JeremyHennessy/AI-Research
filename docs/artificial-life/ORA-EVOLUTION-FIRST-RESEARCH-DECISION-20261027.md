## 1. Research conclusion and why the priority changes

**Decision:** seek the smallest organism-native, personally acquired, economically useful and ordinarily heritable information-handling capability before expanding the simulated world or adding elaborate learning mechanisms. Treat EKA as **one falsifiable hypothesis**, not a mandatory architecture.

Current evidence supports heredity and narrow code expression. It does not establish that a new information routine is useful, reachable through viable intermediate mutations, independently repeated or selected for its **fully paid descendant fitness**.

The [Ora noisy-credit frozen negative](https://github.com/JeremyHennessy/Ora2.0/blob/140c40c42f0540cf7914d89ea4ca592afe470cfe/prototypes/noisy_credit/RESULT.md) is particularly informative: its authored delayed-credit learner survived 0/32 reserved cases, while a cheap fixed strategy survived 32/32. Its 12,896 evaluations were isolated controllers (zero births/mutations). Learning establishment and early energy cash flow failed in this design. This is a **candidate-specific NO-GO**, not a general impossibility for native evolved learning. The original 4,096-controller census and Context90/Inheritance83 limitations remain separate observations.

Native EKA-1 original-event provenance is **not missing from a known authentic executed original**: the proposed original assignment/grant, physical producer, complete selected pre-READ state and derivative continuation have not yet been generated/authenticated under registered native qualification. Draft [Ora #323](https://github.com/JeremyHennessy/Ora2.0/pull/323) head 48dee3ee6dc6b8ecb1c98bc690ebb806475a7321 defines preparation/semantic panels only; ten external proof fields are null. No formal new population is justified. Source-level preparatory checks do not confer a scientific acceptance gate.

**Current overall verdict:** EKA substrate feasibility UNKNOWN, new native population NO-GO. The persistent World is not authenticated LIVE. PILOT74 and separate assistant operations do not change this verdict.

## 2. The minimal scientific question

**Thesis to test eventually:** Is there a legally actor-observable, *predictive* difference between two situations whose present visible inputs are identical but whose useful later actions depend on the organism's own earlier feedback? Can an affordable minimal rule exploit it while paying for acquisition, retention, action, failed actions, COPY and daughter construction, and can ordinary mutation generate and maintain that rule?

Before requesting a new native assay, independently answer the cheaper questions:

- **Opportunity:** Are two such histories possible under frozen actor-observable source laws, before expiry/death and with reachable future resources? Compute a bound or mark UNKNOWN. A secret analyst-derived target does not count as an organism-observable cue.
- **Economic value:** Does the *best justified* gross benefit exceed unavoidable formation and operation costs, including early time-to-first-reward, missed resources, overflow, expiry, material acquisition and subsequent birth? Separate an existence witness from expected selection advantage.
- **Mutational corridor:** Is expression through ordinary mutations and viable intermediate reproduction plausible? A hand-designed 9-slot or compact 3-edit path is not itself an evolved route. Counts of VM steps are not counts of candidate mutant births.
- **Transmission:** Is the acquired information in private state, copied genetic code, persistent environment, or an external source? Distinguish an inherited capacity to learn from a fixed inherited answer, received assistance, and genuine cultural accumulation.

Do not insist that information must only help on the same site. But require any cross-site predictive relationship to be source-legal, accessible to the actor and economically tested rather than assumed.

## 3. Four hypotheses and decisive counterexamples

| Hypothesis | Evidence that would support it | Falsifier or alternative explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1: history adds usable predictive information | Source-legal pair of histories with matched present inputs but a different later profitable action, distinguishable by earned earlier feedback | Present cue/clock/location/last feedback already solves it; nonpredictive cue performs equally well |
| H2: cheap learning pays its way | Prospectively frozen, held-out net survival/resource superiority over efficient fixed/reactive strategies with matched costs | High accuracy from subsidized formation, more observations, lucky reward access, invalid cost exchange or low-power selective survivors |
| H3: viable mutation supplies the trait | Ordinary lineage origin, expressed new behaviour before death, paid birth and descendant expression across true denominators | Hand-injected solution, posthoc winner selection, only lethal intermediates, mutation-free supplied optimizer |
| H4: selection favors learned capacity | Causal loss on selective own-history disruption, legal transmission, independent descendant benefit in unseen cases and separate origins | Fixed inherited answer, generic resource transfer, state-damage lesion, direct delayed reflex, observer oracle or matched-seed pseudoreplication |

**Key nulls:** matched information-predictive versus nonpredictive cue, no-information cost-matched comparator, intact versus erased personal history, identity sham, accurate versus corrupted feedback, fixed0/fixed1/alternator/last-feedback/cheap reactive policies, and genotype/SELFWRITE/inheritance controls as appropriate. XOR alone is a corruption test, **not** an information-free sensor reference. An intervention changing survival energy or material must include cost/sham analysis.

If a claimed information benefit persists with uninformative cues, it does **not** support the stated information mechanism. If no legal discriminatory opportunity exists in the specified candidate ecology, terminate that candidate rather than introducing a sophisticated learned decoder.

## 4. Evidence tiers and claim ceiling

1. **SOURCE-ONLY:** Parsed VM code, cost bounds, informational inequalities, and synthetic validation. Verifies consistency or necessary constraints, not that an original executed under native control.
2. **ISOLATED-SOFTWARE:** A preregistered, resource-bounded authored controller beats/loses to controls. Proves that exact authored policy and abstract ecology result; not natural mutation or paid reproduction.
3. **AUTHENTIC NATIVE ORIGINAL:** Actual original-owner assignment, producer/raw/EOF/source/interpreter/compiled-code and prestate custody, complete native resource fit and registered semantic trials. Proves execution identity and allowed causal testing only.
4. **EVOLUTIONARY MECHANISM:** Legal uninjected mutational origin, retained all-death/all-birth denominators, predeclared information-specific causal lesion, actual paid reproduction and descendants, unseen validation.
5. **INDEPENDENT SCIENTIFIC REPLICATION:** Independently registered originals, true experimental sampling units, power/uncertainty and multiple lineages with the result surviving strong alternative hypotheses.

A lower tier cannot be called a higher one because it has more observations or tests. A source pin is not producer proof; a population completing windows is not continuing on its own or becoming more intelligent.

## 5. Prospective minimal comparison (NOT an experiment registration)

Only **after** the source/opportunity/affordability gates justify further work, propose one bounded information task with:
- Immutable source, initial ordinary founders, legal mutation law, original-owner role boundaries, and independent reserved environments before results.
- Predictive and nonpredictive versions matched in food, timing, resources and reward; no cue-oracle supplied to the organism.
- Equal endowment and complete formation/read/write/update/energy/material/transfer/expiry/failed action costs for efficient blind, reactive, minimal-memory and any authored learning ceilings.
- Complete World-level results, all natural births, unexpressed mutants, nonreaders, failures, no-opportunity rows and independent lineages; shared seeds/branches are correlated blocks.
- Prespecified primary metric of **fully paid descendant advantage**, not simply correct predictions. Separate a prerequisite within-life net benefit analysis from the eventual evolutionary endpoint.
- Prospective stopping, meaningful effect size, power, uncertainty, multiplicity and independence checks defined by the experiment owner/reviewer, not invented in hindsight.
- Control interventions that distinguish a fixed inherited answer from a new capacity to acquire information. No tuning against unseen validation results.

This is a research template, **not authority** to run 1 World, select a winner, qualify a native job, replay a sealed producer, or revise existing frozen experiments.

## 6. When to stop, pivot, or preserve UNKNOWN

**NO-GO for the specified task/mechanism** if the requisite historical signal has zero actor-accessible predictive value, if a defensible upper-bound benefit cannot cover necessary costs, or if a preregistered held-out comparison fails. Keep the full failed corpus and do not rescue it by increasing food, lifespan, founder energy, unregistered seed count or computing budget.

**UNKNOWN for the substrate** if opportunities, ordinary mutation supply, full native formation costs or authenticated originals are not established. Say which single observation would be most informative. UNKNOWN is not permission to start a large blind evolutionary search.

**Pivot permitted as proposal only:** if the fixed native ecology itself lacks an affordable corridor, compare exactly one smallest structural alternative against the *unchanged native baseline* with independently declared priors and costs. Do not replace approved VM laws, add a neural model by default, or claim alternative-substrate merit merely from parameter flexibility.

One negative rule tests one rule. Two separate scientific negatives do not imply universal impossibility unless their assumptions and coverage yield a formal impossibility argument.

## 7. Prior art: novelty stays narrow

- [Lenski et al., Nature 2003](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature01568) already investigated complex digital functions through evolution: a digital instruction performing something useful is not novel in general.
- [Hinton & Nowlan, Baldwin effect illustration](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~hinton/absps/evolution.htm) separates inherited capacity/selection from transfer of individually learned content; do not claim assimilation without the relevant causal proof.
- [Yaman et al., plasticity optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.01709) and the existing [Arnold et al. 2026 abstract review](EKA-0-INDEPENDENT-CRITIQUE-20261023.md) anticipate evolved learning rules; an authored memory controller is not a new evolved mechanism.
- [Knafo, Casacuberta & Ruiz-Trillo, PRX Life 2026](https://doi.org/10.1103/5p5z-t8qy) was identified by the October 27 [coordination review](https://github.com/JeremyHennessy/Ora2.0/issues/317). **Evidence limit:** publisher abstract/bibliographic metadata were read, not full methods/raw data; its claim about predictive cues and model-specific noise is a falsifier motivation only. Do not transfer a numerical stochasticity threshold into Ora prices or claim replication.
- Earlier AI-Research reviews of ecological/evolutionary affordances remain the comparative library. Do not open another broad reading campaign unless a new source supplies a falsifier the current decision lacks.

A future Ora result would first be a **narrow, independently audited finding in this particular substrate**, not a field-wide first or evidence of general intelligence.
