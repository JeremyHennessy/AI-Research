## Temporal89 diagnosis is finished: do not repeat it

The new bounded source-free retained-history reader inspected all400 originals, including240 learner histories and160 references. It reconstructed2481 qualified origins,43 changed uses across arms,192 registered contrasts and178844 instructions, with no new World or queried target label. This is a closed-data diagnosis, not fresh experimental replication or another physics referee.

- Smaller memories interfered: intact q2 cross-cue overwrites165 warm/55 late; q4 92/36; q8 zero. This diagnoses the supplied indexing scheme, not universal memory limits.
- All17 q8 opportunities lost to model-zero and all9 lost to recall-disable were first late visits to a personally observed changed association. New feedback was not yet available. A lucky default can win the first prediction after an unobserved change; no method can guarantee anticipating an independent hidden change.
- All33 intact q8 late updates correctly bound cue and chosen action. An action ledger is not a demonstrated fix for this source.
- The controls affect different paths. Credit erasure and recall disable can disrupt updater/action consistency differently; preserve their specifications and negative acceptance results rather than repair or rescore Temporal89.

## Current work: Context90 source, not an admitted experiment

Context90 asks a distinct question: can personally earned evidence select, revise and reuse an older model when familiar conditions return, rather than repeatedly overwrite and relearn? Temporal89 never returned to an earlier context.

Context90 source-only checks have now completed:147456 exhaustive abstract cases across all6 neutral descriptors, with two original/restored native exports and exact archive restoration. These are abstract source/dataflow/cost checks with zero Worlds or targets, not ordinary-world survival, task affordability or scientific context recognition. No scientific task/cohort, exact source CI for an experiment, execution registration or learning outcome is claimed here.

The proposed source uses two initially blank model routes and its own feedback to choose when to update and switch. The routes can overlap in physical memory, and an error alone does not identify a changed context. Source implementation, cost feasibility and actual retention must therefore be checked before a scientific cohort. Switching machinery is supplied; recognizing a returning context remains unproven.

Immediate decision sequence:

1. Reuse the now-completed source/dataflow/address-collision and abstract cost checks. Do not rerun them unchanged or repeat old history/broad-suite checks. The derived conditional60-loop constructor bounds are1101/1103/1107 for the three capacities; these abstract bounds do not certify resource timing or survival.
2. Establish that the source can actually retain older information and has affordable, informative reuse opportunities against efficient reactive and overwrite-only alternatives. A passing source test alone does not establish this. Preserve infeasibility if overlap or timing defeats the question.
3. If feasible, freeze one distinct returning-versus-novel-context task, actual-channel controls, unseen confirmation, complete denominators and finite budgets before targets. No public predictable return clock, host context identifier, warm-state insertion, outcome-selected cues/entry/capacity or hidden free confidence.
4. Require exact source CI and separate bounded execution registration before a World. Report qualification, admission, launch and scientific results separately.

No additional feature layer or architecture rewrite is justified merely by a failed comparison. First identify the representation or opportunity limit it would solve.

## What counts as progress

Report four separate answers: functional own-experience benefit; actual computation/resource cost; useful inheritance/selection; and runtime persistence. Nominal decision score, captured energy and retained formation-inclusive energy are different quantities. Energy caps/spill and finite opportunities must remain visible. Retained-energy superiority is not a universal requirement for intelligence, but a cost-covering or fitness claim requires the corresponding measured advantage.

An appropriate next functional milestone is evidence-driven reuse of personally learned older information beyond immediate feedback and overwrite-only learning, under unseen conditions and actual-channel controls. Recognition of change, appropriate switching and cost-covering advantage remain unproven. Supplied switching machinery and invented witnesses cannot satisfy that milestone.

Persistent-world engineering can progress separately; the Observer remains recorded playback and no continuous world is activated by this note. Save/replay/process-interruption evidence does not certify physical power-loss or off-drive durability. Evolution remains a separate paid-fitness hypothesis, not a gate on within-life learning.
