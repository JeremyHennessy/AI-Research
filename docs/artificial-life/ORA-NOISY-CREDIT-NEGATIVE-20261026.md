# A supplied compact learner fails the frozen economic gate

The new isolated noisy delayed-credit assay on [Ora PR325](https://github.com/JeremyHennessy/Ora2.0/pull/325)
is a **closed negative**, independently reviewed before and after outcomes.
It does not establish useful learning, evolutionary improvement or inherited
fitness. Native EKA feasibility remains UNKNOWN; native populations remain NO-GO.

This is distinct from the closed 4,096 binary-controller census: four recurring
cues, four actions, noisy success, delayed food, changing hidden mappings,
limited energy, paid formation/storage/sensing/updates and independent validation.
Food8 was frozen before outcomes and not retuned. Optimistic counter learning
was supplied, not discovered. Six counter configurations competed only by
development score; no population, mutation, copying or offspring ran.

Selected counter3-drop1 survived 1/16 development worlds and 0/32 validation
worlds. The development-selected constant3 reference survived 32/32 validation
worlds, mean terminal energy443.40625 versus0. Candidate paired wins0/32;
true/clamped/erased/misattributed experience all had terminal scores0. Strong
contextual win-stay/lose-shift survived2/32, mean2.125. An expensive cumulative
estimator failed formation; this is not a refutation of optimized estimators.
All264 references, including256 cue-fixed maps, remain in validation records.
Total12,896 isolated lifetimes:4,320 development,8,448 validation references,
128 causal arms. All failures retained. No seed banks or native Worlds read.

The selected learner paid80 formation energy, leaving48 of128. Steady recurring
bill5.0625 requires success above0.6328125 at reward8, before formation recovery
and timing losses. Its488 successful attempts/1048 attempts≈0.466 are censored
by death; only456 paid food arrivals occurred. None of32 lifetimes completed.
Every ledger conserved energy. True feedback extended some partial lives,
but true-versus-erased attempt wins15/ties15/losses2 do not rescue the failed
endpoint. Do not report that descriptive difference as net learning success.

This supports an establishment/cash-flow bottleneck for THIS learner, task and
declared price model. It does not prove insufficient VM expressiveness, a
universal economic impossibility or that more evolutionary time would fix it.
Perfect preferred-action choice has success probability0.8 and could cover the
recurring bill; acquiring that competence affordably is unproven. Cheap blind
action has positive expected flow, so a learning advantage cannot be presumed.
No reproductive or descendant advantage has been demonstrated.

Strategy **C**, with limited independent tracks: retain native authentication
with its owner; require isolated learning to clear an efficient-control and
within-life survival gate before paying for evolutionary-population work.
A genuinely different cheaper credit mechanism needs prospective review and
fresh validation. Do not rescue this attempt by increasing food, founder energy
or rewarding selected outcomes. Larger neural scaffolds are not justified by
this result alone. No approved architecture is replaced.

Prior research shows why learning and evolution must be separated. Yaman et al.
explicitly optimize supplied plasticity-rule encodings with a genetic algorithm
([primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.01709)); Miconi evolves plastic networks
and evaluates acquisition of novel cognitive tasks
([primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.08588)). Neither establishes spontaneous
learning in Ora or pays Ora's native reproduction bill. Our assay has not
reproduced either evolutionary result; no novelty claim follows from a new task.
