## What the next Avida comparison can and cannot establish

[Lenski et al. 2003, *The Evolutionary Origin of Complex Features*](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature01568) is primary evidence that a complex computational function (EQU) arose through selectable intermediate steps in Avida, under authored merit/fitness rules. The study reports 50 experimental populations, 23 positive for EQU. These are not proof that *Ora* is capable of the same outcome, that evolved useful computation is unprecedented, or that a genotype tree is an event-verified birth ledger.

[Avida official source](https://github.com/devosoft/avida) includes genotype classification and analysis infrastructure; the [ALife standard Avida converter](https://github.com/alife-data-standards/converters-avida) documents `.spop` files with extant and ancestral **genotypes**. It includes example archives unrelated to the original 2003 experiment. Provenance must establish which original 2003 genomes/configurations/pivotal genotypes survive, and what individual birth histories are unavailable. Missing historical original records stay UNKNOWN. Do not silently substitute later Avida configurations or example archives.
