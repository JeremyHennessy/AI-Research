# PHYSICAL-FORMATION-01 — heat-flow compartmentalization versus self-renewal

**Research-only, unnumbered primary-literature critique.** Prepared 2026-10-10. This document does not authorize or describe an AI-Research or Ora experiment, deployment, model, organism, persistent pilot, or observer activation.

## Original physical effect

Sustained heat flow across a structured environment produces thermal and convective concentration and confinement of pre-existing soluble/macromolecular components. A localized region can accumulate supplied biological machinery sufficiently to support an in-vitro protein synthesis reaction. Spatial concentration and the observed biochemical activity are physically meaningful, not purely numerical novelty. The available environmental thermal-gradient power is an ongoing externally maintained energetic input; it is not free exergy or spontaneous energy generation. The paper's long-duration (six-month) retention claim is a **model-derived projection**, not a six-month directly observed run of continuously renewing machinery.

## Apparatus ancestry and intervention boundary

The original test begins with supplied nucleic acids, protein synthesis machinery or precursors, vessel geometries and a maintained physical temperature gradient. It does **not** demonstrate de novo production and renewal of all essential translational machinery, energy-coupling systems, containment devices and the constructor itself. Nor does the paper present full input/output accounting for sustaining the gradient, feed replenishment, thermal losses, wear, repair and export of waste. The mechanism can concentrate already functional parts without having constructed or continuously repaired them.

The rival explanation is ordinary gradient-driven accumulation and consumption of premade molecular stock. A more expensive gradient device may lose to a simpler attainable chamber, passive concentration, or direct reagent-mixing comparator when all facilities and energy are charged. No numeric net-positive operating cost should be inferred from missing costs: **unknown is not zero**.

## Future-only tests, not implemented

- **PF-H1:** removal of the sustained gradient reduces confinement and functional expression. *Falsifier:* equal persistence under gradient-free source-accounted conditions.
- **PF-H2:** maintained concentration requires continued heat flux whose total cost overwhelms a suitably simpler control. *Falsifier:* paid end-to-end ledger shows robust net benefit.
- **PF-H3:** depleted translation machinery is not regenerated by the physical concentration mechanism. *Falsifier:* isotope/ancestry-traced production of all required roles from bounded feedstock without supplied replacements.
- **PF-H4:** the six-month simulated retention duration overstates directly observed biochemical continuity. *Falsifier:* independently replicated continuous measurements beyond predicted bounds.
- **PF-H5:** no failure-inclusive daughters maintain functional renewal after specialist intervention withdrawal. *Falsifier:* multiple independent traced generations sustain full function without continued operator assembly.
- **PF-H6:** an attainable simpler chamber or mixing process matches effective useful output at lower fully paid cost. *Falsifier:* successful preregistered positive-surplus comparison favoring the complex gradient setup.

**Research decision:** retain as one physical localization mechanism. Do not confuse expression of supplied components with birth or reproduction of a system that renews all parts.
