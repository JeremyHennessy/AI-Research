## 1. One strategic decision

**Next progress means ruling in or ruling out a physically coherent, fully paid, independently renewable mechanism — NOT publishing more loosely connected findings.** We have genuine positive component results and many informative failures; none of the audited sources by itself establishes the complete conjunction of:

1. **Accessible raw formation and renewal of ALL essential roles, including the constructor that makes them.** Track fresh identity after meaningful damage and who/what actually makes each indispensable component.
2. **A complete, physically possible finite-source-to-retained-useful-work pathway.** Account for available work/exergy, matter, heat, waste, transport, separation, return, actuation, storage, wear and replenishment. An isolated high efficiency/velocity/ATP-output number is insufficient.
3. **An actually attainable, profitable fully paid simpler stationary/passive comparator on the SAME feed** plus meaningful no-function and history controls. A hypothetical or loss-making control cannot establish candidate advantage.
4. **Independent failure-inclusive continued reproduction after specialist assistance is withdrawn** only if the earlier prerequisites are present. Adaptation and consciousness claims need entirely separate evidence.
