# Unified scientific synthesis — Research Passes 1–28

**Status: research-only integration, 2026-10-09.** This is the **single forward-looking synthesis and question-priority document**, not a replacement for the [28-stage publication index](PASS-1-28-PUBLICATION-INDEX.md), original source reviews, [append-only research log](../../data/research-log.md), canonical source/claim registries or immutable [EERC v1–v7](../../data/alife/combined-path-eerc-v7.json). No new EERC version is proposed, no source is upgraded by synthesis, and no original experiment has been performed here.

## 1. The question and the result so far

**Unifying question:** Under fixed, resource-accounted substrate rules, can interacting components *endogenously* make a previously inaccessible **useful function** reachable, maintain the organization responsible, transmit that function through independently viable descendants without special researcher assistance, and later adapt to unforeseen ecological change?

**Current answer:** The 28 published research stages supply **partial mechanisms and substantial counterevidence**, but **no one examined system has established this full conjunction**. Observed cooperation, genomic symbiont persistence, within-individual memory, a new colony shape, host development, daughter transmission and predicted metabolic function are distinct observations. They cannot be added together as if they came from one reproduced organism or one independent experimental cohort.

The working hypothesis family remains [EERC](53-pass11-new-path-ecological-reproductive-closure.md) and its separately preserved [v7 falsifiers](../../data/alife/combined-path-eerc-v7.json). **EERC is an original unverified research proposal, not a validated theory, implementation or architecture mandate.** Alternatives remain competitive.

**Gates are separable, not a compulsory ladder.** An association can transmit and harm its host; a cell can remember without reproducing; a lineage can reproduce without creating new ecological functions. Failure at one gate does not negate a genuine result at another.

**Negative evidence is not a defect to tune away.** Important retained failures include Stringmol extinctions and semantic incompatibility, AlChemy's higher-order coexistence barrier, induced symbiosis washout after external selection, selection-dependent transmission, harmful transmitted symbionts, and nonnative egg-packaging failure. ## 4. Three genuinely competing research explanations

1. **EERC relational-interface hypothesis:** inhabitant-produced ecological constraints make a new useful function selectable; a self-maintained interface carries that relationship into daughters. This is a *candidate*, not an established answer. [Original](53-pass11-new-path-ecological-reproductive-closure.md) · [v7](../../data/alife/combined-path-eerc-v7.json).
2. **Component-only adaptation null:** all observed gains arise from ordinary genetic/physiological evolution of components, generic nutrients, and externally supplied copying or packaging; no new higher-level individual is necessary. A matched resource rescue, random partner reassignment or interface ablation that leaves descendant function intact would favor this explanation.
3. **Passive/dynamical-structure null:** long-lived boundaries, ecological traces and apparent memory are physical attractors, relaxation or survivor filtering rather than an evolving reproductive organization or learned controller. Compare inert/passive analogues, time-matched decay and genuine parent→daughter causal ancestry.

No hypothesis wins from conceptual elegance. The decisive evidence must discriminate **the same causal question** with properly independent units and predeclared failure cases, not combine favorable statistics from different papers.

## 5. The one highest-information unanswered question

**Can a newly established, experimentally traceable partnership preserve a net-beneficial function across multiple unassisted reproductive generations after the researcher stops transfers, sorting, selective rescue and partner replenishment — while including all failed founders?**

This is a **literature and evidence-reconciliation question only** under present authorization. Before proposing any new experiment, find existing published studies with:

- Exact founder identity and independent cohort count; nesting of cells, daughters, hosts, lines, assays and repeated samples.
- When interventions cease, whether partners are reintroduced, whether a propagule/egg package is built by the parent, and whether the combined lineage survives beyond one transfer.
- Joint measurement of **offspring partner acquisition, offspring reproductive success, actual useful function and net costs**. No adult-emergence-only, PCR-only or survival-conditioned shortcut.
- Failed founders/extinctions, matched ancestral/native/control arms, study reuse and all source-data discrepancies.
- Explicit negative outcomes after selection withdrawal; separate **natural historical acquisition** from investigator-induced founder establishment.
- Source rights, exact article/supplement version, E1/E2 limits, available raw data and independent replication status.

**Stop criterion for documentary review:** if no study simultaneously reports these measurements, record the missing joint evidence rather than claim a mechanism has been demonstrated. A new experimental design, if ever proposed, must be separately approved and preregistered; do not execute it here.

## 6. Intelligence and consciousness are parallel, not inferred, tracks

The [Pass 9 conceptual separation](42-pass9-pathways-to-life-mind-and-consciousness.md) remains authoritative:

- **Adaptive intelligence:** evaluate novel, resource-bounded, history-conditioned problem solving and transfer with blinded perturbations, while separating within-life learning from inherited fixed responses. Neither an LLM benchmark nor a physiological memory kernel alone establishes this.
- **Phenomenal consciousness:** compare explicitly competing theories (e.g., global workspace, recurrent processing, higher-order and integrated-information approaches) by **different predicted observables** and counterexamples. No accepted substrate-neutral test currently proves subjective experience; never award a consciousness status from complexity, recursion, speech, self-report or reproductive success.
- **Conscience/normative behavior:** study stable reciprocal constraints, learned social commitments and generalization separately; rule compliance does not prove experience or intrinsic morality.

Research can proceed in these tracks without assuming life is a prerequisite for consciousness or consciousness a prerequisite for intelligence.
