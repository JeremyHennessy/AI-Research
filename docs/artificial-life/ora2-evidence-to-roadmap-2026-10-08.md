## What the latest experiments actually establish

INTERFACE-01 has **16 independent seed initializations**, paired into two material regimes and five correlated arms (160 records, **not** 160 independent populations). Funded active worlds produced **109 paid carriers and 136 installed nutrient conversions**; the funded ghost conversions were zero. Fixed and retrospective externally yoked controls reproduced active histories after declared normalization. **Every work bank exhausted at the finite endpoint**, including the funded active worlds, and material-free arms could not construct. These are meaningful checks of installed substrate physics and paid opportunities, **not** an autonomous constructor, endogenous maintenance, organism, living consciousness or learning.

The earlier CLOSURE-03 assay found no naturally exposed causal repair pairs under its frozen law; static throughput calibration also failed its predefined opportunity benchmark. Preserve those negatives and reserved seeds `8000..8063`. Do not lengthen a studied horizon, install favorable actors, relabel fixed machinery as emergent organization, or combine unrelated earlier positive features into a composite without separate preregistration.

## Scientific crosswalk: proposed questions, not results

| Research insight | Next discriminating question | Stronger null/control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pass 15: inherited changes may expose **latent accessible states** | Can a material- and work-accounted network produce a component *required to produce the constructor*, without a privileged free-bank build? | Direct engine construction, researcher-supplied constructor, fixed rule/table, paid matched impairment |
| Pass 15: **within-life physical history** differs from **across-generation evolution** | At the same genotype/current environment, can randomized past exposure alter an individual's later useful response? Separately, can inherited variation change which transitions are accessible? | Passive relaxation, fixed integrator/filter, stochastic preprogrammed switch, population genotype sorting |
| Pass 14–15: readiness and memory can be costly or misleading | Does history-dependent behavior increase held-out usable resource/viability **after total material, processing and maintenance costs** and after unexpected resource reversal? | Permanent readiness, no trace, false/foreign marks, fixed feedback, matched resource flux |
| Emerging Pass 16: potential chemistry, accessible function and **selectably useful function** are different claims | Which alternatives are merely physically possible, which occur under natural contact rates, and which measurably contribute to survival/reproduction? | Weak promiscuous function, absent partner, researcher-imposed selection, engineered novelty reward |
| Previous ecology and lineage reviews: stable collectives need not reproduce | Can independently formed daughters/granddaughters reconstitute a useful collective function after external copying or sorting is withdrawn? | Built-in group-copy API, externally supplied partner, related-but-nonfunctional offspring |

**Ordering matters.** First demonstrate an **internally produced constructor/production network**, with natural opportunity denominators, paid impairment, resource exhaustion and appropriate external-source controls. Only if its mechanism merits new registered tests should questions about within-life memory, heritable access to latent states, functional daughters, and long-horizon novelty be evaluated. EERC versions 1–5 remain independent research hypotheses; this crosswalk does **not** authorize concatenating them into an architecture.

**Caution on fixed physics:** A fixed finite law/table does not by itself rule out new reachable organization. The present limitation is narrower: in INTERFACE-01 the constructor/access gate and scheduler are externally specified. Distinguish *a capable physical substrate* from *a system-produced organization that exploits that substrate*.

## Separate engineering track: continuity is not adaptation

Ora2.0 may plan a bounded, operator-controlled **persistent-world engineering prototype in parallel** with scientific experiments. The scientific library neither runs nor controls this track. A meaningful technical heartbeat records an authoritative, monotonic simulated tick and state hash, not just a wall-clock timer; a read-only observer must report stale/stopped states accurately and never advance the world.

Prior RUNTIME-01 process-exit/journal/checkpoint testing was bounded to an AL01 reactor fixture, with Windows acceptance reported by local Codex. It does **not** validate process-population restart or unattended operation. Gate any later persistent worker on exact schema-specific recovery, one-writer controls, pause/stop quotas, off-drive backup, physical power-loss recovery, hardened isolation and separate authorization for unattended operation. No metered model/API usage, public self-hosted runner, secret transfer or automatic execution of fetched GitHub commits is implied.

A heartbeating simulation may advance while **zero learning occurs**. Conversely, a finite well-controlled learning experiment may yield evidence without continuous uptime. Track software reliability, individual plasticity, inherited adaptation, collective heredity, ecological novelty and possible consciousness as **separate, falsifiable axes**.
