## Evidence and relevance

- **Companions**: [2017 Forbus and Hinrichs](https://doi.org/10.1609/aimag.v38i4.2743) report bounded analogy-based learning results, not their intended human-level system. [Nakos and Forbus, September 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cogsys.2025.101378) describe knowledge-management infrastructure and explicitly characterize full knowledge autonomy as an outstanding objective. Their SocialBot has restricted opportunities and types of knowledge acquisition. This recent continuation is a valuable counterexample to assuming a long-running research programme has attained its objective. Full 2025 article not accessed.
- **CORTEX**: [Bustos et al., 2019](https://research.aston.ac.uk/en/publications/the-cortex-cognitive-robotics-architecture-use-cases/) report physical robot use cases organized around a designed graph representation and functional decomposition. Changing internal state is not proof of retained learning or endogenous acquisition of the architecture.
- **CoSy**: [Hawes et al., 2007](https://cdn.aaai.org/AAAI/2007/AAAI07-245.pdf), especially pp.1551–1553, report acquisition of visual attribute mappings through tutor dialogue, followed by use in a physical manipulation task. Most other mappings and the learning infrastructure were supplied. Page 1552 explicitly describes a reference assumption about the most recently presented object; p.1553 says the schema has no built-in learning method at its own level. Both pages visually verified. The dialogue and prose appear inconsistent about example colours; no exact colour-specific inference made. A designed scaffold does not negate actual learning of particular associations. But adding modules externally is not organism-level development.
- **DIARC**: [Scheutz et al., 2018](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2018/752) present a physical robot learning a previously unknown composite action and a constituent subtask through nested human instruction. The action primitives and teaching procedure are supplied. Distinguish learning a composition from inventing its underlying objectives or primitive operations. The 2019 lab chapter provides architecture context.
- **DiPRA**: [Pezzulo et al., 2007](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2007/235) describe a resource-bounded architecture in which feedback changes priorities among authored goals, plans and beliefs. The 2009 report extends its account of perception and action. Goal switching and subgoaling do not prove endogenous origin of objectives. Simulation case study; no E2 assessment here.
