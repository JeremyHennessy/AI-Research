## Scientific findings

* [Altmann and Schunn 2002](https://escholarship.org/content/qt9zw301c8/qt9zw301c8.pdf), first paper page visually verified: fits a joint decay/interference model to two historical human memory datasets. [Altmann 2002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12466926/) reports three task-switching experiments consistent with temporary weakening of old task codes reducing interference. These are evidence for functional retrieval dynamics, not physical deletion of stored bits, metabolic savings or independently acquired forgetting machinery.
* [Altmann and Trafton 1999](https://gregtrafton.com/papers/memory.for.goals.pdf) and [2002](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog2601_2) propose treating pending task goals as ordinary memory rather than perfectly accessible stack entries. Modelers supply the task, encoding and retrieval machinery; goal persistence and retrieval are not goal invention.
* [Altmann and Trafton 2007](https://gregtrafton.com/papers/Altmann-Trafton2007_Article_TimecourseOfRecoveryFromTaskIn.pdf), pp.1079 and 1083 images verified: 375 human participants, more than 13,000 sampled interruptions, response recovery over about 10 responses / 15 seconds. Authors propose gradual associative reconstruction of context, but explicitly acknowledge alternative models, including repeated retrieval of one item. Human performance data do not independently establish the proposed computational mechanism. No artificial-organism recovery demonstrated.
* [St. Amant et al. 2004](https://acs.ist.psu.edu/papers/stamantHR04.pdf), author manuscript p.8 visually checked: the more detailed ACT-R model gave inferior detailed predictions relative to expectations; authors identify novice behavior, learning, error recovery and transfer across devices as further work. Their menu optimization savings are simulated, with randomized access frequencies rather than measured user outcomes. The simpler GOMS comparator remains useful. [2007 publication](https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/model-based-evaluation-of-expert-cell-phone-menu-interaction/) is related, not assumed to independently replicate the study.

Ora crosswalk: distinguish retained information, retrievability, interference from stale state, resource cost of storage, reconstruction after interruption, and the origin of the underlying objective. Selective reduction of accessibility is not equivalent to freeing a resource; persistent goal pursuit can occur under authored objectives; matching aggregate performance does not uniquely validate internal mechanisms. These are research interpretations, not candidate modifications or permission to run tests.
