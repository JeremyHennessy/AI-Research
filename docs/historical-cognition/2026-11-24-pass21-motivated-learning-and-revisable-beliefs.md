## Key findings and boundaries

* **MLECOG**: Starzyk and Graham, [2015 online / 2017 issue](https://doi.org/10.1109/JSYST.2015.2442995), section V, pp.1280–1282. The simplified implementation reported action learning and derived needs from authored primary drives. Scarce resources could prevent exploration; abundant food could remove motivation to learn; overly lengthy actions could prevent satisfaction of competing needs. Symbols, available motor options, world rules and initial needs were supplied. Full planning and grounded semantic memory were unfinished. This is direct prior art for resource-sensitive development and derived goals, not evidence of self-originating ultimate goals or reproductive evolution. Source is indexed institutional PDF text; page-image route returned 502 for three requested pages, so no visual authentication of curves or acknowledgement. [2016 dissertation metadata](https://etd.ohiolink.edu/acprod/odb_etd/r/etd/search/10?clear=10&p10_accession_num=ohiou1455711952) establishes additional methods lead; full dissertation not reviewed.
* **MusiCOG**: Maxwell et al., [July 2012 conference paper](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/64487a14945a646fa6f7a229/t/64f96847f82e5559e26919d4/1715876819379/2012-SMC-MusicCOG-Maxwell_Pasquier.pdf), pp.521,527,528 visually checked. The authors report 1,375 added nodes per 2,202 inputs for a first fugue and 1,847 per 4,167 inputs during subsequent training. These ratios are approximately 62% and 44%; they are node/input ratios, not measurements of storage bytes or energy. The sequential training order and different pieces mean that the numbers alone cannot isolate the causal effect of prior memory. They report generation using both supplied seeds and generated material, but key/scale induction and learning from user feedback were future work. No independent aesthetic or transfer claim.
* **Novamente**: [August 1, 2004 author note](https://www.goertzel.org/papers/ExperientialLearning.htm) explicitly reports experiential interactive learning had not yet been conducted, and estimates roughly half the overall design implemented at that date. [2004 symposium proceedings](https://m.aaai.org/Papers/Symposia/Fall/2004/FS-04-01/FS04-01-010.pdf) describe designed probabilistic and evolutionary optimization machinery and anticipated cognitive self-modification. Keep the dated negative evidence, without extending it to later versions. Indexed symposium text only; first-page image 502.
* **OMAR**: Deutsch, [1998 proceedings](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/8ht8f19k), p.303 visually checked; [companion experiment abstract](https://doi.org/10.1177/154193129804201712). Authored distributed task procedures can coordinate without a central executive. Human-performance simulation is not evidence of acquired control mechanisms.
* **OSCAR**: Pollock, [1991 theory abstract](https://doi.org/10.1002/int.4550060103) and [2008 symposium](https://cdn.aaai.org/Symposia/Spring/2008/SS-08-02/SS08-02-010.pdf), first page visually checked (copyright 2007). Inference can support beliefs that are subsequently withdrawn. The initial inference schemes and task prioritization are supplied; revising conclusions differs from evolving inference machinery. The 1991 full text was not accessible in the publisher result.
