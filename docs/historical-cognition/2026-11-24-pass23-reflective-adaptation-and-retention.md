## Findings and evidence boundaries

**RALPH**: Ogasawara and Russell [1993 IJCAI paper](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/1993/193) and Ogasawara [1993 UC Berkeley dissertation](https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Pubs/TechRpts/1993/6292.html), UCB/CSD-93-777. Abstracts describe four alternative decision implementations with speed/correctness tradeoffs and a method for deciding whether further planning is worthwhile. No full-method results verified. This is a historical precedent for authored resource-sensitive metareasoning, not evidence that a resource-bounded organism developed it independently.

**Recommendation**: Coward [2001](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1389-0417(01)00024-9), [institutional manuscript](https://users.cecs.anu.edu.au/~Andrew.Coward/Lessons.pdf) pp.30–31 selected text and p.31 image (PDF index 30). Clustering retains feature combinations and competition learns behavior from feedback. Figure 15 reports 94.7% versus 93.8% average accuracy in its two category-introduction conditions in the final 1,000 presentations; difference 0.9 percentage points. Figure labels and denominator are not a matched individual causal effect or a universal forgetting bound. Narrative separately reports a transient average 5% decline in old-category recognition when new categories are introduced. Distinguish these metrics, and do not silently reconcile differing category counts in text and table. Authored clustering and feedback mechanisms remain prerequisites. Prior-art relevance: measuring retained skill alongside acquired skill is essential; no independently reproduced finding.

**REM**: Murdock and Goel [2008](https://doi.org/10.1080/09528130701472416), [author preprint](https://bill.murdocks.org/MurdockGoelJETAI.pdf) sections 3, 5 and 6.1 and preprint p.15 image. The system adapts an explicit model of an existing disassembly planner to assembly. Table 3–6 illustrate an opaque procedure that cannot be modified directly, an optional inserted task, and an editable symbolic assertion. Q-learning resolves optional modifications. The reported relation-mapping cost advantage over primitive-only planning and reinforcement learning uses access to a complete pre-existing task/method model, which the comparison arms do not receive. It is a meaningful scaffold-sensitive result, not evidence of unrestricted self-modification, independent origin of reflection, or recursive general intelligence. The physical movement simulation was excluded from the cited experiments. IBM and Georgia Tech are author affiliations, not proof of funding or contractual outcomes.

**RoboCog**: Bandera and Bustos [2014 chapter](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-319-12084-3_8), BrainComp 2013, online October 17, 2014. Publisher abstract identifies a shared representation of robot and world, specialized agents, internal emulation/planning and coordination. Recursion of the software architecture is not organism-level reproduction. DOI redirect failed; direct publisher metadata acquired. Full chapter and learned-capability outcomes remain unverified.

**SAL**: Jilk et al. [2008](https://doi.org/10.1080/09528130802319128), publisher abstract; Vinokurov et al. [2012 workshop paper](https://people.cs.ksu.edu/~hitzler/nesy/NeSy12/vinokurov.pdf), original title has spelling “Unsurpervised,” first and final PDF pages visually checked. The hybrid uses internally generated labels and rehearsal to learn novel image categories. Figure 7 shows five of fifteen categories at 100% assignment accuracy, with heterogeneous results across others, including zero. Its criterion uses labels assigned by the system, not externally validated semantic understanding. Motor interaction and richer features are proposed extensions, not established results. Modelers supply perceptual and symbolic learning machinery, images and evaluation. No full-method audit or replication.

## Ora interpretation and remaining frontier

A model that rewrites an explicit description of its own task can be useful without having evolved the machinery for reflection. Likewise, a learner that retains an old category may use fixed representations supplied by the experimenter. Distinguish the causal origin of an internal model, the mechanism that modifies it, its resource cost, acquisition of new skills, retention, and inheritance. These are literature-based comparison criteria only; no novelty claim or execution recommendation.
