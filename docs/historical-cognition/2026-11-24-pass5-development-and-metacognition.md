## Strongest evidence and limits

### Copycat / Metacat

The [1995 author chapter](https://melaniemitchell.me/PapersContent/HofstadterMitchellCopycat1995.pdf), architecture section, especially pp. 211–216, describes a fixed concept inventory with dynamically adjusted relevance and links. Critically, it explicitly disclaims persistence of those changes across successive runs and permanent concept creation. This is a direct historical example of flexible within-run computation that should not be counted as durable learning. The first-page image was checked. The [2002 Metacat report](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/3vc7r0b6) and [2006 journal article](https://doi.org/10.1080/09528130600758626) introduce self-monitoring and episodic recall in the analogy microworld. Abstract-level evidence only for those two records; not general concept formation or conscious introspection.

### Kismet and MACSi

[Breazeal and Scassellati (2000)](https://doi.org/10.1177/105971230000800104) report social interaction regulation in an embodied robot. [Breazeal's thesis](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/d66425a5-e57e-4bc8-95db-3fff5149c899) is a follow-up full-method lead, not reviewed in full. Human interpretation of expressive behavior and acquisition of new skills are distinct outcomes. The [MACSi 2012 architecture paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/HUMANOIDS.2012.6651528), [2013 experiment](https://doi.org/10.1109/DEVLRN.2013.6652525) and [Inria 2012 activity report](https://rex-radar.inria.fr/report/2012/flowers/uid143.html) document a related developmental robotics program using iCub to acquire object representations through exploration and social assistance. The lab's [method overview](https://flowers.inria.fr/active-learning/) explicitly describes learning-progress-based internal rewards. This is authored exploration machinery, not evidence that the underlying objective developed without specification. No numerical superiority claim from abstracts.

### MIDCA: objective selection versus objective origin

The [2013 University of Maryland report](https://drum.lib.umd.edu/items/7ed71b26-3a9e-4705-a038-eb18671bed41) identifies ONR grant N000141210172 and a June 2012–May 2013 research interval. The [2016 AAAI paper](https://dtdannen.github.io/papers/aaai16.pdf), printed p. 3716 (image verified), compares three conditions in a simulated construction task: externally given goals, a trained statistical response to disturbances, and goal generation using an explanation of the underlying cause. The latter maintains high scores under the tested disturbances (Fig. 4). It also presents an example of replacing a failed planner. Importantly, the authors distinguish that meta-level response from generating explanations: the implementation directly maps discrepancies to goals. This is evidence of a designed recovery mechanism in a particular domain, not of an independently acquired underlying purpose or open-ended self-understanding.

The funding acknowledgement (printed p. 3718, image verified) names ONR N00014-15-1-2080 and N00014-12-1-0172, DARPA W31P4Q-15-C-0071 and unnamed NSF support. The second ONR identifier normalizes to the 2013 report identifier. Thus there are four explicit document/award pairs across two documents and three unique award identifiers; not four separate awards. Official award-level funding records/acceptance remain unverified. The 2015 awards are outside the FY1996–2012 FOIA target; record as extension evidence rather than pretend they close archive coverage.

### CogPrime: a private-sector / academic collaboration with explicit limits

The [2011 AAAI paper](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/download/7831/7690) by Goertzel et al. names Novamente LLC, Hong Kong Polytechnic University and Xiamen University. It describes memory/inference integration, research applications in virtual and robotic environments, and a proposed synergistic effect of coupled components. The authors explicitly distinguish then-current functionality from ambitions for child- and adult-level general intelligence; they also identify a missing low-level sensorimotor capability in the core as of that paper. Indexed author text reviewed, PDF image unavailable (interstitial). No audited quantitative causal comparison or independent replication. This is a public technical disclosure involving a private firm, not access to undisclosed proprietary work.
