The choice of dependent-error model has a substantial effect. This checks quantiles, not the assumed correlation structure.
2. Table 3b LIPFC retrieval versus imaginal model BIC difference is only 0.08. Under the usual BIC approximation, relative evidence exp(0.08/2)=1.04081, conditional on these two candidates and equal prior odds. Calling retrieval uniquely identified would be too strong. The authors themselves note unresolved theoretical alternatives.
3. Candidate-selection sensitivity: using the paper's suggested extra penalties 2 ln(5)=3.218876 for a competing singleton, 2 ln(15)=5.416100 for best-pair selection, the PPC event-locked visual competitor remains below the intended imaginal model (22.908876 versus 23.55); the caudate retrieval competitor remains below the intended procedural model (18.558876 versus 21.41). These are arithmetic sensitivity checks on Table 3b, not an independent likelihood or formal posterior calculation.
4. Distinguish ranking from adequacy: Table 1b manual/motor discrepancy rises from 58.93 to 165.60 under the alternative alignment, a 2.81011 ratio. The scale of this change is informative about the dependence of a diagnostic on representation, not a direct effect size or evidence of learning.
5. Correct-trial conditioning excludes failures from the main model fit. Neither the 1,105 trial observations nor repeated scans are 1,105 independent participants. The transfer test is between conditions within a shared experiment, not a held-out participant population. Correlated-event alignment can expose deviations while concealing activity in an interval's discarded middle. Original timing and aligned timing should be retained separately.

## Ora crosswalk: analytical requirements, not engineering authorization

- **Mechanism versus outcome.** Two hypothetical systems could have identical food-acquisition counts and birth counts while differing in whether their actions depend on internal state. Counts alone cannot distinguish them. A causal account must demonstrate the provenance and actual use of the information rather than label a successful outcome as cognition.
- **Alignment.** An observer could align histories by birth or food arrival to expose repeatable event relationships, yet lose absolute duration and energy expenditure. Any such analysis should preserve raw ordering and costs, distinguish truncation from censoring, and avoid silently treating an aligned trace as the original history.
- **Dependent evidence.** Related descendants and adjacent events may share causal history. Counting events as independent samples would overstate the evidence. An appropriate future analysis should specify its independence unit and report uncertainty at that unit; no universal effective sample size is assumed here.
- **Alternative explanations.** A better fit than one weak rival does not establish the correct internal mechanism. Competing fixed, reactive and state-dependent explanations need common evidence and a separate absolute adequacy criterion. Failure histories and selection effects matter.
- **Existing evidence boundary.** Prior reported Ora reproduction and inheritance milestones do not, without intervention and verified information-dependent action, establish the causal cognition mechanism. This note does not reassess or modify engineering evidence.
