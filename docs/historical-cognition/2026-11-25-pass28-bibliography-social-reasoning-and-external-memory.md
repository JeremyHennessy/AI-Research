## Key observations and Ora crosswalk

- [Arslan, Taatgen and Verbrugge, ICCM July 2013](https://www.ai.rug.nl/~barslan/resources/Arslan_Taatgen_Verbrugge_2013.pdf), Groningen, pp.77–82. The model gradually selects higher-order false-belief strategies after feedback. Original PDF pages 1 and 5 visually checked. Figure 2 and discussion report 100 simulations of 100 modeled children (10,000 runs, not 10,000 human subjects); the authors acknowledge that rules needed to reason about others were built in. At modeled age 8 the strategy becomes perfectly stable, unlike the behavioral data. The human training test was proposed future work. A model can show developmental transitions even when the machinery responsible for those transitions is authored. In Ora, acquired selection of a strategy must be distinguished from origin of the strategy-producing machinery; no present claim of theory of mind.
- [Borst et al., 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23973793/), DOI 10.1016/j.actpsy.2013.07.016. Human multitasking improved with external cues; participants used external support when faster, per abstract. For Ora, a useful environmental marker can be a valid extended-memory strategy if generated and used by the organism, but it is not necessarily internal memory; distinguish investigator-provided cues from organism-produced environmental state and account for all costs. Human timing does not directly establish metabolic benefit.
- [Baker, Corbett and Koedinger, 2003 manuscript](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~rsbaker/BCKACTR2003v24.pdf), page 1 visually inspected. Proposed stochastic model comparisons explicitly restrict scope to relatively stable cognitive models, excluding new production creation and changing production utilities. Do not transfer static complexity penalties directly to a lineage whose rule set changes; define model-selection unit and time horizon.
- [Borst et al., 2015](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0119673), publisher HTML. Uses five prior datasets as basis for mapping (via earlier meta-analysis) and tests two other datasets; one model is adjusted to current behavior, the other uses earlier parameters. Important to separate fitted, predictive and independently reproduced results. Same research lineage as rows 116/117; overlap pending, not independent corroboration. [Borst et al., 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20886034/) explicitly reports mismatched neural predictions alongside support for the bottleneck hypothesis.
- [Brumby, ICCM 2004](https://iccm-conference.neocities.org/2004/proceedings/DCabstracts/Brumby.Abs.pdf), first page visually checked; text reports model missed an effect of prior search difficulty on later caution. Retain this negative evidence: a correct immediate action does not establish adaptation to previous experience.
