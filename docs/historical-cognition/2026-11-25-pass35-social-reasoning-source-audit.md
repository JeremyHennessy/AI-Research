## Independent static and analytical checks

1. **Representation provenance.** Model source lines 92–126 predefine semantic records, observation relations and temporal ordering, and seed the elementary strategy. Lines 294–499 contain separate authored answer pathways for all three reasoning orders. The learned variable is the selection/availability of strategy chunks; the procedures that implement perspective reasoning are supplied. No perceptual discovery or independent creation of a higher-order reasoning procedure is established.
2. **Feedback versus programmed transitions.** In the paper the researcher describes feedback-dependent updating. In the linked code the three answer pathways directly set the subsequent update state, with the higher-order stopping condition authored (lines 147–178, 295–499). No external correctness check is visible in the trial driver (lines 30–58). The specific story is known in advance. This is static evidence of a supplied transition policy in the linked version, not proof that a different implementation could not learn from environmental feedback.
3. **Observable non-identifiability.** For the published story, reasoning orders zero and one both predict the current object location; order two predicts the original location (paper PDF indices 2–3). Let latent strategy proportions be p0,p1,p2. The two observed location probabilities are p0+p1 and p2. This observation map has rank 2 and null vector (1,-1,0), including on the probability simplex; any feasible shift between p0 and p1 leaves all location observations unchanged. Thus a developmental change between the first two strategies cannot be inferred from location answers on this story alone. The source records strategy labels directly from authored response branches (lines 319,378,498); these are model-internal labels, not independent evidence of the corresponding human process. A distinguishing task with three distinct predictions or a separate causal measurement is necessary. This proof is original analysis of the observation map, not an experiment.
4. **Independent units and clock.** Source lines 41–58 show a loop of 100 trials per reset; the paper reports 100 simulated trajectories, 10,000 trials. The trial loop retains model memory within each trajectory. The independent sample unit cannot be assumed to be 10,000, and PRNG independence across resets is not verified. The model clock is mapped to human age by interpretation; no actual simulated biological lifetime or 10,000 human participants. The code's driver has no calendar-year variable. The publication's graph reports complete selection stability around modeled age eight, unlike its human comparator. The linked code is bounded by explicitly authored reasoning levels, so the paper's theoretical arbitrary-order extension is not a demonstrated implementation.
5. **Comparator regression sanity check.** The 2012 paper p.1294 reports age-only F(1,66)=83.965 and full F(5,62)=17.519 for the same child sample (n=68). Independently reconstructing R²=kF/(kF+df_residual) gives 0.559897 and 0.585548, respectively. The four added predictors improve in-sample R² by about 0.02565; nested partial F=(delta R²/4)/((1-R²_full)/62)=0.95929. Under conventional nested OLS assumptions this is below the 5% critical value. This calculation agrees with limited additional predictive power in that sample; it does not prove that memory or language is irrelevant or that a module is innate. No raw data, residuals or out-of-sample prediction reproduced.

## Ora crosswalk and disposition

The strongest counterexample is observational equivalence: even a correct answer from a future organism can be compatible with a simpler reactive mechanism. For an ecological analogue, compare two contexts with identical current resource cues but different access histories, then separately test whether organism-specific prior observations causally explain different responses. Distinguish externally recorded event history from internally acquired state, and learned selection among provided strategies from evolution of the strategy mechanism. Account for resource costs and lineage dependence. These are research-level criteria only; no new implementation, test authorization or claim of existing Ora social cognition.
