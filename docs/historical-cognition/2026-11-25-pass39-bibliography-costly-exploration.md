## Crosswalk and negative evidence
Fu/Gray (2006) provides a direct historical comparator for paid information seeking: locally adaptive stopping can coexist with persistent global inefficiency. In a hypothetical decision with exploration cost c, improvement probability p and benefit B, one-step exploration is worthwhile in expectation when pB exceeds c, but an authored local estimate can be biased by prior observations. This is an illustrative decision-theoretic condition, not the historical model's exact equation or an Ora measurement. Distinguish immediate cost savings from long-horizon reproductive advantage and separate authored stopping policy from evolved policy. Fu/Gray (2004) supports considering immediate feedback as a confound, and Fowles-Winkler/Michaelis (2005) supplies an alternative mechanism for apparent semantic competence. Both remain E1. Fincham/Anderson (2006) suggests separate retrieval and control costs in humans; not evidence that the same decomposition exists in Ora. CoJACK's supplied moderation and SNIF-ACT's supplied relevance structure are not evolved functions. No new engineering or execution authorized.
