## Evidence crosswalk

The 2009 fatigue paper reports a constrained mapping with four adjustable coefficients instead of 88 separately adjusted session parameters (44 sessions times two control variables); the authors acknowledge unconstrained fits could be closer. This is a useful distinction between descriptive fit and a parsimonious, transferable account. It is not an E2 review, a claim of better out-of-sample fit, or evidence that the model learned its own fatigue mechanisms. The 2009 driving abstract is a negative-evidence comparator: predictions without matching quantitative observations should remain predictions. The 2011 spatial paper explicitly identifies missing mechanisms, rather than showing comprehensive spatial cognition. These publications share personnel and mechanisms, so multiple papers do not automatically supply independent corroboration.

Original analytical illustration: suppose a system has response score u=a−b and executes only when u≥t. For any scalar d, simultaneously replacing a by a−d and t by t−d preserves the inequality on every trial, holding b fixed. Thus a reduction in an internal resource proxy and compensatory threshold shift can be behaviorally indistinguishable from no change for this restricted observation map. This elementary invariance is not an assertion about the complete historical model. For Ora, later interpretation should distinguish measured available resources, action-admission policy, actual cognitive performance and independently acquired regulation; success rates alone cannot identify them.
