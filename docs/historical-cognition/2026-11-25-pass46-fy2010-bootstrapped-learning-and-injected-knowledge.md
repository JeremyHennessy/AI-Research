## Original analysis and Ora crosswalk

Task completion and mechanism acquisition have different denominators. In the 2011 hidden-domain paper, five of seven final problems involved at least one injected concept. Thus only two of seven problems lacked a reported injection (about 28.6%); this is NOT an observed counterfactual unaided task success rate, because the effects of withholding assistance were not measured and prior knowledge or other scaffolds may matter. The reported 96% measures penalized curriculum rungs, not autonomous success probability across problems. A system may achieve every assigned task with external help while learning only part of the required knowledge. Record assistance and acquired behavior separately, and do not subtract incomparable percentages to infer cognitive improvement. The simulated human-controlled student experiment likewise distinguishes interface usability from autonomous agency. These are research comparisons only, not modifications or execution instructions for Ora.
