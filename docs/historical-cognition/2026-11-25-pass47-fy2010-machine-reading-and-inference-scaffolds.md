## Ora crosswalk and original analysis

The visually checked 2012 Table 3 illustrates why coverage and precision must be reported together. At the 0.95 confidence cutoff for human-agent membership, manual-weight BLP returned 26/26 correct under the authors' adjusted precision measure, learned-weight BLP 2/2. Both equal 100% adjusted precision but the former produced 13 times as many correct outputs in that example. Neither ratio establishes recall without the full ground-truth denominator, and absence of an output is not proof of falsehood. The authors' evaluation of manually weighted versus learned models is a useful counterexample to equating output correctness with autonomous parameter acquisition. For Ora, separate provenance of representations, training signals, rule structures, weights, and downstream reasoning, and report output coverage alongside correctness and resource cost. No runtime changes or experiments proposed.
