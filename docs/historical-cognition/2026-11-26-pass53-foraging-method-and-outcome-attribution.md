## Original analytical crosswalk: three independent attribution failures

1. **Marginals cannot identify individual joint competence.** Let A be correct classification and B correct strategy. If their marginal frequencies are a and b, then max(0,a+b−1) ≤ P(A∩B) ≤ min(a,b), from the union bound and set inclusion. Both bounds are attainable by allocating probability over the four joint cells. For hypothetical a=0.7, b=0.6, the same marginals permit joint competence from 0.3 to 0.6. This is an illustrative calculation, not an estimate of the paper's joint outcomes. Aggregate fit across task conditions is even further removed from a per-lineage joint criterion.
2. **Time-conditioned choice is not unconditional success.** If T is the subordinate decision time and D a competitor decision time, the counted event for target type j is P(J=j,T<D). This equals P(T<D)P(J=j|T<D) when the qualifying event has positive probability. A change in speed can alter the observed count without any change in conditional preference. Toy example: holding conditional preference at 3/4 and increasing qualification from 1/2 to 4/5 increases counted probability from 3/8 to 3/5. These are not fitted paper values; retain missed deadlines separately from choice and actual resource delivery.
3. **Proxy feedback cannot determine outcome ordering without an error bound.** Suppose mean real net outcomes V_i and mean proxy scores Q_i satisfy |V_i−Q_i|≤epsilon for both policies. If Q_1−Q_2>2epsilon, then V_1−V_2≥Q_1−Q_2−2epsilon>0. Without the error bound, even a proxy margin of 1 can coexist with reversed real outcomes (e.g., Q=(2,1), V=(0,3)). The paper does not establish such a bound for its truncated trials. This is elementary decision analysis, not a novelty claim.

For Ora's future authorized evaluations, distinguish per-lineage joint competence, deadlines, paid resource balances and actual completed outcomes from surrogate scores. A simple authored comparator that matches the same measurements is counterevidence to a claim of necessary higher cognition, not a proof that higher cognition is absent. These are research-only measurement recommendations, not a proposal to implement or execute an Ora candidate.
