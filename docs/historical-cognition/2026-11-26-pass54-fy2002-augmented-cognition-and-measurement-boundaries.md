## Ora crosswalk — measurement is not mechanism

Distinguish the internal state of an agent, an observer's estimator of that state and the agent's own use of an internal state. High prediction accuracy alone cannot establish that the agent represents the label or uses it to decide. For a toy binary task, let externally assigned workload L be fair, an observable cue X equal L, and latent internal state S be independent of L. Estimator f(X)=X predicts L perfectly, yet carries zero information about S. Even if S=L, predicting S externally does not establish that the agent internally reads or causally uses it. This counterexample is original illustrative analysis, not a historical result or an Ora experiment. A future authorized evaluation should therefore separate telemetry validity, agent-accessible information and causal behavioral effects, with matched present observations and costs; preserve tradeoffs across tasks rather than reporting only improved primary outcomes. No proposal to expose observer telemetry to Ora or modify its architecture.
