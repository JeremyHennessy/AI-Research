## Crosswalk and public funding
COGBOT's 2003 abstract (p.1372) explicitly replaces the game bots' original perfect map memory with an authored ACT-R declarative memory system. It reports a two-agent game demonstration, not comparative human validation, independently evolved memory, or general tactics. The acknowledgement names the Office of Naval Research Cognitive Science Program, contract N00014-03-1-0031 to Frank J. Lee (publication affiliation RPI); no final report, award accounting or agency acceptance established. The 2005 spatial visualization and 2006 spatial-field papers acknowledge US Air Force Office of Scientific Research grant 02HE01COR; count once, no award amounts or final acceptance inferred. Both identifiers are new exact matches in the screened corpus; these are publication-to-funding leads, not DARPA annual-list coverage.

The 2006 spatial-field model attributes errors to time-dependent availability, spatial similarity and revisits. It reports fitting a previous participant dataset then using fixed parameters on 13 new participants (r=.95, RMSD=.023); removal of spatial interference worsened fit. This is author-reported within-task transfer and a useful ablation lead, not an evolved memory mechanism or independent reproduction. The 2008 expanded paper DOI 10.1016/j.cogpsych.2007.12.003 is a follow-up lead, not counted as another row or independent corroboration here. For Ora, distinguish storage capacity from retrieval interference and environmental cues. Original analytical example: two equiprobable hidden contexts require opposite actions; if a cue is ambiguous with probability e and otherwise reveals the context, an optimal memoryless policy succeeds with probability 1-e/2. This ranges from 1 at e=0 to 1/2 at e=1 even when internal memory capacity is unchanged. A history-dependent policy can exceed this bound only if history supplies additional predictive information under the stated task distribution; demonstrate causal use and control immediate cue leakage rather than inferring memory from accuracy.
