## Original analytical crosswalk, not an Ora execution
Let fixed mechanisms A and B yield identical observed action a in each of n situations. A immediately selects a, B takes k additional internal steps before selecting a. Choice-only accuracy is identical for all n, even with infinite observations of these situations; timing under matched costs or a direct trace can discriminate. More strongly, if B has a different response only in a context outside training support, the two are indistinguishable on training support regardless of sample size. Testing held-out contexts is necessary but still insufficient to prove acquisition: a supplied rule may generalize. Distinguish supplied memory/state, rules, resource budget and observed outcome; require prospective comparison of plausible fixed-rule, history-dependent and environmental-only explanations, plus provenance of any genuinely acquired mechanism. Any future evaluation requires separate authorization; no engineering recommendation is executed here.
