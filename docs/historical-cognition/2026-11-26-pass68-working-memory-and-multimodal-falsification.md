## Original Ora crosswalk: activity can fall as reliance rises
Let an authored system perform n direct memory reads of cost r and s save/restore pairs of cost 2r. Total counted activity A=r(n+2s). A revised policy with n'=n+d direct reads and s'=s-k pairs has delta A=r(d-2k). For r>0 and 0<d<2k, direct reliance increases while aggregate activity falls. Example: n=4,s=6,r=1 gives A=16; n'=8,s'=1 gives A'=10. This toy accounting identity is not a neural model or a claim about Ora. It illustrates why totals alone cannot identify strategy or acquisition. Separate useful reads, switching overhead, retention and output; compare matched alternative mechanisms and reserve a prospective readout not used for model selection. The 2016 paper itself describes reduced aggregate retrieval activity after removing state swapping (section 4.8), so no novelty claim for that mechanism. No Ora intervention authorized.
