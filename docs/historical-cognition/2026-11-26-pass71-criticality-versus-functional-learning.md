## Independent critical assessment and original crosswalk
Parameter selection explicitly uses the baseline outcomes later presented as desirable; therefore their presence is not an untouched validation endpoint. Recovery after the specified disturbance is a separate conditional test. Generalization across independently held-out parameter sets, random topologies, perturbation families and longer horizons is not established by the reviewed evidence. Avalanche membership is an estimator, not direct causal attribution; dependence on window choice and sampling can bias the fitted distribution. A high log-log fit R-squared does not distinguish a power law from competing heavy-tailed distributions; no independent likelihood/model comparison performed here. A state difference after a disturbance establishes history dependence, not useful memory, improved performance or acquired representations. Individual units and plasticity laws were designed; only conductance trajectories and collective statistics changed. Non-conservative neural simulation is not evidence of an energy-accounted reproducing ecology.
Original counterexample (not a simulation): consider N>=3 fixed nodes on a directed ring, exactly one active token, synchronous update x_i(t+1)=x_(i-1 mod N)(t). The number of active nodes is identically one, so aggregate successive activity ratio equals one at every step. The state is periodic with period N and the update law is immutable. Hence stationary activity and aggregate unit branching alone imply neither a power-law avalanche distribution nor functional learning. This ratio is a simplified aggregate statistic, not a reproduction of the article's graph-window estimator. A second distinction: two fixed attractors can encode the direction of a perturbation but, in the absence of a downstream task/readout, do not establish any benefit. These are logical counterexamples to broad capability inference, not evidence against the reported network results.
For Ora, distinguish (a) finite-horizon persistence, (b) recovery of measured macroscopic observables, (c) retained internal state change, (d) demonstrated causal utility under new conditions, and (e) provenance of the mechanism. Matching (a)–(c) does not establish (d)–(e). Future authorized evaluation would need distinguish adaptive benefit from fixed dynamics, externally chosen parameters, resource changes and environmental cues; this note neither specifies nor authorizes an Ora execution. No novelty, AGI or consciousness claim.
