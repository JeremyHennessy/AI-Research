## Original analytical implication for Ora
Separate (a) detecting a signal, (b) estimating a latent state under supplied assumptions and (c) acquiring a function that causally improves behavior. An observation y=s+n cannot alone identify s: for any delta, s'=s+delta and n'=n-delta yield the same y. A filter imposes constraints that make estimation possible but does not by itself prove the inferred state is cognitive or that it has a functional role. For future separately authorized research, compare inferred state to interventions and held-out behavior, distinguish researcher-side estimators from organism-side learned mechanisms, and preserve noise/artifact controls.
