## Ora crosswalk and reconciliation
A representation can cover many categories without supplying enough rules to perform useful reasoning over them. For example, a designed hierarchy can label every object while encoding no causal relation between actions and later outcomes. Vocabulary coverage, rule reuse, successful transfer and acquired functional competence are separate measurements. In future separately authorized research, attribute semantic content to inherited, learned or authored sources and test its causal contribution on held-out tasks. No novelty or hidden AGI claim.
