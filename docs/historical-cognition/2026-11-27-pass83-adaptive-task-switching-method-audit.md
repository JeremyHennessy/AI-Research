## Original analytical crosswalk
Two hypothetical timing policies can have equal mean but different tail risks: a fixed delay of 2 time units and a policy selecting delays 1 or 3 with equal probability both average 2. If a critical resource must be revisited by time 2 inclusive, their missed-deadline probabilities are respectively 0 and 1/2. These are elementary constructed distributions, not historical estimates, Ora runs or a novelty claim. In future separately authorized evaluation, compare complete delay distributions, resource losses and deadline misses in addition to averages, hold sensing and supplied priority constant, and vary regime order to distinguish adaptation from practice. A static counterexample and a changed mean alone cannot identify whether a timing mechanism was acquired, inherited or authored.
