## Ora crosswalk
Distinguish an authored chunking algorithm from acquisition of particular chunks, and both from independently evolving a chunking mechanism. Hypothetical illustration: training exclusively on ABAB and CDCD can make AB/CD familiar chunks; the unseen string ABCD uses those familiar units but violates a grammar allowing only repetitions of one pair. Familiarity can therefore disagree with structural validity. This is an analytical counterexample, not a claim about the original experiment's exact grammar or a measured Ora result. Separate novel-combination transfer, structural rule generalization, representational cost and provenance in any later authorized research.
