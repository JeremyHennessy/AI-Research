## Original Ora relevance and counterevidence

Attribution therefore requires separating supplied representation, acquired internal state and external scoring/readout. A conceptual four-cell comparison crosses fixed versus acquired representation with fixed versus trained readout; a gain in the latter alone does not establish internal learning.
