## Original analytical crosswalk
For two histories that end in the same observed reward but have distinct unobserved causal chains, identical terminal observations alone cannot identify which earlier action mattered. Moreover, predictive accuracy can remain high for a shortcut cue even when intervention on that cue would not change the outcome. Distinguish (a) predicting a delayed resource outcome, (b) changing behavior in response, and (c) identifying its causal origin; none implies independently evolved credit assignment. The historical paper supplies useful prior art for (a)/(b), not (c). In Ora, previously documented reproductive variation is not evidence of a lifetime learning mechanism; no new Ora capability, novelty or experimental authorization follows from this review.
