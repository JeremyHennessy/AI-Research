## Original Ora interpretation and limitations
The Doyle lineage deliberately transfers domain-specific descriptions from an agent to its environment. Adaptation across such worlds can be useful without proving an ability to infer new semantics independently. Distinguish raw environmental constraints from explicit task descriptions, action affordances from externally provided instructions, and acquired representations from inherited/designed ones. A hypothetical agent that chooses correctly by reading a supplied answer label differs from one that infers a relation from consequences; equal observed success does not identify which mechanism produced it. This is a research interpretation, not a claim that Ora uses such labels or a request to modify its substrate. The reported campaign supports functional inheritance, not adaptive advantage or reasoning; no local evidence inspected.
